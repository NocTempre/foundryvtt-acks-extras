## 2026-11-13 — A hub's wait and an agreement's price steps are read, not coded (T-0512, batch B11)

**Ruled:** the line under the 2026-09 figures ruling, "Procedure, kept in
code: dice expressions ... which hub takes days and which weeks", is
superseded in part for the import wait. A printed value found in source moves
with the work that finds it, and a figure a string states comes from an
existing recipe or is dropped (the owner's answers on the T-0512 report).
`availability.marketRulesProse` reads each hub's wait as a phrase
(`localHubWait`, `regionalHubWait`); `rules/imports.mjs` `parseHubWait` turns
the phrase into dice and a calendar unit; the order rolls those dice.
`mercantile.negotiationProse` reads `priceSteps`, how many steps an agreement
moves the price. With either unread, the action refuses with the table named
(`printedError`). The coin changer offers the denominations the money items
carry (`coppervalue`), where it offered three literals.

**Kept in code:** that a wait counts in days or weeks (the unit set is
calendar structure), that the transit roll is the wait's own dice, and that
the price moves by the merchandise row's own step.

**Rejected:** a new setting for either figure; the recipes own them.

*Cost:* a world imported before this change re-runs Rules tables to gain the
two waits and the step count; until then an import from a hub and a
negotiation refuse.

## 2026-11-13 — A false assessment's figures are the Judge's, and every chosen good waits for the Judge

**Asked** (owner, 2026-11-13): "Should a false market assessment keep the
module's own dice for the Beta?", with the buttons "Keep the dice" and "Ask the
Judge". **Answered:** "Ask the Judge".

**Asked** how partial reports behave, with "Both wait (Recommended)" (every
chosen good, partial or false, shows "Awaiting the Judge" until the GM answers;
partial is one "Answer from the market" click; false the GM types; the mask
holds) against "Only false waits" (partial instant, the waiting mark gives a
false report away, reversing the 2026-10-15 masking ruling). **Answered:** "Both
wait (Recommended)".

**Ruled:** the dice retire: `wrongBelief` is gone and the module rolls no
direction and no size for a false assessment. RR ch. 8 (False Assessment) prints
no die, so nothing is imported and no default figure stands in. Every chosen good
is stored with a null modifier and reads "Awaiting the Judge" until the Judge
answers it, so a partial and a false report store and show the same thing and
the 2026-10-15 mask holds. On a partial report the Judge answers with one
"Answer from the market" click, which writes the market's demand at that
moment; on a false one the Judge types each figure. A relayed choice whispers
the Judges one card so they know to answer. New evidence against the 2026-10-15
entry's dice: the owner's ruling above; nothing else in the code or the book
changed.

**Rejected:** only the false report waits (a waiting mark on one outcome gives
the false report away); a prompt on the GM client during the relay (it runs on
the elected lead client, may hang the player's button, and its timing tells);
one offset per report (a procedure the page does not print).

*Cost:* every partial pick costs a Judge click; a residual timing tell remains,
since a Judge who answers one outcome faster than the other can be read, and the
Judge controls it; a Judge-facing input lands in a fixes-only release; reports
chosen before this keep their dice-made figures, with no migration (owner,
2026-11-11, no tools for previous-version data).

## 2026-11-14 — A market may charge its city's own gate toll and duty

Asked "At the AX3 capital's gates, which toll does entering the market
charge? RR p. 372 tolls each entry per stone of cargo capacity plus a tariff.
AX3 p. 56 (written for ACKS I) tolls merchandise passing any of its eight
gates, entering OR leaving, by its own dice, plus a duty. The two give
different amounts. Under the later-printing ruling, ACKS II wins over the AX
books.", with the options "A: RR at every gate (Recommended)" and "B: AX3 toll
at that city" (a per-market switch, described as a new feature), the owner
answered: "B: AX3 toll at that city". The gate the toll is charged at is
`docs/location/DECISIONS.md`, 2026-11-14.

**Ruled.**
- `goods.gateToll` is `"class"` or `"city"`, null reading as class. A market
  set to city charges the city page's procedure in place of the class toll
  and tariff: the toll on the stones of merchandise brought in, not on
  capacity, in the page's units rounded half to even, the dice scaled by the
  units as the page's worked example scales them and thrown once; the duty as
  one thrown percent of each good's market price this month, with no
  exemption, since the page prints none. The figures are the imported
  `cityTravel.gateTolls`.
- The Ruler's Privilege and sneaking in waive both, as they waive the class
  charges: entering a market is RR's procedure, and the city page says its
  rules are built on the core rules. Smuggled goods are outside both bases.
  The stay stays the class's.
- The switch reads the world's one imported figure set. The AX3 capital's
  market record carries `meta.gateToll: "city"`, a structural binding like a
  place's role, which the import writes only where the place holds none. This
  supersedes in part the importer's "The doing-business figures ride the
  city-travel document, and a gate is marked rather than measured
  (2026-09-30)": a second city no longer charges the first city's toll
  unless its market is set to city, and the gate action it names is not built.

**Rejected:**
- A market-local copy of the figures. The switch defaults off, so no other
  market inherits the capital's toll. A copy would also be a second store of
  an imported value, which a re-import or the Ruledata Browser's override
  cannot reach. It becomes necessary when a second book printing a city toll
  is imported into the same world.
- Charging on leaving. Option B as put to the owner left leaving uncharged,
  and no leave-the-market surface exists for the charge to attach to.
- The grain exemption on the duty, which belongs to RR's tariff.
- Pricing the duty at base cost. The page names the market price.
- The page's own impact divisor, stabling rates, labour fee and syndicate
  service: features beyond the gate.

*Cost:* one figure set per world. The duty reads the month's price of a good
before the party has assessed the market, and rolls that price at the gate
where none was rolled. Merchandise under half a unit pays no toll, as the
rounding prints.

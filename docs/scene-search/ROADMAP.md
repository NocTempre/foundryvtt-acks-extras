What is not built. The slices and their owners are in `wip/20261118-scene-search-plan.md`.

- **What S2 left for its owners.** Doors and old-style traps as entries (`session.mjs` `derivedInputs` answers none), the seams to close-out (`resolvePlanRound` in `round.mjs`, `revealSecretDoor` in `seams.mjs` `revealExit`, the trap hasty-search ledger, registrant labels and reason roots for `scene:` refusals), and the plan relay passing the requesting user so it can refuse a hidden entry.
- **Harvest and identify (S7b), deferred out of the minor.** Waits on the owner's own audit of the monster imports, with Treasure Tome's MM harvest work (its H1–H4) deferred with it; DECISIONS 2026-11-19, "Harvest waits on the monster-import audit". Until then a fallen body opens its own sheet and nothing is harvested. The settled answers it carries forward are in the plan (round up between printed steps; any of a dual-typed creature's rows qualifies a harvester, with a per-body override). To check first, unverified: `markets/engine/identify.mjs` `throwTarget` reads `system.rollTarget`, while the importer writes an ability's throws only to its flag, so an imported identifier may read no target there.
- **What S7a left.** A keyed proficiency rolls its first throw, and the rail cannot name another (Naturalism and Magical Engineering each carry two): a chooser is a new control and goes to `/design` first. The Judges' card names the ability and the figure but not which of its throws was rolled (needs an additive field on `resolveCheck`'s answer). A throw a spell made automatic reads as no figure here (`targetOf` answers null), so such a holder is refused as not imported. The column's refusal for a blank skill figure has no words and no tooltip (the picker's has the page).
- **What S6 left.** Nothing yet derives the "From the map" list (`derivedInputs`), so the tab shows none, and a door or an old trap zone cannot be named from the rail as the thing a clue shows. Only a trap, an obstacle and a condition have a drop: no kind accepts a token, a container, a journal page or an image, so a body, container, fixture, exit, lot or floor thing is made by the importer or by hand. The methodical check has no Disarm option: it needs a party check of its own (S7b, or close-out's trap ledger). The board's Seen line has no key in the thing shape.
- **A per-rank step in `materializeRolls` (R-I1b).** Magical Engineering's and Naturalism's throws improve by a printed step per rank, and Loremastery's by a printed step per level. Today their specs, or for Loremastery the scan's unlabelled lift, give the rank-1 target flat, so an identifier at rank 2 or above reads the rank-1 target. Loremastery keeps the lift's positional key until the step is supported.

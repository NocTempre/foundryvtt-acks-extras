## 2026-11-13 — A Judge named as an owner is not one

**Asked** (owner, 2026-11-13): "should a GM listed as an owner stop counting
toward deposit/coin reach in this release?" **Answer, verbatim:** "Fix now
(Recommended)". This reverses 2026-10-10, which ruled that it stays and that
leaving Judges out of `ownersShare` is roadmap work. For example: a Judge
creates a character and a warehouse; both carry that Judge at OWNER by
Foundry's own stamp; the character, standing nowhere near it, no longer reaches
the warehouse until it is pinned, stood at, or a player is given OWNER on it.

**Ruled.** `ownersShare` skips an owner whose user is a Judge (core's `isGM`,
which includes the assistant role), read from the user the ownership map names
and never from the seat. An id that names no user counts for nobody. A Judge
stands for no character, so sharing one says nothing about whose a place is.

**Evidence (what 2026-10-10 did not weigh).** The answer depended on who pressed
Create: the same character and place were in reach or not by which seat made
them, with no pin, token, link or vault in play. The stamp also widened through
`companionOwns`: a player's character marching beside a Judge-made NPC reached
every place that Judge made.

**Rejected:** a `game.user.isGM` bypass (the seat then decides, 2026-09-15's
defect again). **Rejected:** counting `GAMEMASTER` only, which keeps the stamp
alive for an assistant who creates. **Rejected:** stripping the stamp at create,
which reaches no document made before the release and fights core's own rule.
**Rejected:** a setting (a toggle on a gate whose right answer is not in
dispute). **Rejected:** auto-pinning what a Judge makes (writes player-facing
state nobody asked for).

*Cost:* a Judge-made character stops reaching an unlinked, non-pinned place a
Judge also made, and a Judge-only NPC stops reaching any unlinked place on
ownership alone, so deposits and coin acts that passed on the stamp now answer
`notYours` or `outOfReach` in a running campaign. The way back is a Judge's act:
pin the place to the character, stand at it, or give the player OWNER on it.
Retrieval stays ungated, and a Judge's own coin acts still pass.

## 2026-11-13 — A dropped actor means what the tab it lands on says

**Ruled.** On the recruitment tab a dropped NPC is somebody the party can hire; anywhere else it is somebody who is simply here (an occupant: the innkeeper, the garrison, the stabled mule). Both readings are right in their own tab and wrong in the other, so the drop target arbitrates. A location dropped on a location is nesting whichever tab it lands on, through the cycle guard.
**Rejected.** A modifier key to choose the reading: nobody would find it.

## 2026-11-13 — The stash picker moves everything in one transfer call

**Ruled.** Everything picked in the stash dialog moves in one transfer call, because a single move that fails halfway is far easier to reason about than twenty that each might. A container is flagged in the picker so nobody is surprised that its contents travel with it.

## 2026-11-13 — The storage manager writes direct and is last-write-wins

**Ruled.** The manager is a GM tool, so its writes go direct with no sockets. Two GMs driving it at once is last-write-wins on distinct items, which is documented rather than defended against.
**Rejected.** The socket-and-claim-token machinery: it exists for player-triggered flows, not for two people sharing one admin screen.

## 2026-11-13 — A market's till float is stored, and a null one derives once

**Ruled.** `system.market.tillTargetGp`, the till float a market keeps in coin, is stored and never a buried derivation, because it is a knob other systems turn. Null means derive once and write it back: families × income from the imported tables, a placeholder setting otherwise. The month sweep tops the house coin up to it, and `tillRefreshTime` is that sweep's watermark.

## 2026-11-13 — The Judge's record of a place is a second field, not a gate on the shared notes

**Ruled.** `system.gmNotes` is a second field beside `system.notes`, so the two are visibly different records instead of one field whose audience depends on a setting; the notes are shared. It renders only inside the sheet's GM region, which is the whole of its protection: the same UI convention the roster's `hidden` follows, not a security boundary.
**Rejected.** A gate on `notes` that varies its audience by a setting.

## 2026-11-14 — A city gate is the point of entry to the market it stands inside

Asked "Every place offers a Role of "City gate" on its Contents tab, and
settlement imports set it, but nothing in the module reads it. Keep the
control or drop it?", the owner answered: "Fix the gates to follow RAW rules
for entry to a town/city."

**Ruled.** The rules print one entry procedure, the market's (RR ch. 8,
Entering the Market, RR 370–372), and its step 3 is charged at the point of
entry, which the page says a city gate is. The markets feature already
charges that step, so the role is made to lead to it rather than to charge
anything of its own. A place with the `gate` role is the point of entry to its
nearest ancestor with a market. Its sheet links that market and opens the
market's Ventures tab. Standing at it counts as standing at the market, inside
the one definition of reach, so the entry the gate offers is never refused at
the gate, and the next day's trading from the same spot is not either. This
supersedes in part "A place has a role" (2026-09-30): its "settlement board's
gate action" is not built and is not the role's reader.

**Rejected:**
- Gate hours, shutting at night, the watch's word, a refusal at the gate: no
  page prints them, and no house rules ship.
- Entry allowed only through a gate. The page names gates among other points
  of entry, and a market nobody drew a gate for must stay enterable.
- A reach clause for market entry alone. That would be a second definition of
  reach, refusing the next day's trade at the spot where entry was allowed.
- A port or caravanserai role beside the gate. The mechanic is the same, and a
  Judge marks one as a gate.
- Recording which gate a party used. Nothing printed varies by gate.

*Cost:* every gated market has a second body for deposits and coin as well as
entry, since reach is one rule. A world's gates are walked once per reach
sweep, or once per call where a caller holds no scan.

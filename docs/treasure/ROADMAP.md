# Treasure — roadmap

What is not built. How it works now is [MODEL.md](MODEL.md).

| Row | Not built | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ammunition's apparent worth | A rolled ammunition row ranking at its base's cost plus masterwork. | The base lookup refuses ammunition by its import stamp, since the page prints its figure per projectile and a stack has its own sentence; on the Journal an ammunition row reaches a name link because its grid caption abbreviates the word. Ranking one needs the per-projectile figure read at import first. An ammunition card ranks 0. |
| A claimed arm builds its base | A claim of a rolled arm creating the base document with the bonus, not the named placeholder. | `apparent.baseUuid` names the base the card ranks by, and a claim does not use it: a card with no linked document claims as a placeholder item. Building the base needs a ruling on what the bonus becomes (a variation, the system's bonus field). |
| Compound magic nouns | A placeholder whose noun is a choice among lists, or one of each of a parenthesized list, rolled. | Both are refused as `compound` before matching (MODEL, "Magic cards are rolled"); the 2026-11-18 census found 6 classic placeholders of these shapes. How either is thrown is printed procedure, so it belongs in `hoard-map`'s noun split, read from the page, never a word shipped. |
| Deal audit rows posted | The Judge's audit of a letter deal (`runLetterDeal`'s `audit`) posted where the Judge reads it. | The audit is built and nothing posts it. Posting needs a card, so `/design` comes first. |
| A mask's cost from anything but a disguise | `mask.cost` read from a source other than a disguise. | A masked lot's apparent worth is its disguise's cost; a rolled card has no disguise, so nothing writes it. |
| Value card weight on the tile | A value card's tile showing the weight of the coin it is delivered as, and a 0 gp value card weighing something. | Quick grab plans a value card at the coin it is delivered as (DECISIONS, "The owner's answers"); the tile still shows the card's own weight, and a 0 gp card plans at no weight. |
| Coin and gem toggles | "No coin" and "No gems or jewelry" on the deal toggles panel. | Stored under `deal.toggles` beside the two built. On a by-hand pot they would strike coin or value cards at open, and the plan's canvas wanted them for a rolled hoard. |
| Two unruled toggles | "Leave carried gear" and "Hide worth until turned". | The canvas's other two toggles. Never ruled by the owner; a ruling decides whether either exists. |
| Replace ticks | A Judge ticking and unticking the lots a replace seed takes, in "Judge deals" mode. | The planner picks the lots and the staging window shows them struck, in both modes (MODEL, "The replace planner"). A control needs a design first. |
| Replace: exact coin | A replace seed taking the exact amount from coin, in proportion across denominations, in place of whole lots. | Owner question B's counter (moderate): a whole lot can overshoot by a gem's worth. The whole-lot default stands until ruled. |
| Drag and drop: other sheets | A card dragged onto the vehicle, item, party and unit sheets. | These override `_onDrop` and never fire `dropActorSheetData`, so the pot's hook does not reach them; each needs its own binding, as the party sheet's member drop is bound. The character, monster and extras' character sheets are reached. |
| Party picker | A Judge's choice of which party a pot answers to. | Nothing a Judge presses writes the pot's `partyId`; the aside reads the flag, else the world's only party, so a world with several parties has none until a control sets it. | Search: text results | A drawn table result that is text only (no item) becomes a card. | It is listed on the card, since no card shape delivers it. |
| Search: containers | A body's container and its contents move as one. | Every goods item on the body is its own card, a container's contents included. |
| XP award | Experience for treasure recovered. | Out of scope for the pot. |
| Carry limits | A warning when a take overloads a character. | A Take does not read capacity. |

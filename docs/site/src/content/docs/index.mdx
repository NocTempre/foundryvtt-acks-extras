## What it does

ACKS II is a game about what your choices cost — a hand that is already full, a
market that has only so many people in it this month, a torch with eleven turns
left. This module keeps that bookkeeping honest so the table can spend its
attention on the decisions instead.

Every feature is opt-in through its own settings, and the defaults are RAW.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Equipment & fighting styles" icon="seti:pipeline">
    Equip limits and a hand budget, fighting styles detected from what is
    equipped, containers that carry their own encumbrance, ammunition and thrown
    weapons. [Read the guide](/foundryvtt-acks-extras/guides/equipment/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exploration formations" icon="seti:folder">
    The party as one token and one marching order, a ten-minute dungeon clock,
    torches that burn down, and a scout who can range ahead.
    [Read the guide](/foundryvtt-acks-extras/guides/formation/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Henchmen & hirelings" icon="seti:crystal">
    Availability rolled once a month for the whole town, paid recruitment
    postings, hiring throws, loyalty and morale, wages and arrears.
    [Read the guide](/foundryvtt-acks-extras/guides/henchmen/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Proficiencies & class powers" icon="seti:plan">
    An ability item with several throws instead of one, targets that scale with
    level or rank, and rolls that pick up their own modifiers.
    [Read the guide](/foundryvtt-acks-extras/guides/abilities/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Influence & reactions" icon="seti:notebook">
    A social roll with its whole modifier stack itemized before you commit, and
    attitude tracked per subject.
    [Read the guide](/foundryvtt-acks-extras/guides/influence/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Places, storage & markets" icon="seti:db">
    A Location actor that nests, holds goods attributed to their owners, and
    optionally carries a recruitment market.
    [Read the guide](/foundryvtt-acks-extras/guides/location/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

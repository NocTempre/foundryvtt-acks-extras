# Item Markets

Buy and sell equipment at any settlement with a market, at ACKS II
availability and prices.

> Feature under construction — this guide fills in as the flows land, with
> screenshots at the next release.

## Setup

1. Import the market tables from your books via **ACKS Importer** (Equipment
   Availability, Merchandise, Magic Item Transactions), or fill the
   generated placeholders by hand.
2. Give a location a market (its market class derives from urban families,
   or set the override).

# Changelog

Changes through v11.14.5: [CHANGELOG-through-v11.14.5.md](CHANGELOG-through-v11.14.5.md).

## 2026.11.20.1 — Public Beta

### Fixed

- **Dungeonbashing includes the character's Strength wherever its figure is shown.** Character sheet → Rolls tab → Adventuring → Dungeonbash showed the figure stored on the sheet with no Strength in it, so a strong character read the same target as a weak one. The figure now takes the Strength modifier at the factor the Rules tables import reads (RR p. 267), and its tooltip names the Strength part with its page. The party's Dungeonbashing roll and the skill audit read the same figure, so the audit's target now includes Strength too. With a Strength modifier and no imported factor, the row is blank and does not roll.

## 2026.11.20 — Public Beta

### Fixed

- **A summoning spell that names no creature takes the one dropped on it, and a family is made one creature at the cast.** The cast dialog's Summon band listed every creature on every compendium the seat could open in one select, and a family such as an imported cacodemon could not be summoned at all. Where the spell's rows name no creature, the Summon band (Actors tab → a character sheet → Magic tab → Cast, or the system's cast button) now holds a drop zone: drop a creature or a family from the Actors tab, a compendium or the map, and Cast waits until one is there. A family asks for its variant when Cast is pressed, and the creature made from it is the one placed. A spell whose rows name their creatures keeps its select.

- **An import no longer stops to ask which attacks a class's damage bonus applies to.** Importing a character whose class leaves that choice to the player (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Import tab) opened the Damage Bonus dialog and waited for an answer. The class is now applied with the choice left open and no bonus on either side, and the character sheet's Class tab shows a Damage Bonus row to make it on; once made it is kept for good. Applying the class by hand still asks.
- A group's unit Loyalty starts blank instead of a stored 0. On a group's sheet, in the Unit section, the Loyalty field (a mercenary unit's) is empty until a figure is typed, and its tooltip carries the page that prints the loyalty figures. A unit hired before this change keeps the 0 it stored.
- A past venture row's duty label follows the toll its entry was charged. On a market's Trade tab, the Judge's party lines and the entry card now name a tariff or a duty by the toll the party was charged at entry, instead of by the market's gate switch as it stands now, so flipping the switch after an entry no longer relabels it. An entry made before this change has no stored toll and reads the switch in force, as before.
- **A journey Encounter Zone throws the delve's imported die, not a fixed d6.** The zone throw on the encounter card (the **Encounter throw** quick tool of the Formation window on a journey, and the hex and End Day throws) rolls the die the importer reads from the Judge's Journal's wandering-monster passage (JJ p. 36), and its target is no longer cut off at 6. The die's tooltip names the page it was imported from, or says a Judge set it. With no die imported the zone throws nothing: the card still posts with the zone line, the die and roll left blank (the tooltip gives the page reference) and no outcome below it. The die is read, and can be overridden, under `Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`.

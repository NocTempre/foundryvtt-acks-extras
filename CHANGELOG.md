# Changelog

## 2026.11.27

### Added

- **A monster's Strength now adjusts its attacks.** A monster whose Strength a Judge entered (Monster sheet → Classification tab → Attributes) takes one more labelled row on the attack throw and the damage roll of its melee attacks, and on the damage roll of its thrown ones: for a natural attack the row reads from the Monstrous Manual's Strength table, by the score and the attack's average damage, and for a weapon marked "weapon" (the item sheet's ACKS Monster group) it is the system's own Strength modifier. The rows show in the attack dialog and on the posted card, not in the Monster sheet's Attacks tab figures, and a blank Strength adds nothing. The table arrives with the Monstrous Manual's rules tables (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Rules tables).
- The **Camp** section (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the **Wilderness** tab → **Camp**) shows its parts and takes your hand. Hover the **Food** or **Water** figure for each item carried, its size and where the size was read, and for any item with no size the page that prints it. **Vessels** lists every water item a member carries with what is left of it, and **Refill** fills the sized vessels of every member you own (a Judge owns all). **Going without** replaces the list of only the suffering: every member is a row with a hunger rung and a thirst rung, a menu for each on a member you own, and a *set by hand* mark on a rung you set; the next **End day** that steps that ladder goes on from the rung and takes the mark off. Exposure and Constitution lost show at the end of the row.

### Changed

- **A monster's attack row shows how many weapons it carries.** A weapon stack above one prints `×N` beside its name on the Monster sheet → Attacks tab, as the monster's Inventory tab already did, and hovering the count names the item's Quantity as its source.
- **A monster's ability scores now count in the module's throws.** A score a Judge enters gives the acks system's own modifier for it on a drowning monster's breath, a squeezing monster's push, a door bash, a night's Endurance, an ability's score term and, for Wisdom, a saving throw against magic, and a jump reads the entered Dexterity; a blank score is the default specimen and adds nothing, as before. The scores are entered on the Monster sheet → Classification tab → Attributes, and an entered score shows as its own part in the figure's tooltip. The rules the Monstrous Manual gives a monster's scores apart from a character's (Strength on natural attacks and load, Dexterity on speed, Constitution on hit points, Charisma on morale) are not applied yet.
- **Divine and arcane power are one icon each on the casting strip, shown only for a character that has the power.** Under Actors sidebar tab → a character → its sheet, the casting strip (the Magic tab on the module's sheet) draws an icon for each power the character's class casts, or whose ledger holds entries, or, for arcane, that a Judge marked as the class power; a character with none draws nothing. While the balance is above 0 it is drawn as a number over the icon. Clicking the icon opens that power's entries, the owner's controls and the Sacrifice button, which moves in from the strip line.
- **Editing items in the Items sidebar tab no longer reloads the item library on every seat.** After an item there is created, changed or deleted, each seat refreshed the skill ladders (what a throw borrowed from a skill reads) by loading every imported Item compendium whole again; it now reads each compendium's index and loads only the imported skill definitions it does not already hold. A cast or other library action started just after a page loads no longer loads the item compendiums a second time beside the library's own load, and the trade goods a market reads from a compendium's Merchandise Items load in one request per compendium instead of one per good.
- **A world no longer loads its imported roll tables and journal entries on every seat at load and before a cast.** The actions that need those two shelves load them themselves: Build packages and Detach all packages on a Class sheet → Templates tab, the rules-table pass that follows a table import, and a book Adventure's import. A world with imported books opens with less read from its compendiums.
- **A creature a card names is found without waiting for the library to load.** An encounter card posted to the chat tab just after a page loads links its creature at once, where it used to hold the card back until every creature in the imported library had loaded. Roll at random on a faction's Org chart tab or a group's sheet now reads the compendium's index to find the creature each mount or beast word names, and the recipe editor opened from Recipe… on the same chart lists the creatures it can link from the index the same way, instead of waiting for every creature to load.
- **A companion pick, a summoning cast, a lair's Roll and the token art pass load only the creatures they name.** The companion picker, which opens when a companion ability is dropped on a character or from the plus control of the Companions section on a character sheet's Equipment tab, loads the library's animals and no other creature. A summoning spell cast from the Cast dialog finds the creature its row names, and the summoning check finds the spell's library entry, by one request each, where each used to wait for the whole library to load. Roll in the Lair section of a place's sheet finds its monster the same way. Overhead tokens from … and Restore book tokens, on the Tools tab of Manage Imports (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports), load the imported monsters alone.
- **Creature shelves are no longer loaded whole at startup: summon names, places in a compendium and Restore book tokens read the creature index instead.** A summoning spell's creature is named from the compendium's index on the Mechanics tab of the spell's sheet (Items tab of the sidebar, then the spell) and in the effect row window opened from it, which redraws once if the index was still being read when it opened. A place held in a compendium (an Actor compendium in the Compendium Packs tab of the sidebar, then the place) lists its sub-places and the providers in its pack once the pack's index is read, which opening the sheet starts. Restore book tokens, on the Tools tab of Manage Imports (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports), is offered when an imported monster carries the mark, found from the index and not by loading the monsters. A creature shelf is loaded whole only by an action that needs it.

### Fixed

- **An imported city map no longer reads a table's open top row as a large after-dark addition.** The import widens a row whose die is printed open above out to the table's open end. The after-dark figure read that open end as the row's top, so such a table would have shown an addition of hundreds. The figure now takes that row's printed low. No table the importer reads today prints a die open above, so no imported map changes yet. The figure is on the Scenes sidebar → a scene's Configure → Basics tab → City encounters → Added after dark.
- **The road and water presets say what they arm, and look apart.** Pressing Alley now names the street with the surface it keeps ("Paved road, Alley"), where the notice named the surface alone. The shore preset is called Coast, as its wall row and notice already were. The earthen and gravel presets each have a glyph of their own, and so does the mark tool, which had shared the road glyph with three presets. The presets are on the Battlemap scene control; the mark tool is on the Walls scene control → Mark the selected walls as roads.
- **Map setup says which pixels a figure counts.** "px" had named the picture's own pixels on the fit card and Foundry's canvas pixels in the size fields. Figures read off the picture now say "image px": the card's cell, the residual, the box rows and the scale bar. The sizes the scene is given say "canvas px": the square, hex and ruler cell size fields and the Measured line. It is on the Battlemap scene control → Map setup.
- **A drowning monster's breath tooltip no longer lists a Constitution part it does not have.** A monster whose Constitution is blank read "Constitution 0" among the parts on its Breath figure; it now names the breath, the rounds counted and the page alone. The figure itself was already right. A character's tooltip still lists their Constitution, and so does a monster's once a Judge enters the score. It is on the swim row of a monster that is drowning: Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing.
- **A group row rolled for an unmatched creature word now keeps its printed hit points.** Where a roll's stack names a creature no library entry answered, the figure the roll prints for its bodies is stored on that stack (Actors tab → the group's sheet → its stack row), so once a Judge drops a creature on the row its bodies take that figure instead of rolling hit dice. A stack still holding no creature rolls no hit dice at all: the row's Materialize button makes no body until a creature is dropped on it.
- **A fuse's burst card now says which defense changed a body's Damage figure, and by how much.** The figure's tooltip named a defense only as "then the body's defenses". It now reads the defense and the figures it moved, as the spell and attack cards do, for example "then Fire resistance (Ring) 12 to 4.8", and where a defense or a save's share leaves a fraction it ends with the rounding, "then Rounded 4.8 to 5", so the parts arrive at the figure shown. An applied row keeps the stages its Apply used, so a defense the body loses later does not change the tooltip, and a card already in chat keeps its old wording. It is on the Chat tab → a fuse's burst card → the Damage figure of a row.
- **A spell an older printing renames to two alternatives is now found in your library, and when both are there the character is left for you to settle.** The import read "A or B C" as "A" and "B C" and never tried "A C", so a character whose spell was renamed that way showed "Not in the library" although both spells were held. The shared trailing words now reach the first alternative; one spell held is granted, and with both held neither is granted and the biography's As printed block gains a "One of" line naming them, until you drag the right one onto the character, after which a rebuild keeps it and adds no line. The As printed block is on the character's Notes tab: Compendium Packs tab → the Judge's shelf of Actors → the book's Pregenerated folder → the character.
- **An imported creature's encounter recipe now reads four more wordings its page may use for what a group is made of.** A dependant share worded as a percent of the males, dependants worded as eggs, females, children or noncombatants (a bracketed note after the word is fine), a lair a page says is ruled by a rank rather than led by one, and a chieftain's escort worded "accompanied by" or "escorted by" (a rank or a creature, a count or a die, "for every" gang or warband) were skipped, so a Roll of the creature's organisation made none of them. Each is now a part of the recipe, and the parts show in the organisation chart's Roll panel under "This roll will make" (Actors sidebar tab → a faction or a group → Org chart tab, tool row beside Roll at random) wherever the creature's own lines count a unit the part names, and in the recipe editor (Actors sidebar tab → the monster → Ecology tab → Encounter → Organisation…). A guard sentence that names one place (dungeon or wilderness) now keeps it as the part's Found in. A share of the males counts every body of the unit, as a share of the warriors does on a creature the page never splits. A creature imported earlier takes the change with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries.
- **Every importer notification now opens with the module id, once.** A few kinds still went out without it: a cited page whose book is not on the shelf or is not open to the seat, a table import refused to a player or with no provider, a book's location saved or not saved, the folder and browse results, the adventure fill, the re-import and repair results, the market and merchandise warnings, and the token art results. They now read like the rest, and their wording is unchanged.
- **On a scene that auto-sizes tokens, a token a formation re-creates, or a pasted token, keeps a size set in its Token Config.** Joining or leaving a formation and the start and end of a fight re-created the token and fitted it to its footprint again, undoing a hand's width and height. A size that differs from the actor's prototype and is not the one the module last wrote now stays; the size chips' reset and Map setup's Rescale tokens on scene still re-fit it. The setting is Scene configuration → Basics tab → Battlemap → Auto-size tokens.
- **A stat block that names a creature or ability by an older name the conversion table renames to two alternatives now tries each alternative.** The import looked the whole "A or B C" target up and never found it, so the name fell back to plain text. One alternative the importer knows is now used, completed with the shared trailing words; with both known it uses neither rather than guess. The importer's name tables hold neither alternative of the one such rename today, so no imported monster changes yet. Imports run from Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports.
- **Importing the official character sheet no longer unequips what it just placed, and no longer raises a "Not enough hands" toast for every item.** An import puts gear into hands one item at a time, and each placement alone could overflow the hands and be resolved before the next one arrived, so a re-import ended on the old loadout and a first import silently dropped a shield or a bow. The character's hands are now held while the sheet's gear is placed and settled once after the last: the sheet's own placements are kept in the sheet's order until the hands are full (a placement that does not fit is carried, and a later one that does fit is still held), then whatever was held before and the sheet did not place. Where anything was carried, the report shows a **Hands** section after its counts line (Actors sidebar tab → a character → its sheet's window menu (⋮) → Official sheet (PDF) → Import, or Actors sidebar tab → Import sheet for a new character), naming what was carried; swap it into a hand from the character sheet's Equipment tab. An ordinary equip outside an import still warns as before (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Equip-limit enforcement).
- **The party's camp and its Supplies press count only what is food or water, by what an item IS and not by a fragment of its name.** A Ring of Regeneration or a Potion of Brilliant Restoration counted as a day of food each ("-ration"), a Ring of Water Walking, Eelskin armor or a flask of holy water as a day of water each, and Supplies could spend a Ring of Regeneration. Items the importer made from the rations and waterskin entries (and class-kit items cut from them) are read by that identity, a wineskin holds no water, a hand-made item is read only where its name has the word ration, waterskin or canteen, and a part-eaten week's ration now counts the days it still holds, so the Camp forecast and Supplies agree. Seen in Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Wilderness tab → Camp, and on the sheet's Supplies press.
- A ration's size and a water vessel's capacity now come from the imported book, and a food or water item with no size is left out of the camp's count instead of being counted as one day. To set or change one by hand, open the item (Items sidebar tab, or Actors sidebar tab → an actor → its Inventory tab → the item's row), arm its sheet with the pencil, and use the **Provision** controls on its **Details** tab: *Provides*, *Days per unit* and, for water, *Left*. Library items imported before this read their sizes once their entries are imported again (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries); a copy already on a character keeps reading unsized until a size is typed in its Days per unit.
- **End day** now spends the food and water it deals (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the Wilderness tab → **End day** at the tab's head): each member's share comes out of their own pack and then the rest of the order's, through the same routine as the **Supplies** press, so a member the press fed that day is not fed again. A ration worth several days is eaten a day at a time and an emptied stack stays at zero; water comes out of each vessel's **Left, days** (the item's sheet → **Details** tab → **Provision**), and an emptied vessel stays in the pack; water found by foraging adds to what is left. A member short of a day whose only food or water has no size keeps that hunger or thirst as it was, and **The weather's toll** card names them, the items and the page; setting the item's **Days per unit** brings them back into the count.
- A dehydrated member now stays dehydrated until **End day** gives them a full day's water (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the **Wilderness** tab → **End day**); a half day after a dry one no longer ends it, which the **Going without** table in the **Camp** section shows.
- **A summoning spell names its creature to a player who can open it, even where the world holds a copy they cannot see.** On the Mechanics tab of the spell's sheet (Actors tab → the character → Magic tab → the spell) and in the effect row window opened from it, a player was shown "A creature this seat cannot open" whenever the world held a copy of the creature they could not observe, although the creature's compendium was open to them and the cast summons from it. The sheet now names the copy the cast would summon.

## 2026.11.26.1

### Upgrading

- **Judges: the square-size table in the Ruledata Browser is read again** (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`, document `monsters`, table `sizeSquare`, row `squares`, cell `feet`). v2026.11.26 stopped reading it. A world that imported the Monstrous Manual's rules tables in v2026.11.14 through v2026.11.24.1 still lists it, and importing again keeps it. A positive figure there, as imported or as a Judge overrides it, is what one battle-map square is worth in feet, and `Scene controls → Battlemap → Map setup → Token sizes → Rescale tokens on scene` and every placement that sizes a token read it; a blank, zero or negative figure, or no such table, leaves the module's stated square.

### Added

- **Set one cast's control end by hand.** On the Chat tab, the held-end card of a conjuration whose concentration ended, and the time-end card of one that ran its duration, draw an Outcome select (Under control, Control lost: they stay, they go, they turn hostile) in place of the outcome line, for a seat that owns every creature the card lists; a hint line under it says what the chosen value did, and a Judge's pick applies to the cast's creatures at once. Under control gives back the owners and the disposition a hostile end took and puts nothing back on a party roster.
- **A Judge can cast past the fight that holds a caster.** When a begun fight holds the caster and it is not the caster's turn, the cast window warns the Judge which fight holds them and offers **Cast now** between **Cancel** and **Declare**, which casts at once as an out-of-combat cast does, spending the slot and posting the card with no declaration; a Player is held and still declares: character sheet → Magic tab → Cast → the cast window's footer.

### Fixed

- **A location created after the world loads is no longer deleted by the empty-location clean-up before anything is put in it.** The clean-up that runs when the world starts (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Clean up empty locations) now leaves alone any location created since the world was launched, including one another Judge made while the game master seat was still being chosen; it is considered again at the next launch.
- **A trader's trade history reads its net to the copper.** In the history on a character's Trade tab (a trader's character sheet → Trade tab → Trade history) and on a market's Trade tab, the summary's net and each row's gold print as whole coppers, so a net of -248.7 gp no longer shows as -248.70000000000002.
- **A market's ledger no longer shows a document id in its notes.** Rows in the trade history (a trader's character sheet → Trade tab → Trade history) and in a market's ledger (a place's sheet → GM View tab → Market ledger) read "enter posted (ruler's privilege; nothing paid)" without the bracketed code that ended them. The code is kept on the row in a field of its own, which is what a repeated request is refused on; rows written by an earlier version keep their notes as written and are no longer matched as repeats.
- **The Judge's own library shelves no longer stock the shop until the Judge ticks them in.** A market's catalogue and a purchase by name could answer with an item from a Judge's adventure shelf. Those shelves now start unticked, and a shelf the importer makes later starts unticked too; tick one to sell from it under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Shop sources → Choose sources. A Judge shelf with nothing the shop could list stays out of that window until ticked.
- **A crossing member's target tooltip names the page of the ability that supplied the base figure** (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → the crossing's member rows → hover a target). It read "<ability>, base N+" and ended on the Spelunking page, which is not where that figure is printed; it now carries the ability's own page beside the base, and the Spelunking page ends the line only for a stored figure, which names none.
- **A monster that is a Carrier counts its own pack** (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Order tab, the carrier's encumbrance cell and the carrying warning's per-carrier line). Its base was always 0 because the figure came from core's character-only encumbrance; it is now the load its items and coin weigh, so its share, its capacity check and its "cannot move" warning start from what it carries. A character carrier's figures are unchanged.
- **A monster with a load set squeezes at its own speed, and a monster whose Speed table is its only speed has a squeezing throw** (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → a Squeeze crossing). A creature with a normal load or capacity set was priced by the character movement calculation on that load, so it crossed at a character's pace and could be left with no speed, no target and no Throw. It now reads its own speed: the speed its sheet derives, else the land row of its Speed table (monster sheet → Speed). The swim rows read the same speeds. A character's speed is read as before.
- **Breaking a container open takes the basher's Strength into its Dungeonbashing throw (character sheet → Equipment tab → a locked container's Bash control)**, at the factor imported from the Judge's book, as a door bash already does. With no factor imported, no Strength term is added and the throw rolls as before.
- **A Judge's lock of a Trapbreaking choice in the Turn plan (party sheet → Dungeon tab → Turn plan) is refused, as it already is for a player, when the trap itself stops the throw**: a trap already disarmed or spent, one not found, or no trap in reach. Before, the Judge was warned past it, the entry was written, and it then resolved refused. A refusal about the character (no thieves' tools) is still warned past.
- **A Trapbreaking choice aimed at a trap on a place reads "Trap · <place>" in the Turn plan's choice list (party sheet → Dungeon tab → Turn plan)**, as it does in the Trapbreaking window, instead of "Trap area".
- **A repair or a rules-tables import now keeps your own edits to a class's build and race.** Applying the class-builder tables wrote a class's build fields (hit dice, fighting, thievery, magic, racial value, notes) and its race over whatever the class held, so a Judge's change to them was lost on every Repair everything. The import now records what it wrote; a field you changed since is left as you set it and listed with the repair's other kept edits. A class imported before this release has no record yet, so its first apply still writes. Where to look: Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Import tab, **Repair everything**.
- **A Judge casting a door-sealing spell past its stated range is warned and the seal goes ahead**, as it already was when picking the door; a Player's request past the range is still refused. Before, the Judge's request could be refused after the spell slot was spent.
- **The follower roll (Compendium tab → ACKS Extras Macros → Generate Followers (Selected)) now blocks a Player and warns a Judge**: below the arrival level, or with no stronghold found, a Player is warned and the class prompt does not open, while a Judge hears the same warning and the prompt opens. Before, a Judge was stopped at the level gate and a Player with no stronghold was warned only after choosing a class.
- A player who owns the caster can press Apply on a spell's cast card in Chat (Chat tab → a spell's cast card) even when the Judge or another user cast it; the Judge's client carries it out after checking that player's ownership of the caster. Throw and Apply to the player's own targets stay with the user who cast.
- **A domain's sheet never loses a hidden holding when a player's seat saves it** (domain sheet → Holdings tab). Hidden holdings stay at their place in the list and are not shown to players.
- **A treasure pot's "Who rolls" now decides who deals it** (a pot's window → staging rail → Who rolls): with Table chosen and hoards listed that nobody has dealt, "Open to the table" offers the pot instead of dealing it, and a player who holds the pot sees "The table rolls this pot." and a "Deal n hoards" button where the cards would be (a pot's window → open it from the chat card). Pressing it deals the Judge's list, opens the pot and says so in chat under that player's name. A lair warning that stands is settled first in the rail, with "Open anyway" beside Cancel. The Judge's own Deal still works in both modes, and Judge mode or a pot with nothing to deal opens as before (T-0818).
- **A Player's trade can no longer set the merchant's Bargaining ranks or Charisma.** Those two inputs are drawn on a Judge's seat only, in a place's sheet → Trade tab → a catalog item's Buy window and a carried item's Sell window; the engine now reads them only from a Judge's request, where a Player's own payload used to be priced with them.
- **A locked container's Unlock, Empty and Unmake come to the owner who carries its key, and a Judge without the key is warned** (a character's sheet → Equipment tab → a locked container's row): an owner holding one of the container's keys can Unlock it, a Player with no key is not offered them and still has the pick and the break-open, and a Judge's press without the key goes ahead with a warning of what a Player would have been refused.
- **An employer can flip "players see details" on their own posting, and still sees their own candidates** (a place's sheet → Recruitment tab → Paid searches): the eye control now shows on postings whose employer you own, a player's flip is carried out through the Judge's client, and a candidate in a segment that a posting hides from others is no longer masked for the employer whose own active posting covers it.
- **A character dismissed on core's own sheet is free again everywhere the module asks who is in service.** Core's dismissal clears the employer and leaves the hireling mark on, and the module read the mark alone, so the character was left out of a hireling row's Transfer list, refused as a drop on a roster, missing from the employer choices of a new posting and of a recruit attempt, skipped by the wage sweep, given no Hireling roster button, and shown a Service tab for an employer it no longer has. A character with no employer now counts as free in each: the roster (a character's sheet → Followers tab → Roster; on the system's sheet, the Hireling roster button in the window header), a place's sheet → Recruitment tab → New Posting, the handshake on a candidate's row in a place's sheet, and the hireling's own sheet → Service tab.
- **A player's level-only post is refused as a criteria problem on a world with no arrival shares imported.** Posting from a place's sheet → Recruitment tab → New Posting, a Player who asks for the level-only post, or posts for a character they do not own, is now told what they may not ask for; the refusal for the missing arrival import used to come first and hid it. A Judge's post and a Player's permitted post still stop at the missing import.
- **An item's Source cell shows to the item's owner, as the character sheet's does** (an item's sheet → the editor rail → Source): it stays the Judge's only while the item is disguised or its magic is unidentified or undetected, since it names the item the document was made from.
- **A player whose character a Judge casts a spell for can now Throw, Apply to their own targets, Undo and Revert on the Judge's cast card.** On the card in the Chat sidebar tab, the owner of the caster sees Throw on an entry waiting for it (and on a row with no target), the Apply to my targets button on a card with no target, Undo on an entry within the reach of the spell's own figures, and Revert on a performed step. The throw dialog opens on the player's own seat and the roll posts as the player's, while a connected GM's client writes the result onto the card; with no GM connected the press asks first and spends nothing. A player who owns neither the caster nor the target still sees none of them.
- **The merchant's Charisma modifier starts blank, and hovering it names the page that prints a typical merchant.** On a Judge's seat it is drawn in a place's sheet → Trade tab → a catalog item's Buy window and a carried item's Sell window; it used to start at a figure of its own. Left blank, it adds nothing to the merchant's roll.
- **The importer's failure lines open with a sentence in the interface language, and every importer notification carries one module prefix.** A book that cannot be added to the server and a scan of the server's shelf folder that stops show the reason on the book's row or under the scan, in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Books tab; a table import that stops says so in a notification. Each line used to show only the raw English error, and now leads with the sentence and ends with that error as detail. A book-open warning about a different edition or printing is worded the same way. The notices that a book was opened, that its text was saved, refused or could not be read, and that tables were imported or skipped carried their own "ACKS Extras |" title, and now open with the module id like every other importer notice.
- **A container's key is listed once, and a compendium key opens its lock only for a copy of that key** (an item's sheet → the container's lock zone, where keys are dropped): dropping the same key twice, from its compendium and from a character's inventory, lists it once, and a key taken from a compendium no longer opens the lock for any item that merely shares its name.
- **A character dismissed from service on their sheet is a free character again in markets and on their own sheet.** Core leaves a dismissed character marked as a retainer with no employer, and they were still left out as a hireling: the buyer list of a place's sheet → Trade tab → a catalog item's Buy window and of its Commission window, and the character list of Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Market parties → Configure parties, passed them over, and their character sheet drew no Roster button on its Followers tab or in its party menu. They are listed and offered there again, and a location's occupant list shows them as an ordinary occupant rather than a henchman.
- **Token HUD → a condition on a monster: its Armor Class and saving-throw steps will be counted once when the ACKS II system starts counting them itself.** The system applies these steps only to characters today, so the module adds them for a monster, on attacks against it and on its saving throws. The module used to add them by actor type, so a system release that counted a monster's steps would have counted each one twice. Now it tests whether the system already counts the Armor Class step and stands down if so, and it keeps the saving-throw steps out of the save modifier the system reads. Nothing changes on the current system.
- **Dismissing a held conjuration's last creature ends the spell.** Once none of a held cast's creatures stands, the dismissal ends the caster's concentration with it; the held-end card that follows names the dismissal, lists no creature and turns nothing hostile. A cast with a creature still standing keeps its concentration. Character sheet → Magic tab → a summoning spell's Dismiss, or Dismiss on its card in the Chat tab.

- **A sideways market grid's duplicate-goods warning names the good by its printed label.** Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Import tab → **Import everything**, at *Importing market profiles…*: where two columns of a grid printed sideways feed one good, the "Market profiles: … printed good(s) feed a merchandise item another column already fills" warning now lists the good as the page labels it, as the unmapped-goods report already did, instead of by its column key.
- **Vehicle sheet → Seamanship block → Hazard throw dialog, and the Aboard tab's Seafaring field:** the Seafaring rank that makes a master mariner is read from your own copy of the Revised Rulebook when you import the voyages tables. The dialog's "Master mariner at the helm" box is ticked from the best Seafaring aboard once the rank is imported, and its label names the pages; the Travel tab's master-mariner tack line and the Seafaring field's ceiling follow the same rank. With nothing imported the box is left unticked, the field has no ceiling, and no vessel counts as having a master mariner.
- **A named item's bonus rows:** each row is labelled with the field its points go to (To hit, Damage, Armor class, Encumbrance, Power or spell), and the count beside it comes from the ladder the Judge set. Items sidebar → open a named weapon or armor → Effects tab → **The Name**.
- A monster's natural attacks take their damage type and their extraordinary mark from your own Monstrous Manual's Damage entry (MM Overview, p. 12) when Apply Stats or the first-pass monster import fills an actor: the damage type each natural weapon is listed against, and the hit dice and size an animal and the other listed types must pass, are read at import and editable under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables (the `monsters` document, tables `naturalWeaponDamage` and `extraordinaryRule`). A weapon the page does not list carries no damage type beyond the one its stat block's icon names, and with the rule's figures not imported an attack of a type that needs one carries no `extraordinary` flag. The library's `NATURAL_WEAPONS` carries keys and labels only, and the library API version is 42.
- **Importer, AX1 pregenerated characters:** a pregen whose page prints a portrait imports with it as the character's image; the others keep the default image, since their pages print none.
- **Ability sheet → Mechanics tab → a "Progresses As Class" row → Edit:** the fraction of class level a borrowed skill reads at is two number boxes, top and bottom, typed as the page writes it, in place of a pick from four fractions; blank is the whole level. The import reads the fraction from the words of your own copy of the book, so a skill taken at a fraction the old list did not name is imported at that fraction.
- A rider's body weight is read from the Revised Rulebook (RR p. 161) by the importer instead of a figure held in the module: until it is imported, a ridden mount's load, the rider's line on the monster sheet's Inventory tab and the Mount line on the character sheet's Equipment tab show no figure and their tooltips name the page, a mount or wagon is no longer refused or priced on a weight the module does not have, and a vehicle's hold counts a passenger of unknown weight for nothing.
- **Character sheet → Equipment tab, the ring place's slot badge:** the number of magic rings a wearer benefits from is read from your own copy of the Judges Journal when you import, and the badge's tooltip names the page. With nothing imported the badge shows the count with the figure left blank, its tooltip names where the book prints it, and the place is never marked as over. The item sheet's slot note for a ring carries the same figure and page.

A hire a player's request relayed to the Judge's seat, and a hire queued while no seat could create actors, are still paid as a player's.
- **The three slayer proficiencies carry the bonus their page prints at each level step, and the kinds it is against.** Imported with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Import tab → **Import everything**, two of them take their attack-throw bonus from the page's ladder at each level step, where each carried the first step's figure at every level, and the third carries its attack-throw and saving-throw bonuses; each names its kinds. The rows are listed with the modifiers an ability carries on a character sheet's Stats tab, and an ability's modifier row can be added as a quick effect, which applies to every roll while it is on. Nothing yet checks the target's kind, so none is applied against a target by itself.

The hireling's Service tab names which one a base is: hover the Morale figure. A troop hired through **Hire as Group** (location sheet → Mercenaries tab) from the combined composite/longbow entry now takes the base morale of its settlement's variant, where it had none.
- **An import no longer stops to ask which attacks a class's damage bonus applies to.** Importing a character whose class leaves that choice to the player (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Import tab) opened the Damage Bonus dialog and waited for an answer. The class is now applied with the choice left open and no bonus on either side, and the character sheet's Class tab shows a Damage Bonus row to make it on; once made it is kept for good. Applying the class by hand still asks.
- A group's unit Loyalty starts blank instead of a stored 0. On a group's sheet, in the Unit section, the Loyalty field (a mercenary unit's) is empty until a figure is typed, and its tooltip carries the page that prints the loyalty figures. A unit hired before this change keeps the 0 it stored.
- A past venture row's duty label follows the toll its entry was charged. On a market's Trade tab, the Judge's party lines and the entry card now name a tariff or a duty by the toll the party was charged at entry, instead of by the market's gate switch as it stands now, so flipping the switch after an entry no longer relabels it. An entry made before this change has no stored toll and reads the switch in force, as before.
- **A journey Encounter Zone throws the delve's imported die, not a fixed d6.** The zone throw on the encounter card (the **Encounter throw** quick tool of the Formation window on a journey, and the hex and End Day throws) rolls the die the importer reads from the Judge's Journal's wandering-monster passage (JJ p. 36), and its target is no longer cut off at 6. The die's tooltip names the page it was imported from, or says a Judge set it. With no die imported the zone throws nothing: the card still posts with the zone line, the die and roll left blank (the tooltip gives the page reference) and no outcome below it. The die is read, and can be overridden, under `Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`.

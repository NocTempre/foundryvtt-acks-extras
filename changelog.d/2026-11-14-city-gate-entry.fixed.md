- **Locations, City gate role:** a place marked *City gate* (Actors sidebar → the place's sheet → Contents tab → Role) is the point of entry to the market it stands inside. Its Contents tab names that market and offers **Enter the market**, which opens the market's sheet on its Ventures tab, where entry is charged (RR 372). A character standing at the gate counts as standing at the market for entering, trading and storing goods.

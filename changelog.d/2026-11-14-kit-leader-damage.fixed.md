- **A beastman leader generated in a troop kit takes the kit row's damage modifier plus what it has over its warrior's.** The modifier is worked out at generation, as the armour class already was, and written to both the melee and the missile modifier: Actors sidebar → a generated creature's sheet → Statblock tab → hover an attack's damage tag to see its parts and the row's page. Where a figure it needs is not imported the modifier stays blank and the tag shows the die alone, with the row's page as its tooltip. A rank and file keeps the row's modifier, and Unarmoured still changes the armour class alone.

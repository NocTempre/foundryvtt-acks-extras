- **An effect that sets or multiplies a figure says so wherever its step is named.** Hover an Armor Class figure or the saving throw modifier beside its label on the character sheet's Stats tab: the line for an effect that overrides, multiplies or bounds the figure now says the change's type after the effect's name, in Foundry's own word (Override, Multiply, Upgrade or Downgrade). The same word follows the effect's name on the attack card's line for a character target's Armor Class, on the parts of a saving throw, and in the notice that tells the table a monster's saving throw step. An effect that only adds or subtracts reads as before.

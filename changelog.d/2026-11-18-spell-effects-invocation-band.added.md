- **In the cast dialog (opened by using a spell from a character sheet), a casting tradition with an invocation cap now has one Invocation band between the Spell and Saves bands.** The band is headed by the tradition's name. Its Level menu offers the levels of the tradition's cap ladder and opens on the cap; for a Player the levels above the cap are disabled and say so, with the cap and its source in a tooltip; a Judge can choose one, in the warning colour, and the footer warns. The level you choose is the one the rows, the saves and the damage are read at, it is named on the cast card ("Invocation 3"), and a declared cast keeps it. Where a tradition asks for implements, the band's Implements list opens holding the sets the caster carries: drag another set from the inventory onto it to add one, X removes one, and a radio picks the set used. The pick opens on the lowest set whose effective level (level less damage) reaches the chosen level, a set too low is dimmed with its reason, and with none reaching it the footer blocks a Player and warns a Judge. In a begun fight, a spell declared from this dialog now resolves at the caster's turn with no dialog, on the targets, the level and the implement set declared.

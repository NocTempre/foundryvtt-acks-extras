- **A character wearing a helmet with a suit reads a lower Armor Class after the update, and that figure is the right one.** On the ACKS II system 14.2.0, releases before this one counted the suit's Armor Class twice for such a character. The module rebuilds each character's Equipment Loadout effect when the world loads (character sheet → Effects tab → Equipment Loadout), and the extra leaves it then. Nothing needs doing by hand.

- **Item sheet → Appearance tab → What The Players Know → Cursed:** a Judge marks a magical item as cursed (pencil armed). The players are shown nothing of it until the curse is known: a full identification sets it, or the Judge ticks **Curse known**, which appears beside Cursed once the item is cursed. Once shown, a Cursed tag sits in the item sheet's title band, and a Judge always sees it, with a tooltip that says whether the players have been told. It is a mark only: no curse effect is applied.

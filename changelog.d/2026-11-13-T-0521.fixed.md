- **A weapon drawn or swung in the first seconds after a reload is judged like any other.** The weapon table
  is read from your imported library a few seconds after the world loads, and a two-handed weapon drawn
  from its row's Draw control on the character sheet's Equipment tab in that window took one hand, while
  an attack rolled from the sheet's attack control carried no Non-proficient row for a weapon the
  character cannot use. A draw, a readied torch, a loadout rebuild and an attack roll now wait for the
  weapon table, so they read the same answer they read a minute after load. A world with no weapons
  imported does not wait longer than the load itself.

- **The scene screen** (opened with `acksExtras.sceneSearch.openScreen(partyId)` until the fight-over card and the Dungeon tab open it): a window for a party's scene search. Every entry at the place is a card with two checks, hastily and methodically, and you assign a character to a check from a picker on the card or by dragging the character's tile onto it. The Judge's header has **Show the scene**, which opens the screen on the players' seats; the bar has **Round** and **Spend a turn** (a player's press asks the Judge), what was found and what is untouched, and the log. The haul tray under the board holds one tile per dealt lot, and **Open the pot** opens the pot's window. A player reads only what the party has found.

- **Markets, the city's own gate toll:** a market can charge its city's printed gate toll and duty in place of its class's toll and tariff, set from Actors sidebar → the market's sheet → Ventures tab → **Gate toll**, which names the toll in force and its page (AX3 p. 56 for the city's own, RR 372 for the class's). The toll falls on the merchandise brought in and the duty on its value at this month's market price; the Enter dialog shows each as the span its throw can come to, with tooltips naming the parts, and the entry throws each once and takes both in one payment. Importing the AX3 capital's market sets its toll to the city's own.

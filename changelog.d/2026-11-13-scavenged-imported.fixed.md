The Condition control on a weapon's or armour's item sheet (Construction tab) offers and rolls the scavenged condition rows read from your own book, and the floor on stacked attack and armour class penalties is read from it too; both are editable under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables. Where the table is not imported the control says "not imported", offers no rows and refuses to roll; with no floor imported, penalties are not capped.

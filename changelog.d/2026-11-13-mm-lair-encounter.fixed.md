A Monstrous Manual monster whose entry prints a prepared lair or encounter, or a notes block, after its Spoils now carries that text on its sheet: Encounter for a lair or encounter and Notes for a notes block (Actors sidebar → the monster → its sheet's description). Its spoils stop above the label, and side-tab letters no longer creep into its prose. A lair's occupants no longer lend their figures to the monster's own defenses, effects or rolls. Monsters imported earlier gain this on Repair in place or a re-import (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports).

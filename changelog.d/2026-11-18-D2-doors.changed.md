- **The door window and the Dungeonbashing throw read the import.** In the door window (Scene controls → Walls → ACKS door actions, with a door selected), Construction lists the rows of the imported door grid (RR p. 266) and starts unset; it no longer assumes a door. A door with no row picked shows no battering time. A picked row shows its battering time and the tool its notes name, and the Batter button spends that time on the basher's formation clock, in rounds where the grid counts rounds. Needs, the modifiers (Strength, a second basher, a crowbar, size, spikes), the spike limit, the botch band and the rebound damage all come from the imported bash paragraph (RR p. 267); the target is the basher's own Dungeonbashing throw, so a token with none (a monster, or no token) cannot bash. Where a figure a bash applies is not imported, Needs is blank, Heave is disabled and no spike is hammered past an unknown limit; the Bash Door party check names the members it did not throw for. Re-run the Rules tables import to bring the door figures in; a door's earlier construction reads unset until a row is picked again.
- **Macros and companion modules → `acksExtras.formation.doors`:** `BASH_TARGET`, `BASH_STR_FACTOR` and `MAX_SPIKES` stay exported and are `null`, and `DOOR_KINDS` stays exported and is an empty list, since the figures now arrive from the import. Added the readers to use instead: `bashThrow(actor)` (the target, as the basher's own Dungeonbashing throw), `bashStrFactor()`, `maxSpikes()` and `doorRows()`, with `bashBotchBand()`, `bashReboundDamage()`, `doorRow(key)`, `doorKey(row)` and `bashStrength(actor, partner)` (a solo basher's own Strength adjustment, negative included; a pair takes the stronger), each figure null until imported. `bashStrBonus(strMod)` answers null for a non-zero modifier while the factor is not imported. `bashPlan` takes the basher's `target` (and `proficient`) and returns `ready`, `missing`, `needs` and `hopeless`; `bashDoor` answers `{ok: false, reason: "missing", missing}` or `"noThrow"` without rolling. `doorState().kind` is the key of an imported row (`"<ordinal>:<label>"`) or null; the five old kind names are gone. `batterPlan` and `batterDoor` return `count`, `unit`, `tool` and `rounds` in place of `turns`, and `batterPlan` answers `noRow` for a door with no row picked. `rollCheckFor` answers `{roll: null, row: null, missing}` for a Strength-scaled check whose factor is not imported.

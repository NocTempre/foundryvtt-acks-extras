- **Character sheet → Equipment tab → a weapon's attack (and every other attack button):** a weapon with no damage formula keeps it blank instead of being given a made-up die, including a named weapon with unlocked damage rungs, a scavenged or masterwork weapon, a Follower Card's new attack and a template package's weapon. The first attack with such a weapon asks for its formula and saves the answer to the weapon; closing the prompt cancels the attack.

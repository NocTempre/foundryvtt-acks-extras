- **For macros and modules calling the API: two payloads now carry an item's quantity as a whole number.** `acksExtras.classes.buildGearData` returns `data.system.quantity` as a number, and each snapshot in `acksExtras.markets.removeLoads`'s `removed` carries `system.quantity` as a number. Both used to be `{value, max}`, and both now follow the quantity field of system 14.2.0. A caller that read `system.quantity.value` reads `system.quantity`.

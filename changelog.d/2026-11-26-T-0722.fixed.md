- **A count printed as a die for every so many of the creature's own kind is now read into its encounter recipe.** A page that gives its dependants (the drudges and whelps of a village) as a die for every block of its bodies had that sentence skipped, so a Roll made none. The part now holds the die, the block size and what is counted, and a Roll throws the die once for each block the bodies make. The organisation chart's Roll panel (tool row, beside Roll at random) lists it under "This roll will make" as the die for every block, and the recipe editor shows the same on its row (Actors sidebar tab → the monster → Ecology tab → Encounter → Organisation…). Where the page prints no rounding for the blocks, the Roll panel's "Rounding where the page prints none" choice applies. The block size cannot yet be edited in the recipe editor: a die or figure typed in its Count box is the one thrown per block. A creature imported earlier takes the change with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, with its row ticked (Repair in place).

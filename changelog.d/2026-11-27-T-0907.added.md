- **A monster's Strength now adjusts its attacks.** A monster whose Strength a Judge entered (Monster sheet → Classification tab → Attributes) takes one more labelled row on the attack throw and the damage roll of its melee attacks, and on the damage roll of its thrown ones: for a natural attack the row reads from the Monstrous Manual's Strength table, by the score and the attack's average damage, and for a weapon marked "weapon" (the item sheet's ACKS Monster group) it is the system's own Strength modifier. The rows show in the attack dialog and on the posted card, not in the Monster sheet's Attacks tab figures, and a blank Strength adds nothing. The table arrives with the Monstrous Manual's rules tables (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Rules tables).

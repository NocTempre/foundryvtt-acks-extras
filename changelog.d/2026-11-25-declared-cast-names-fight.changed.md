- **A declared cast says which fight holds it.** Actors sidebar tab → the character's sheet → Magic tab → Cast, in a fight where it is not the caster's turn: the foot of the cast window names the fight's scene and round beside Declare and says the slot is spent at Declare. The spell's row on the Magic tab then shows a Declared tag, with the same in its tooltip, until the caster's turn resolves it.

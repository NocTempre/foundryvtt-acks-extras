- **A place's owner administers it, in place of the Judge's seat alone.** A Player who owns a place (every hand-made place opens to the table at Owner; a Judge lowers a seat in Configure Ownership) now has, from the place's sheet from the Actors sidebar tab:
  - a **Settings** tab (it was "GM Settings"), holding the stack fields, **Split** where the seat may create an actor, **Unlink map**, the market's configuration, the demographics (its **Export** where the seat may create a table) and **Remove market**. The Judge's slander section is drawn on a Judge's seat alone, and Things and GM View stay the Judge's;
  - **Add market**, in the sheet's header and in the Settings tab's no-market block; on the **Contents** tab, the role select, an occupant's note edit, nesting a place by dropping one the seat owns, and **Detach** on a row the seat owns;
  - on the **Recruitment**, **Henchmen**, **Mercenaries** and **Specialists** tabs: removing a candidate and a special hire's note, limit and removal, and listing an actor for hire by dropping it on **Recruitment** when the seat owns both the place and the actor;
  - on the **Storage** tab: the house bucket's **Take** and its lock (now labelled **Retrievable**; a spoil row reads "released by the place's owner"), **Hold goods here**, and a compendium item dropped on the sheet, filed under the house.

  **Remove market** on a seat that is not a Judge's removes the market whole, the Judge's slander with it, and its confirm lists no slander line. A place a seat only observes shows none of these controls.

Hire as Group (location sheet → Mercenaries tab) no longer stores a base morale of 0 for a troop type whose figure your book import did not supply. The stack keeps no figure, its unit morale counts none for it, and the hire warns that the troop type's base morale is not imported; the figure is editable under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables. A candidate's age (location sheet → the Age column of the Henchmen and Specialists tabs and of the directed-search list on the Recruitment tab) is no longer invented when the NPC age table is not imported or holds no figure for the class: the cell is blank, and so is the age-based proficiency count of a 0th-level candidate.

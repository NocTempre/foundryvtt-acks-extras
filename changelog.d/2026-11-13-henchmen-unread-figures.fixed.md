Hire as Group (location sheet → Mercenaries tab) stores no base morale for a troop type whose figure your book import did not supply: its unit morale counts none for it, and the hire warns that the troop type's base morale is not imported; the figure is editable under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables. A candidate's age (location sheet → the Age column of the Henchmen and Specialists tabs and of the directed-search list on the Recruitment tab) is blank when the NPC age table is not imported or holds no figure for the class, and so is the age-based proficiency count of a 0th-level candidate.

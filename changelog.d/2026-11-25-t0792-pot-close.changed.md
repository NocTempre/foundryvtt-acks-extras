- **Closing a treasure pot is the pile's owner's.** In the Treasure window, opened from the doorway card in the chat log, the **Close the pot** control now shows to a seat that owns the pot's pile, not only to a Judge, and its place list offers the places that seat owns. A player the Judge has set to OWNER of the pile in Configure Ownership can close it, filing what is left at a place they own and turning the doorway card into the record; a player with less than OWNER sees no Close, and a close sent for a place they do not own is refused. Opening a pot still gives players LIMITED, so no player owns a pot until a Judge grants it. A Judge's window and Close are unchanged.

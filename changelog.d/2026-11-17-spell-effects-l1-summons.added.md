- **Casting a summoning or animation spell (Character sheet → Magic tab → Cast):** a calling, a summoning, a conjuration and two animation spells now carry a summon row read from your own copy of the book, so the creature prompt offers the creature the page names (or the creature list, where the page names none), opens the count on the page's own count where it prints one, and refuses a count over it. The summoning spell whose count scales with the caster's level states that ceiling on its row and is not capped at one. A summoning held by concentration now leaves the caster's concentration record, as any concentration spell does, and casting a new summoning while holding one stops the old concentration with a warning that names both, whether the caster is a Player or the Judge. Ending a held summoning posts the usual end card in the Chat tab, with Restore and leaves its creatures on the map. Cast during a fight, a summons resolves at once and is not declared.

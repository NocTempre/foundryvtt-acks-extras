- **Pressing Unarmed Strike on a character sheet's Rolls tab, under Attack, with no unarmed die imported asks for the damage die in a window of its own.** A Judge can tick *Save as this world's unarmed die* there to keep the answer, and later presses ask nothing; the kept die is the world's override of the unarmed strike table, shown and cleared in the Ruledata Browser (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser). A player's window has the field and Attack only, and its die applies to that throw. An answer that is not one die written like 1d6 is refused on the window, and closing the window cancels the attack. A spell's attack whose row states no roll still asks nothing and rolls no damage.

- **Pressing Unarmed Strike on a character sheet's Rolls tab, under Attack, with no unarmed die set asks for the damage die in a window of its own.** A Judge can tick *Save as this world's unarmed die* there to keep it for every character; the kept die is the world's override of the unarmed strike table, shown and cleared in the Ruledata Browser (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser). A player who owns the character sees *Save as this character's unarmed die* instead: the die is kept on that character, its Unarmed Strike row on the Rolls tab shows it with a *set by hand* mark and a Clear control, and the Judge is whispered a card with one press that makes it the world's die. A seat that owns nothing has the field and Attack only. An answer that is not one die written like 1d6 is refused on the window, and closing the window cancels the attack.

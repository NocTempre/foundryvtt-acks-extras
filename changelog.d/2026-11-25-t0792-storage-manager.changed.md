- **The storage manager opens for any player and works on the places they own, in place of the Judge's seat alone.** Open it from a place sheet's **Storage** tab → **Open manager** (on a place the seat owns), from Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → **Storage manager**, or from the Compendium Packs tab → ACKS Extras Macros → **Open Storage Manager**. It lists, and moves, reassigns and turns off storage for, only the places the seat owns, a player's own vault included; a place the seat does not own is not listed, counted or named, and **Reassign owner** offers only characters the seat can see. **Add a place** lists the actors the seat owns. **Give a character a vault** and the **a new location** move target show for a seat that may create actors in Foundry's own Permission Configuration, and for no one else. The macros **Enable Storage Here** and **Disable Storage Here**, from the same compendium, work on an actor the seat owns and warn "You do not own …" on one it does not. A Judge's manager is unchanged.

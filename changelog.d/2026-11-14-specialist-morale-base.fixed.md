A specialist hired from the market (location sheet → Specialists tab → **Attempt to recruit**) starts with the base morale your book prints for its kind of specialist (RR p. 166), read from the imported Rules tables; a kind whose figure is not imported has no recorded base and shows the hireling sheet's own Morale field instead, and a henchman still does, as before. The hireling's Service tab names which one a base is: hover the Morale figure. A troop hired through **Hire as Group** (location sheet → Mercenaries tab) from the combined composite/longbow entry now takes the base morale of its settlement's variant, where it had none.

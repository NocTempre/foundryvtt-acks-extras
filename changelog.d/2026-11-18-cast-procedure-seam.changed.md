- **On a character sheet, casting a spell (the cast dialog):** in a begun fight, a spell whose casting time is longer than a round is no longer declared. A Player's cast of one is refused with a notice, and a Judge's cast is warned and resolves at its confirm, with the card naming the time. A ritual spell is likewise refused to a Player and warned for a Judge until rituals are cast from the dialog. A casting tradition can now add its own fields and its own band to the dialog's Invocation band, findings to the footer, and a step that runs before the rows, which the Judge can revert from the card.

- **A place's Storage tab shows to a seat that observes the place, not only to a Judge.** Open the place from the Actors sidebar tab and its **Storage** tab lists whose goods are held there and the till's total, to a Player at Observer or Owner of the place; a Player at Limited or below has no tab. Its **Deposit** and **Take** controls work on a sheet the seat cannot edit, each moving goods through the storage relay for a character the seat owns; **Take** is offered on the rows stamped to such a character and on a house row a Judge marked retrievable. **Open manager** stays the Judge's. A ground pile takes no deposit from anyone, a Judge included.
- **A pile's Open follows the same test.** On the character sheet → Equipment tab → Kept elsewhere, a pile's row has **Open** for a seat that observes the pile (every seat does unless a Judge lowers the pile's default in Configure Ownership), and it opens the pile's own sheet with its Storage tab listing the rows. The system sheet's Storage tab does the same.

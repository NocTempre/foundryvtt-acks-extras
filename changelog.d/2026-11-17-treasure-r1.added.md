- **Scene controls → Token controls → New treasure pot (Judge only):** a window a table turns treasure over in. A Judge drops items and coin onto the pot, adds a value card, and drops seeds into the Add and Replace buckets beneath it, then presses **Open to the table**. A chat card, "Treasure on the table", opens the pot's window on every seat: each card shows the pool it belongs to and its value band on a face-down back, a player turns one by pressing it, and **Take** puts a turned card on a character the player owns. A magic item a Judge left undetected shows as an ordinary item with no aura, and its pool reads "Item" until it is identified. The Judge closes the pot, filing what is left at a place if one is chosen, and the chat card becomes the record of the haul.

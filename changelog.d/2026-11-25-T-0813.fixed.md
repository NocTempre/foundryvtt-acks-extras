- **A place made from a point of interest nobody has found is no longer listed for players with the Judge's note on it** (party sheet → Wilderness tab → Points of interest → **Make it a place**). The place was made open to every player and only its token was hidden, so it stood in their Actors tab and its sheet showed the point's note. It is now made with ownership None for players. When the Judge shows its token, the place opens to the table as any other place made by hand does; if the Judge changed its ownership before that, it is left as set. Hiding the token again does not close it. This keeps the place off a player's Actors tab and keeps its sheet shut; Foundry still sends every world actor to every connected client, so it is no protection against a player who reads their browser's data.

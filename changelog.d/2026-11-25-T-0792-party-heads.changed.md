- A player who owns a member of a party can now press the head of the party sheet's tab for the mode the party is in (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the Dungeon, City or Wilderness tab → the tab's head). In a dungeon and in a city that is Rest a turn and Hurried (the pace figure beside the picture presses too), and in a city Hop to a place the Judge has not hidden; on a journey it is Next hex, Work the country and End day. Each press runs on the Judge's client and is announced to the table with the player's name. Turn and Round ask the Judge instead, who still presses them, and a line under the pills says so. Undo and Hunted here stay the Judge's, and a player who owns none of the party's members sees the heads without these presses.
- Pressing End day from a player's seat now also answers the day's end the Judge was asked about (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Wilderness tab → End day). A Judge's dialog that was still open then closes nothing when answered, so the day ends once.
- Hover the title of a Dungeon, City or Wilderness head (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the tab's head) for the figures it prints and the page they come from.

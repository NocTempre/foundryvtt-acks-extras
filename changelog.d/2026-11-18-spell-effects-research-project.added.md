- **A Research tab on a place (Actors tab → a location → its sheet → Research tab, after Storage):** research is kept as a project at the place where it is done, not on the researcher. A Judge writes the value of the place's library and workshop in gold, and each project reads the one its kind needs against the minimum read from your Revised Rulebook, and the throw shows the bonus the place gives. A project below its minimum is refused to a Player and warned to a Judge; with nothing imported (Import Everything, or the **Reimport One Shelf (GM)** macro in `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`) it is neither, and the facility chip carries the page reference alone. A project holds its kind and subject, the level, the three costs as due (a Judge enters them), a researcher and assistants picked from the characters on the place's roster, the material paid, the research credited, the components dropped into its bucket and the throw. A Player starts projects for the characters they own and keeps their assistants and components; a Player who cannot edit the place is relayed to a connected Judge. Posting research days and finishing a project are not built yet: Finish is drawn disabled.

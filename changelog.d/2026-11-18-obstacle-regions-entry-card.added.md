- **Party token enters a region whose place holds an obstacle:** the Judges get one card per move, a row per obstacle with the figures typed on the place and a **Begin** button, and the place's conditions. Begin opens the party sheet's crossing (**Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing**) already set up from that obstacle and every condition of the places the party stands in; with a crossing open, it asks to end that one first. A move a trap wall halts posts no card, and a place with no obstacle posts none.

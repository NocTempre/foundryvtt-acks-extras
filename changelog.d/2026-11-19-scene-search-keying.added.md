- **A place's sheet → Things here tab (Judges only), and the scene screen's left aside (the party sheet's Dungeon tab → Search here, or the fight-over card's Search the scene, opens the screen):** the Judge keys what a place holds. The tab lists the place's things (a trap, an obstacle or a condition dropped on it, or on its area on the map), each with a summary of its two checks and a flag. Opening a thing shows the keying rail, the same fields as the scene screen's left aside for the entry selected there: Hidden from the table and Behind (the clue that shows it), the optional Something more line, and for Checked hastily and Checked methodically the check (no throw, the party's search, or a proficiency), Others may look hastily, the clue it yields, and, for the methodical check, the tools it needs (drop an item) and its turns, left blank until the Judge writes one. Show to the table shows the entry now; Remove takes the thing off the place. A trap, obstacle or condition item dropped on the tab becomes a thing of the place; any other item dropped there still goes to the place's goods.

- **A family template shows the family's own picture.** The Beastman template showed its first breed's portrait, and the full-page picture of the Monstrous Manual's Beastman section was imported nowhere. A family whose overview prints a picture of its own (in the Monstrous Manual, Beastman and Lycanthrope) now takes it on an import, and each variant keeps its portrait: Compendium Packs sidebar tab → ACKS II — Extras → From your books → ACKS Cookbook — Actor → Beastman. A template imported before this release takes it on a repair with the Monstrous Manual open: Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, with the family's row ticked. The repair writes the family's picture over whatever the library's copy holds; a copy you dragged out of the library is not touched.

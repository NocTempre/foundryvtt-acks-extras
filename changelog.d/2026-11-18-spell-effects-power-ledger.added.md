- **A power ledger on the casting strip (Actors tab → a character → its sheet → Magic tab → the casting strip, and the same strip on the system's own sheet):** each character keeps the divine and arcane power it has gained and spent, apart from its casting pools, so a rest does not clear it. A tag per power carries the balance, and its tooltip lists the entries, the next to fade and the page. The entries are rows below, newest first, each led by its source's own picture where it has one. Power fades only once you have read how long it keeps from your Revised Rulebook (Import Everything, or the **Reimport One Shelf (GM)** macro in `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`); until then nothing fades. Arcane power's page names the unit and no count, so the Judge types the count in the box beside the arcane tag, and it stays blank until set. A Judge has a plus and a minus on each tag (one power at a time) and Undo on a spend; a Player's strip shows the power it holds and draws none of them. A Player's spend past what is held is refused, and a Judge's takes what is held and warns. A player who cannot write the character is relayed to a connected Judge. Nothing spends or gains power yet but the Judge's plus and minus; the ritual and sacrifice windows will.

- **A condition's figure is applied once, by the system's own condition effect.** For Berserk, Blessed, Blinded, Charging, Disordered, Hungry, Infuriated, Inspired, Prone, Queasy and Shaken, the effect that Token HUD → Status Effects makes now carries the ACKS II system's own modifiers, and the system applies them to attack throws, a character's Armor Class, saving throws and damage; the module no longer adds a second copy. Where the system's effect does not reach (a monster's Armor Class and saving throws), the module reads the figure from the effect and applies it, and a condition's other figures (morale, surprise, speed, a thrown Berserk attack) stay the module's and still come from your imported books. A condition set before this update carries no figure until it is taken off and set again.

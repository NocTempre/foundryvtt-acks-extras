- **An undetected magic item ranks at what it seems to be worth.** In a treasure
  pot (Scene controls → Token controls → New treasure pot), a magic card nobody
  has detected now takes its band from the apparent value your imported book
  prints for the item, unless the item carries a disguise of its own, which
  keeps its own cost. A seed hidden by Replace weighs the same. A find with no
  printed apparent value still ranks lowest.

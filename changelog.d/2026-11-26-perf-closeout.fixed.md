- **Moving a token no longer redraws every open actor sheet on every seat.** A token's move, or a change to any Region, re-rendered every open sheet at every table seat, on any scene. Now a move reaches a sheet only where its scene holds an item's reach, only the character and monster sheets whose body stands on that scene, and once for a burst of moves: Actors sidebar tab → a character's or monster's sheet, its "In reach of" strip and its gear rows' ring counts.
- **A Judge's seat no longer recounts every group in the world on each frame a group's token is drawn.** The count beside a group token's name (a group revealed by its noun) was rebuilt on every animation frame. It is now rebuilt when the token's nameplate is, and when a group in its tree changes, is made or is deleted.
- **A character's sheet reads its hirelings once for a render instead of twice**: Actors sidebar tab → a character's sheet → Followers tab.

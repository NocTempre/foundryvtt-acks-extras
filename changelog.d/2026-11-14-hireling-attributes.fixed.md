A hireling's six attributes are rolled at hire with the dice your Judges Journal prints for a prospective henchman's attributes (JJ p. 252), read from the imported Rules tables and editable in the Ruledata Browser (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser), where the module rolled them from its own fixed formula and cited Revised Rulebook p. 168; with that table not imported nothing is rolled, the hireling keeps the character sheet's own scores, and the Service tab on the hireling's sheet has no Attributes row. Hovering the row now names the page.

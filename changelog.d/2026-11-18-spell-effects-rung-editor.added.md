- **A Judge edits an imported invocation-level table.** A spell's figure that
  varies by invocation level now has an inline rung editor. In the effect-row
  editor (a spell sheet's Mechanics tab, the row's Edit button) and in the
  spell sheet's Overview tab, a level-value figure's kind switch is now a
  button group (Flat, By caster level, By invocation level). On By invocation
  level the rungs show under it as a level and the figure that holds from it.
  Add, remove and change rungs; they stay a draft until you save them, so no
  invalid ladder is ever written. Levels must be whole numbers and rise, and
  Save row (pinned at the foot of the effect-row editor; under the figure on
  the Overview) stays off, with the reason beside it, until they do. A figure
  left blank stays blank. A rung you changed gets a gold edge, and its tooltip
  names the imported figure it replaced and the page it came from; Restore
  imported puts changed rungs back and drops added ones. A Player sees the
  rungs read-only.

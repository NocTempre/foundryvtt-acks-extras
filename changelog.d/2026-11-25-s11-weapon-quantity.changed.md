- **A weapon or a suit of armour resting in a stack is offered the count controls any other gear has, and every list that shows a count shows it.** On the character sheet's Equipment tab (Actors sidebar tab → a character → Equipment tab) a resting stack's Divide control opens the prompt for how many to take off, and hovering its ×N names the figure's source, the item's Quantity. Draw and Equip on a resting stack say they take one and what the stack keeps, and every state control there carries a name for a screen reader. A row in use, drawn, worn or thrown, is offered no count control: the field that holds its count stays editable on its item sheet, and its list rows still print the count.
- **An item sheet's Details tab opens with Quantity for a weapon and for armour, as it does for other gear** (an item's sheet → Details tab, and the pencil in its editor rail unlocks it). The count in the title band and this field show one value, and a container's Contents tab prints a count only above one.
- **Goods kept at a place show a weapon stack's count and take back part of it.** A stack of three spears stored at a place reads ×3 and its take-how-many field takes two back, in the place sheet's Storage tab (Actors sidebar tab → a place → Storage tab), the character sheet's Storage tab, and the storage manager (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Storage manager`). The picker that opens from **Leave something here** shows the same count with the same tooltip and a how-many box for a resting stack (T-0746).
- **The same count reaches the other windows that list a stack.** A market's sale dialog, opened from the sell control in a place's Trade tab → Sell goods, asks how many of a resting weapon or armour stack to sell. A vehicle's Hold tab offers its count field on a resting stack. The Follower Card's wear control on a resting stack says it wears one and what the stack keeps (the follower's sheet → Equipment), and a monster sheet's Inventory tab prints ×N beside a carried item above one, with its source in the tooltip.

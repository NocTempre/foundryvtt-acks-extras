- **Character sheet → Magic tab → a sealing spell's Cast → the door pick:** casting a spell that seals a way now asks for a door on the scene in view before the slot is spent. Click a door to lock it, press Enter to cast with no door, or right-click or press Escape to cancel at no cost. A Player is offered only the doors they can see and is refused a door beyond the spell's range where they click, while a Judge is warned and may seal it anyway. The door stays locked while the spell lasts and is closed again when the spell ends, unless a hand has since unlocked or opened it or it was already locked before the spell. A Player is refused before the pick when no Judge is connected.

- **Items sidebar → a Trap item:** the trap sheet scrolls inside its form, so a field changed low on the sheet keeps its place, and it opens as wide as the seat's type size asks. The level strip says its state in words: a line gives the level on screen and how many levels are filled, and each step names itself to screen readers and in its tooltip. The Pit damage line stays when no dice are imported, with its figure blank and its tooltip naming the depth, the fall dice and the spike dice with the page each was read from, or the page reference alone for a part not imported. The Springs-on field gains an In force line showing the band the trap uses, with a tooltip saying whether it is the trap's own or the imported one.
- **Scene controls → Regions → the region → Behaviors → Trap Zone:** the State select lists armed, spotted, disarmed and spent in words rather than raw ids, and the Known checkbox carries a label and a hint.

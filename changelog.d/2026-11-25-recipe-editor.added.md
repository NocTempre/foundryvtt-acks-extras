- **The recipe editor opens from three places.** `Actors sidebar tab → a monster → its sheet → Ecology tab → Encounter → Organisation…` edits the monster's own recipe; `Actors sidebar tab → a family template → its sheet → Edit structure → an option of the variant axis → Recipe…` edits that option's recipe; `Actors sidebar tab → a faction or a group → Org chart tab → Recipe…` edits the option the chart's Roll recorded, which is the recipe the next Roll reads. Each change is written as it is made, a part changed by hand is marked, a part resets to the import alone or all at once (Reset all has no undo), and a mount or beast part links to its creature's own entry.

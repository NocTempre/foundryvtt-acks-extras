- **A chart row can be deleted, with a warning, and the Delete can be undone.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a faction, its Org chart tab, press a card's pen, and use **Delete…** in the form's foot (shown only to a seat that owns the faction and may delete every group the Delete takes). The warning lists the rows and their groups, the prototypes the chart's Rolls minted that no other group uses, and one tick, on by default, for the linked actors (the officers) you may delete; an officer you may not delete is listed as staying. "Don't warn me before a Delete again" saves a one-click Delete with that choice. After every Delete a strip at the top of the Org chart tab offers **Undo**, which brings back the rows, groups, minted actors and chosen linked actors with the same ids until the page is reloaded. The warning has its own setting under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → **Warn before deleting chart rows**; turn it back on there. A row whose group was deleted from the Actors tab now reads "Its group was deleted outside the chart" and offers Roll and Remove from chart. Delete is not offered on the row that holds the sheet's own group: delete that group from the Actors sidebar tab.

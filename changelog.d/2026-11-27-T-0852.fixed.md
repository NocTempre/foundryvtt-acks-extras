- **A group row rolled for an unmatched creature word now keeps its printed hit points.** Where a roll's stack names a creature no library entry answered, the figure the roll prints for its bodies is stored on that stack (Actors tab → the group's sheet → its stack row), so once a Judge drops a creature on the row its bodies take that figure instead of rolling hit dice. A stack still holding no creature rolls no hit dice at all: the row's Materialize button makes no body until a creature is dropped on it.

- **A group's body keeps a size a hand gave its token when the group is gathered and sent out again, and the scene's auto-size leaves that size alone.** Resize a body's token (its width and height, or its texture scale) and gather the group; the next send-out lays the size back. A remembered width and height go back with the footprint lock, so a scene with auto-size on does not re-size them, and Scene controls → Battlemap → Map setup → Token sizes → Rescale tokens on scene skips such a token until the arrow button (Reset to automatic) in the same Token sizes section is pressed on it. A body nobody resized is sent out at its prototype's size, and an auto-sizing scene sizes it as before.

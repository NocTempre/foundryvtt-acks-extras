- **Casting a light spell now lights with the reach your book prints, and the perpetual form can be cast.** The importer now reads the bright and dim radius of RR's timed illumination spell, and the daylight, bright and dim radius of the perpetual one, from your own copy, so the cast light on the party sheet's Kit tab (Actors sidebar tab → the party's sheet → Kit tab → Light Sources) carries a reach instead of a blank. The perpetual form is offered in the Cast a light spell row and, once cast, stays lit until its owner removes it. Spells already in a world need a re-import: Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, and pick the two illumination spells. A radius you typed on a spell's Light row is kept and listed as held, not overwritten.

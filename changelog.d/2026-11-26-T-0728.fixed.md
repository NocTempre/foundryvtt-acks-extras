- **A monster's weapon now lists the special maneuvers on its Rolls tab, as a character's does.** With Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Overlay: combat maneuvers on, a weapon held by a monster showed no maneuver rows at all, so a Judge had to script one to make a monster disarm or trip. Now the weapon's item sheet (Actors sidebar tab → the monster → its weapon → Rolls tab) lists the same rows with the same penalties, and pressing one rolls the monster's attack with that maneuver declared.

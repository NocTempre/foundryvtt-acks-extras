- **A beastman's optional equipment writes its figures.** A creature made from the beastman template with an Optional equipment piece picked (Compendium Packs sidebar tab → ACKS II — Extras → From your books → ACKS Cookbook — Actor → the beastman template's sheet → Generate) now carries what that piece's page prints, read from your own copy: a marking's Armor Class bump, with its part and page in the Armor Class hover on the Full Monster sheet, and its reduction per die against mundane weapons, shown on the Apply card of a hit; an armour's extra damage on an overrun or a wrestle, added as a row on the attack card; and the hooked flag on its melee weapons, which puts the hooked part on a disarm. A creature made with no piece picked gets none of it. Any weapon is hooked when its sheet's tags carry `hooked`, and the Disarm row on its Rolls tab then shows the penalty with the hooked part and its page in the tooltip. The extra damage and the hooked part follow Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Overlay: combat maneuvers.

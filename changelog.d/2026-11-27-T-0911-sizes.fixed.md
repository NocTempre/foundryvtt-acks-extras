- A ration's size and a water vessel's capacity now come from the imported book, and a food or water item with no size is left out of the camp's count instead of being counted as one day. To set or change one by hand, open the item (Items sidebar tab, or Actors sidebar tab → an actor → its Inventory tab → the item's row), arm its sheet with the pencil, and use the **Provision** controls on its **Details** tab: *Provides*, *Days per unit* and, for water, *Left*. Library items imported before this read their sizes once their entries are imported again (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries); a copy already on a character keeps reading unsized until a size is typed in its Days per unit.

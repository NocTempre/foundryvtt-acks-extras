- **Light sensitivity on the attack throw:** an ability's attack throw modifier can now apply only in daylight. Set it on an ability sheet's Mechanics tab: open the modifier row in the effect row editor and choose a level under "Only at light level". The attack dialog then lists the penalty, labelled with the ability, the light level and its page reference, whenever the attacker's token stands in daylight. Daylight is open sky in a scene whose global light is on, or a magic light's daylight range; an area marked indoors keeps the sky out. It works for characters and monsters alike, and a fresh monster import brings the penalty in from entries whose page prints one. Saving throws, proficiency throws and morale do not read it yet.

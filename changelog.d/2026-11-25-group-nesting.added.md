- **A group can stand inside another group.** A gang's group can be placed inside a warband's group; the warband's token, added to a combat from the combat tracker, puts every body of every group inside it on the map, one combatant each, and the end of the combat (or adding the token again) gathers them all back into their own groups. A party member that holds a group deploys and recalls the same bodies. A group's count on a location's occupants, a faction's members and the location's total is now the group's living bodies read at that moment, with the groups inside it counted. A stack made by the encounter generator shows a note where the Roll button would be (its count is changed by hand) and a hover on its count names its part and page. A stack with no creature behind it, when dropped on the map, deployed from its row or carried in by its group's token, whispers the Judges instead of doing nothing.

- **A Judge named as an owner no longer puts a character within reach of a place.** Foundry names whoever
  creates a character or a place as its owner, so a character and a place one Judge made shared an owner, and
  the character could leave goods and coin at the place from anywhere. A character marching beside a
  Judge-made NPC reached every place that Judge made the same way. A Judge, an assistant Judge included, no
  longer counts as an owner when a deposit, a coin payment or an exchange asks whether a character reaches a
  place; a player who owns both, a pin, standing at the place and a vault all still do. Retrieving goods from a
  place stays open to the character's owner.

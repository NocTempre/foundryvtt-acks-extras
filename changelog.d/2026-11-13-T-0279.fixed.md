- **A character reaches a place by its player's ownership, a pin, standing at it or a vault; a Judge named as an
  owner does not count.** Foundry names whoever creates a character or a place as its owner, so a deposit, a coin
  payment or an exchange does not count a Judge, an assistant Judge included, as an owner when it asks whether a
  character reaches a place. A Judge-made character and an unlinked place that Judge made, or a Judge-only NPC and
  any unlinked place, therefore do not reach each other by that shared owner. To put a place within reach, pin it to
  the character (drag the place from the Actors sidebar tab onto the character's sheet), give the player ownership
  of it (Actors sidebar tab → right-click the place → Ownership), or stand the character at it. Retrieving goods
  from a place stays open to the character's owner.

- **Party sheet → Order tab → Skill audit:** each party's audit is a window of its own, so a second party's audit, or a second press, no longer kills the first one's buttons. The window now redraws when a member joins or leaves and when a member's ability is added, changed or removed. Its scroll position survives a ruling, a roll or a select change, and focus returns to the control you used. The tables scroll sideways instead of cutting off their right-hand columns, and the window, its selects and its inputs follow the type size. On an ability item's sheet, the Skill tab's select and number field follow it too.

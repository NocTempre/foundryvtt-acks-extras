- **The power ledger's Fades / Stays switch now holds its setting, and its minus follows it (Actors tab → a character → its sheet → Magic tab → casting strip, and the same strip on the system's own sheet; Judge only):** the switch used to fall back to Fades after every plus or minus, so a Judge had to press Stays again before each point that should stay. It now keeps what you pressed, for each power, for as long as that character sheet window stays open; a second window on the same character starts on Fades, and so does the same sheet closed and opened again. Nothing is written to the character for it. With a power's switch on **Stays**, its minus removes a point that stays, oldest first, and leaves the points that fade; with a power with no point that stays, it writes nothing and warns. On **Fades** the minus spends as before, points that fade first. A removal shows on its row as "Removed a point that stays" and has Undo like any spend, and the minus's label names what it does. A Player never gets the removal: it is refused.

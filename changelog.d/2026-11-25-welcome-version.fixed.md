- **The browser console of a Player who loads the world after a system update, before a Gamemaster has, no longer logs a permission error.** The system stores its new version in a world setting on every seat that loads, and a Player's write is refused by the server. The module now sends that write only from a seat allowed to change settings. The welcome window still shows to the Player on load, until a Gamemaster has loaded the world on the new version.

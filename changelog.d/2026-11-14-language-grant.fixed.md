- **Languages a proficiency or power grants:** the Language proficiency and the Bonus Languages power grant the number of languages their own pages print (RR p. 115, JJ p. 313), read from your books when the ability is imported; an ability with no figure grants none. Items sidebar → open an ability → Mechanics tab → **Languages granted** shows the figure, blank until one is imported or typed, and hovering it names the page the ability was imported from. Items sidebar → **Update Abilities** fills a missing figure and keeps one you typed. The lib API is version 44: `classes.languages` exports no per-grant count.

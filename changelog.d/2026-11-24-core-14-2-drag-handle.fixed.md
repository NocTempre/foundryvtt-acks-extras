- **A weapon's or armour's quantity can be selected again on the system's character sheet.** On the ACKS II system 14.2.0 an inventory row is dragged by its name, so its quantity box can be clicked and its text selected; the module had been marking the whole row as a drag handle again and undid that. Only a coin row, which has no name to grab, is now marked as draggable: character sheet (the system's own sheet) → Inventory tab → the weapon, armour and money rows.

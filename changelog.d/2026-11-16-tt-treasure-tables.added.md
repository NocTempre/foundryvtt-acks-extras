- **Imported rules tables → Treasure:** the Judges Journal's treasure tables (the Classic and Heroic treasure types, gem and jewelry values, and the eleven special-treasure lists) are read from your own Journal and appear in the Ruledata Browser (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`); the value lists and the special-treasure lists are also RollTables you can roll and edit there.

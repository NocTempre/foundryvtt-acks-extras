- **A power's frequency shows the words your book prints.** An ability, a monster power or an item power whose
  page gives one of three frequencies the module names only by number now shows the phrase the importer read
  from your copy, in the ability sheet's effects list and the monster sheet's ability line, where it showed a label
  that spelled the frequency out. Picking one of those frequencies by hand, in the Usage list of a monster
  ability's item sheet (the "ACKS Monster" section) or in an effect row's editor, lists it as "Printed frequency 1",
  "2" or "3" (from the book) and shows no phrase. Re-import the book that holds the power (Settings tab, Game
  Settings, ACKS II — Extras, Manage imports) to refresh an imported row; a frequency an earlier import stored
  does not resolve, and no migration is built.

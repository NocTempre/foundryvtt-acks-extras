- **A power's frequency shows the words your book prints.** An ability, a monster power or an item power whose
  page gives one of three frequencies the module names only by number shows the phrase the importer read from
  your copy, in the ability sheet's effects list and the monster sheet's ability line. Picking one of those
  frequencies by hand, in the Usage list of a monster ability's item sheet (the "ACKS Monster" section) or in an
  effect row's editor, lists it as "Printed frequency 1", "2" or "3" (from the book) and shows no phrase. The
  phrase arrives when the book that holds the power is imported (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras →
  Manage imports).

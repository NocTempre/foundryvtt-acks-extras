- **A Player's summoning spell calls only a creature that Player can open.** A row on a spell (the spell's sheet → Mechanics tab → a summon row's creature field) could name any creature by its uuid or its name, one of the Judge's own included, and the cast made it. A cast is now refused, before its slot is spent, where the casting player holds less than Observer on the creature or on its compendium; the cast window's foot says "This seat cannot open that creature." A Judge's cast is never refused.

- **A weapon with no damage type of its own shows none.** On the character sheet's Rolls tab, an item sheet's Rolls tab, the follower card's attack rows and the attack rows on a monster's Full sheet, a weapon whose Damage Type is empty (item sheet → Details) used to show a type borrowed from a hidden note or from the weapon table's row for its name, a type its attack roll never dealt. It now shows no type, the same as the roll carries. Pick a Damage Type on the item sheet to set one. The same change makes a weapon's own Damage Type decide which scavenged-equipment condition table it uses, ahead of the weapon table's row (T-0659).

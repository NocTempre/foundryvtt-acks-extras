- **The Beastman family's plate comes from your own book.** With the Monstrous Manual open, Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, with the Beastman family's row ticked (Repair in place), reads the section's full-page picture from your copy and keeps it on the family template in the library (Compendium Packs sidebar tab → ACKS II — Extras → From your books → ACKS Cookbook — Actor → Beastman) as its plate; the template's own picture stays the overview's strip of heads. The plate shows on a faction sheet's Org chart tab and on the root group's sheet, which Generate on the template makes with the plate as its picture. Where your copy's picture cannot be read, no plate is written and a held one stays.

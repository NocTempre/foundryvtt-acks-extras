- A player who owns a member of a party now has the party sheet's saves rail, with its Magical toggle, and a Supplies press (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet). Each roll or ration covers only the members whose characters that player owns, and the tooltips say so. The same player gets Checks, and in the delve or a city the methodical search, which go to the Judge to roll (the quick tools beside the figures). They can also edit the party's description, and a Trusted player can change the party's image, from the sheet tools or a press on the picture. A player who owns every member can deal experience; its card goes to the Judges and to the player who dealt. The encounter throw, the journey's hour of searching, Pause, Tweaks and Ownership stay the Judge's.
- A player who owns a member of a party gets Form up on the party token's HUD (the map → right-click the party token). It puts the party back in a saved marching order through the Judge's client and is refused while a combat holds the party. The picker shows Delete only to someone allowed to change world settings.
- The owner of a character can mark one of its abilities as a skill and set its party-roll fields (Actors sidebar tab → a character → its sheet → an ability → its sheet: the Skill checkbox and the Skill tab), as the Judge can.

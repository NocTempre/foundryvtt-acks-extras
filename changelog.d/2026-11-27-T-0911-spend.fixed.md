- **End day** now spends the food and water it deals (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the Wilderness tab → **End day** at the tab's head): each member's share comes out of their own pack and then the rest of the order's, through the same routine as the **Supplies** press, so a member the press fed that day is not fed again. A ration worth several days is eaten a day at a time and an emptied stack stays at zero; water comes out of each vessel's **Left, days** (the item's sheet → **Details** tab → **Provision**), and an emptied vessel stays in the pack; water found by foraging adds to what is left. A member short of a day whose only food or water has no size keeps that hunger or thirst as it was, and **The weather's toll** card names them, the items and the page; setting the item's **Days per unit** brings them back into the count.

- **A light placed on a scene can now shed daylight.** Scene controls → Lighting Controls → double-click a light on the canvas → its configuration window → Basic Configuration tab → Light Placement → **Daylight radius** (after the bright and dim radii, in the scene's distance unit; blank sheds none). A point inside it reads as daylight to every light reader, such as a typed modifier gated on the light, even at night, and counts as bright light for the party's light and the mapper; Foundry still draws only the two radii it has, and they stay as you set them. A light reading now also names what decided it (the sky, a placed light, a token, the marching column) for rules that ask.

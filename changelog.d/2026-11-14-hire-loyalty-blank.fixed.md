- **A hire's starting loyalty is the figure an import read, or it is blank.** With no figure imported, a henchman's Service tab (Loyalty row), the Roster (the **Roster** chip on an employer's Followers tab), the character sheet's Stats tab Loyalty field and Rolls tab row, a Follower Card and a monster sheet's Statblock tab show no loyalty, each with the page reference as its tooltip; the Loyalty roll from the Service tab, the Roster, the Rolls tab and the actor's header button warns "has no loyalty score to roll with" and names the sheet's Loyalty field instead of rolling. A score typed into that field is kept as the hire's loyalty. A calamity or a level gain records its loyalty change only where `influence.loyaltyChanges` was imported. The public `effectiveLoyaltyFor` returns null for a hire with no score.

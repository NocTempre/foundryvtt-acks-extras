- **A spell attack whose damage row states no die asks for one when it is cast.** Casting from a character sheet's Magic tab opens a Damage formula window that accepts dice or a flat figure; closing it cancels that attack. A player who owns the spell's copy sees *Save on this character's copy of the spell*, and the Judge is whispered a card with one press that saves the die on every copy that has none. A Judge's own window offers *Save on every copy of this spell*. A copy that already has a die keeps it, and an unlinked token's own copy is not changed. A row that recurs on later rounds asks at the cast too, and its later turns roll the die typed. Before this, such a spell made an attack throw and no damage roll.

- **A fight on another scene no longer holds a caster.** A caster's cast from a character sheet's Magic tab → Cast was declared whenever any begun fight listed in the Combat Tracker sidebar tab held the caster, even one left on another scene, so an out-of-combat cast spent a slot and placed nothing; a begun fight now holds a caster only where its scene is the one the caster's token stands on, or where it has no scene.

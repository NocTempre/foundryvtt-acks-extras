- **The Walls control's two marking tools mark the preset in hand.** Scene controls → Walls → *Mark the selected walls as roads* marked an earthen road and *Mark the selected walls as water* a river, whichever preset was armed. They now mark what the wall tool is set to draw: with *Paved road* or *Alley* armed from the Battlemap group, the surface and street armed (an alley stays an alley), and with *Coastline* armed, a coast. With a trap line or nothing armed, each marks its first kind, as before.

- A player who owns the party actor (under the default ownership, every player who owns a member) can now run the crossing from the party sheet (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing): Begin one, set it up (the water conditions, the penalty pick and its larger figure, the movement, the squeeze's Shape and length, the Judge's modifier), press Throw all and press End. Begin, End and Throw all run on the Judge's client and are announced to the table with the player's name; Throw all throws only for the characters that player owns; End is refused while a member is stuck or drowning, naming them, where the Judge is only warned.
- The Judge's modifier on the crossing now names who set it: hover the field, or the modifier in a target's tooltip, for "Set by" and the user's name, or "Set by the Judge" (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing).
- A player who owns a character on a crossing can now press that character's Haul to the surface, landing save Roll and failed-landing footing on the row (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing). The landing save rolls on the Judge's client with no dialog on either screen, so a situational bonus is not offered, and a player is blocked on a landing a Judge would only be warned of.
- A player who owns a member of the party can now press Search here in the Turn plan (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → Turn plan), which opens the scene search as the Judge's press does and tells the table, where it only announced that the party wanted to search. With no place under the party the player is told the search did not open.
- The Trapbreaking window's Judge's modifier field is now drawn for a player too, with the tooltip "Typed on this seat", and what they type rides their throw (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Dungeon tab → Trapbreaking). Mark found and Reset stay the Judge's.
- The trap row on a wall's configuration sheet now follows whether Foundry lets the user write the trap onto that wall, not whether the user is a Judge (Scene controls → Walls → double-click a wall → its configuration sheet).

- **An undetected magic arm ranks at its plain base plus masterwork.** A staged
  treasure pot (Scene controls → Token controls → New treasure pot) can carry an
  arms column; with one set, each arm Deal rolls (a "sword +1", say) is typed on
  it, its non-magical base found among your imported weapons and armour, and the
  pot ranks the card at that base's cost plus the masterwork cost, so a +1 sword
  reads as a fine one and no longer as worth nothing. A ring, a cloak or
  ammunition is not an arm and ranks as before. Nothing in the pot window sets
  the column yet (it is set through the API, `setDealOption`, until its control
  ships); re-import your Treasure Tome's item tables so their rows carry the
  link.

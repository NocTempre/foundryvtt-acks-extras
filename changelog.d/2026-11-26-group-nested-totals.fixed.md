- **A group that holds other groups now counts them.** The figure at the head of a group's sheet (Actors sidebar tab → the group → its sheet) was its own stacks only, so a warband holding two gangs read smaller than its roster and its organisation chart; it now totals the group and every group inside it, with the pristine and fallen counts summed the same way, and its tooltip lists the parts, each stack and then each group inside with its bodies. The name and count on the group's token, as the Judge reads it on the map and as a player reads it once the encounter is revealed in full, add the groups inside too, merging stacks of one creature into one count, and a change to a group inside updates the token of the group it stands inside. The **Adjust hit points** tool (scene controls → Tokens layer → Adjust hit points, or the Adjust Hit Points (GM) macro) now reaches the bodies on the map of the groups inside a group it is given, not only the group's own.

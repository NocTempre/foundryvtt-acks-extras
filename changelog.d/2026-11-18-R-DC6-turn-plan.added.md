The turn plan can resolve a round-length choice on a Round press: Actors sidebar → a party's sheet → Dungeon tab → Round. An activity registered with `length: "round"` resolves at once, ahead of the next Turn or Rest, and spends no time beyond the round the press already marks off; every other choice still resolves on Turn and Rest. No choice the formation itself offers is round-length yet, so the press changes nothing until another feature registers one.

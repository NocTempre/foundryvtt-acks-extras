- **Defend is one tile, and the shield is read when the roll is made.** On the token HUD (Token HUD → Status Effects) and in the Token Status Palette window (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Token status palette → Arrange the palette`), "Defending with a Shield" is gone and **Defending** stands alone under States. Its thrown, missile and Blast terms apply when the one defending holds an equipped shield: a character with a shield item equipped gets them, a character without one gets the melee term only, and a Defend given to an ally reads the giver's shield. A monster is not known to hold a shield until a shield item is equipped on it (the monster sheet's Inventory tab, then the item's Equip control): with none, the melee term applies and a notice says the shield terms were not applied and why, so add them as a situational modifier if the monster has a shield. The monster sheet's Inventory hint now says a Defend reads an equipped shield.

- **Creature shelves are no longer loaded whole at startup: summon names, places in a compendium and Restore book tokens read the creature index instead.** A summoning spell's creature is named from the compendium's index on the Mechanics tab of the spell's sheet (Items tab of the sidebar, then the spell) and in the effect row window opened from it, which redraws once if the index was still being read when it opened. A place held in a compendium (an Actor compendium in the Compendium Packs tab of the sidebar, then the place) lists its sub-places and the providers in its pack once the pack's index is read, which opening the sheet starts. Restore book tokens, on the Tools tab of Manage Imports (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports), is offered when an imported monster carries the mark, found from the index and not by loading the monsters. A creature shelf is loaded whole only by an action that needs it.

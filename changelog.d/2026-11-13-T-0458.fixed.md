Rebuild or Repair on a book now writes the book's own rows too, not only its shelf items (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Rebuild, or Repair everything on the Import tab): the bestiary's creatures, templates and families, the rules book's vehicles and journal page, and the Judge's book's roll table are written over in place, and what the world lacks of them is imported. A beastman template imported before 11.14.0 comes back in the current shape under the same id, and a copy of it in the sidebar is left alone. The confirm names the book's own rows. A bestiary repair refills every creature of the book it holds, so it takes minutes.

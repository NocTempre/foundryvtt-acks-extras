- **Two players drawing or splitting from the same stack of gear at the same moment no longer make an extra copy of it.** When two seats equipped (or used Divide on) one row of several swords, torches or the like in the same instant, each made its own new row and each reduced the stack, so a unit was duplicated. Now one seat's divide stands and the other writes nothing, and the second can press again: Actors sidebar tab → a character's sheet → Equipment tab, on a row holding more than one.

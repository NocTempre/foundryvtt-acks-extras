- **A generated beastman's gated troop and trait figures now apply, each under its own condition.** Generate from the Beastman template (Compendium Packs sidebar tab → ACKS II — Extras → From your books → ACKS Cookbook — Actor → the Beastman template, then Generate) and a fighting creature carries one ability per special troop and per tribal trait that holds a gated figure, named for the row with its page. The attack throw and the multiplied damage dice of a troop that strikes vulnerable targets show on the attack card only against a target in the condition the page names, at melee or the band it names; a trait's attack and damage show only while its side outnumbers the other at the printed ratio, in the printed round, or against a target in the printed condition; its morale penalty is pre-ticked on the influence morale page only while the opposing side is the larger, and offered unticked where that is not known. A troop or trait that waives the Armor Class loss of charging or retreating no longer loses it, and its notes carry two lines for what stays by hand. A condition word is matched to the conditions catalogue when the creature is generated; a word that matches none writes nothing, and `extras.organisation.unresolved` on the creature lists it. The morale page now honours a gate on any ability's morale row, which fixes an ability's gated morale part being offered pre-ticked on every roll.

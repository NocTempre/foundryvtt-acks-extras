- **A member's shining magic light no longer reads as no light on the dungeon turn chat card or in the party sheet's header.** With no flame lit and only a magic light shining on a party member, the dungeon turn card still printed its no-light line and the light cell in the party sheet's header still read "no light burning". Both now count that light; a shuttered or stowed magic light still does not, and the header cell then names the light's state ("Shining") where a flame would show its turns left.

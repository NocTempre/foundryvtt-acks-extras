- **Actors sidebar → a group's sheet → a stack's row: a stack that generated some of its bodies with an item reads "n carry <item>", and the row carries a Raise, Lower or Use button for it.** With no carrier out, the button sends one body out where Deploy would put it, carrying the item, and then acts through it; with a carrier already on the viewed scene it acts through that body. The button is drawn only for a seat that may place tokens and would own the body that acts. **Deploy** now sends the bodies that carry an item out carrying it, up to the share the stack named, and a body already sent out with it is never given a second copy.

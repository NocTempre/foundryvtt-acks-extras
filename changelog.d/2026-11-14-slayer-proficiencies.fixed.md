- **The three slayer proficiencies carry the bonus their page prints at each level step, and the kinds it is against.** Imported with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Import tab → **Import everything**, two of them take their attack-throw bonus from the page's ladder at each level step, where each carried the first step's figure at every level, and the third carries its attack-throw and saving-throw bonuses; each names its kinds. The rows are listed with the modifiers an ability carries on a character sheet's Stats tab, and an ability's modifier row can be added as a quick effect, which applies to every roll while it is on. Nothing yet checks the target's kind, so none is applied against a target by itself.

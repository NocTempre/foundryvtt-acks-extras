- **A row's bodies can be rolled again from the dice they were rolled from.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a faction, its Org chart tab, press a card's pen, and look beside **Bodies** in the form: where the row's bodies came from dice, a dice button is drawn there, for a warband, a gang or a part of a village alike. Press it to throw those dice again; the total becomes the row's bodies and the card stops reading "set by hand". The dice button's tooltip names the dice and the page they were read from, and afterwards the Bodies field and the card's count name the dice thrown and the total. Bodies already on a map stay as they are, and the Judges are told so. A row that was never rolled, or one whose bodies are a fixed figure, shows no button; you need to own the row's group and the faction.

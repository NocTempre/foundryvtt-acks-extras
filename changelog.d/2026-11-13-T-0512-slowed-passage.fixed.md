- **A vessel's slowed passage names its speed and damage shares from the imported voyage tables.** The Hazard throw
  dialog on a sea vehicle's sheet (Travel tab, Seamanship), its whispered card and the damage line on a failed throw
  show the speed share and the damage share read from RR ch. 7, and say "slowed passage" or "the reduction is not
  imported" where they were not imported. The voyages import reads both into the `hazardThrow` table beside the
  bonus, and a Judge edits them in the Ruledata Browser
  (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`). The Holed through line on the vessel's
  Travel tab names the condition and the imported sinking roll with its page.

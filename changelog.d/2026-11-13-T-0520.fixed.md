- **A captured monster offered the Irrefusable Offer through the influence roller is now hired.** The offer
  opens from `acksExtras.henchmen.recruitMonster(monster, employer, { captured: true })`, which a macro calls
  and which opens the influence roller's Irrefusable Offer page. The roll named a result and hired nobody,
  and its top result was never read. The monster now enters the employer's service on the result the roll
  names, at the starting loyalty read from your imported table, as the fallback throw dialog already did.
  With the table not imported the roll names no result, nothing is hired, and the Judge is told.

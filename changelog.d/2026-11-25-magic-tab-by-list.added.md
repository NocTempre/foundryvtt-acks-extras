- **A caster's spells are grouped by the list they are cast from.** On the Magic tab (Actors sidebar tab → the character's sheet → Magic tab), a character whose class casts from one or more lists reads a heading for each list, with that list's own slots used and maximum at every level and the spells that stand on it at the level that list gives them. A spell on two of the class's lists appears under both, and each row names the other lists it is on. Spells on no list the class casts from follow under "Other spells". The slot pips sit beside each level's Slots figure, for every user who owns the character. A character with no class, or a class that casts from no list, keeps the tab by level.

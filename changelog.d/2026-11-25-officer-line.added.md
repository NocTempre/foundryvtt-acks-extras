- **A chart card's officer line can make a leader, delete one and show one that was deleted.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a faction, its Org chart tab, and press a card's pen: the officer line offers Open, Clear, **Delete actor…** (asks first, deletes the actor and clears the link) and, where the card has no officer or its officer was deleted, **+ Make the champion** (or the sub-chieftain, or the chieftain), which makes a new leader actor from the chart's template and sets it as the card's officer. A leader deleted from the Actors tab shows as deleted on the card, with Make and Clear beside it, and the open sheet redraws at once; the faction's leader line reads the same way. Each control is shown only to a seat Foundry's own permissions allow, and a card rolled before this release offers no Make.

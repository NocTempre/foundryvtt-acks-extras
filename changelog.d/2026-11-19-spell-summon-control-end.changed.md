- **Chat → the card a summoning spell posts when it ends (a concentration's end card, or the card of a spell that ran out its time):** what becomes of the creatures follows the spell. Each built summoning spell now states whether its creatures stay, go or turn hostile when its control ends. A creature that goes is removed by the module when a timed spell runs out, with no card; one that stays or turns hostile keeps the card, where only a Judge may Dismiss it, and a hostile one loses its owners and turns hostile.

- **Using a beastman's fuse item (the hotbar, or Actors tab → the body's sheet → the item's use control) now lights it, and the Chat tab shows what happened (MM p. 55).** The card says whether the fuse is lit, unlit or fumbled; a lit fuse shows the rounds it has left in a fight, or burns on the world clock outside one. The first card names the throw with its parts, and the owner of the bearer can press **Move the spot** (one click on the board) or **Set it off now** while it burns. When it goes off the same card becomes a table of every body in the area: each Save is pressed by the body's owner, the damage is rolled once and shown per body with the imported share a passed save takes off, **Apply** and **Apply all** write the hit points for the bodies a seat owns, and **Undo** gives them back. A vessel in the area has **Hand on**, which lowers its structural hit points; buildings are a line for the Judge. Figures that were not imported stay blank with the page reference. The Light press and the "Lit" tag on a sheet row come with the sheet-row work.

A spell row that repeats now prompts at its moment, in the Chat sidebar, at the turn it belongs to: the caster's turn, or the start or end of the turn of the creature it affects. The prompt is a card of its own with a Save for the creature's owner (a pass ends that effect, or the whole spell where the row says so, and the spell's end card says why), a Roll for the caster's owner, and for a row the caster aims each time a Roll at your one targeted token, checked against the spell's range. A Player is blocked and a Judge warned; nothing is rolled until someone presses. Outside a fight, the cast card's **Next round** posts the round's prompts. A spell row that sets a creature or object alight now writes a record on its target, lasting the rounds the row says, and a condition lasting one round no longer reads "1 rounds".

- **Joining a group to a faction can nest it under a row of the faction's chart.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a group, its Faction line and press Join a faction…: after the faction, an Under select lists At the top and then the faction's rows, a nested row indented under its parent. Pick a row and the group's top row stands beneath it, with the group's own rows; At the top keeps the group's top row at the top. Changing the faction refills the list from its rows, and a row a Judge has hidden is not listed to a player.

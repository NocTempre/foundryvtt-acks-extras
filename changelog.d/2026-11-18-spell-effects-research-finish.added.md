- **A research project's Finish throws and writes its result.** In the Actors
  tab, open a location and its sheet's Research tab: on a project whose research
  cost is credited, Finish rolls a d20 against the Judge's target or the ladder's
  rung for the researcher's level, with the facility bonus and any substitute
  penalty, and an imported automatic-failure band fails whatever the total. A
  success delivers a known item to the researcher, adds a known spell to their
  spells, marks an item to identify as fully identified, or opens a blank item for
  the Judge where the subject is a new one; a spell's or item's formula is
  recorded on the researcher and read by their next project for it. Every outcome
  spends the components bucket and posts one roll card. Finish stays disabled,
  with its reason beside it, until the cost is credited and every figure the throw needs is
  imported (its target, each facility's bonus, and the substitutes' penalty while
  a substitute is in the bucket), and for a ritual. A Player's own Finish is relayed to the Judge's client and refused
  for components or an item they do not own. The pace pills and the posted-days list
  read "Dedicated", "Ancillary" and "Both".

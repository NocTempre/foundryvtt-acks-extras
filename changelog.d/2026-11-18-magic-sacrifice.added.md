- **Blood sacrifice has a window.** Open it from the Sacrifice… button beside the power
  tags on a character's casting strip (character sheet, under the class field): a Judge
  sees the button on every caster, a Player on a divine caster who is chaotic or one a
  Judge marked as holding the class power. The window holds a bucket of victims dropped
  from the sidebar or the canvas, with the Judge's checks for each inside its row
  (willing or helpless, the traits your imported table lists, young, a value box) and for
  the sacrifice (the site, the class power), and the yield with where each figure came
  from. The Judge's checks are saved on the caster while the window is open and every
  window for that caster shows them live: a Player sees them as text, and the Player's
  Sacrifice frees itself once nothing waits on the Judge. Sacrifice makes the research
  throw, marks each victim with the dead status (never deleting it), and records divine
  power on the character's ledger, or arcane power for the holder of the class power. A
  limit blocks a Player and warns a Judge; a figure your import has not read holds the act.
  The yield figures come from your own copy through the importer.

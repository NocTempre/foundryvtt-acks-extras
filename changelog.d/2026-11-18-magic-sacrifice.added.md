- **Blood sacrifice has a window.** Opened for a character with
  `acksExtras.magic.sacrifice.open(actor)` (no sheet control opens it yet), it holds a
  bucket of victims dropped from the sidebar or the canvas, the Judge's checks for each
  (willing or helpless, the traits your imported table lists, the value, young) and for the
  sacrifice (the site, the class power), and the yield with where each figure came from.
  Sacrifice makes the research throw, marks each victim with the dead status (never
  deleting it), and records divine power on the character's ledger, or arcane power for the
  holder of the class power. A limit blocks a Player and warns a Judge; a figure your import
  has not read holds the act. The yield figures come from your own copy through the importer.

- **An item that is raised and lowered or used up shows its state and its press on its row.** A toggle reads Lowered or Raised with its reach and, raised, how many bodies are in range, and carries Raise, or Card and Lower; an item spent by its use carries Use, and once it has none the press is off and says so; an item that is always on reads so with nothing to press, and an always-on reach of the adjacent square reads whether its ring stands. A press is offered to whoever owns the item, and goes through the same use the hotbar macro does. Where to look: an actor sheet → the Equipment tab's gear rows (a character), and the Inventory tab (a monster).

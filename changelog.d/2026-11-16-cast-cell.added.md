- **Character sheet → right rail (casters):** a character that holds a spell, or has a summon standing, gets a second column beside the right rail. Its top cell opens the cast menu — each spell to cast, and a summoning spell's Cast and Dismiss, Summon… and Release, which moved here from the party cell, so a character marching in a formation on the scene can still manage its summons — and the five cells below track the character's active casts, newest first, each filled to the time it has left and opening the Effects tab.
- **Character sheet → right rail → cast menu:** *Bind*, which marked the selected tokens as a character's summons by hand, is retired; summons come from casting a summoning spell, and *Release* still lets go of a creature bound before.

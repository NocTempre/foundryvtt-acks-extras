- **A gang sent out again after it was gathered back to one row no longer leaves a dead group that shifts the group marks in the Combat Tracker** (Combat Tracker sidebar tab, the `G` mark beside a group's rows). The group control's write now drops the token ids that are no longer in the combat, and any group left with fewer than two bodies, so the re-sent gang is the only group stored. The dead group from the gather itself stands until the next group is made on that combat; it holds no body in the fight.

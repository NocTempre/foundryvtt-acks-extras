- **A monster's Armor Class and saving-throw lines read a condition that sets, multiplies or bounds the figure from the figure the monster stores.** A monster whose Armor Class modifier or saving-throw modifier is not zero, under a condition effect that overrides, multiplies or caps that modifier, showed a step off by the stored figure in the attack thrown against it and in the saving-throw notice; both now start from the stored value, as the ACKS II system's own breakdown does. A condition that only adds a figure reads as before.

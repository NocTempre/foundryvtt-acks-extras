Hire as Group (location sheet → Mercenaries tab) warns that a unit is larger than its officer can personally lead (RR p. 169), reading the ladder from your own book: the officer's level picks the rung, infantry and cavalry are each held to their own count of it, and the warning names the unit's two counts and the rung's. Where the table is not imported the hire shows no warning. The lib API is version 45: a group exposes `troopCounts` and `commandStatus(rungs)`.

- **Party token moves on a scene with trap walls and trap regions:** traps now stack. A move that crosses several trap walls and ends in several overlapping trap regions (or one region carrying two traps) resolves each armed trap in turn, each on its own card, after one search sweep. A trap wall that halts the party (spotted, or sprung) stops the walk at its line, so nothing beyond it is reached; trap regions halt nothing, so every region under the party resolves.

- **Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing:** a Judge begins a crossing of a climb, a rappel, a traverse or a jump and sets its length, depth, rope and modifier (or a jump's gap and run-up), and each member's row shows their own target, whether the table lets them try, the stretches covered and where they stand. A press throws one stretch for one member, *Throw all* throws one for everyone a row allows, and the Judges' card says when a throw went over a refusal, prints the imported outcome words, and reports a fall's distance and dice without touching hit points. A Judge is warned where a player is blocked, and a figure nobody imported is blank with its page reference. A player sees the section while a crossing is open and presses *Throw* on a member they own: the Judge's client checks the press again, declares it in chat for everyone, and posts the card to the Judges, and a press it refuses (not their member, a stale crossing, a member the table blocks) is whispered back to that player with the reason.

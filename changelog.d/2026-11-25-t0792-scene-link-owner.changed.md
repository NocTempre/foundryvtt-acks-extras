- **Linking a scene or a Region to a place follows ownership, not the seat's role.** Dropping a scene onto a place's sheet (open the place from the Actors sidebar tab), the **Place** row in a scene's configuration (Scenes sidebar tab, right-click the scene, Configure, Basics tab), **Unlink map** on the place's GM Settings tab, and the `linkScene`, `unlinkScene`, `linkRegion` and `unlinkRegion` functions each need ownership of every document the link writes: the scene or Region, the place, the place it was linked to before and the scene or Region the place held before. A seat that lacks one gets the warning "You need ownership of the scene and of each place this link changes.", nothing is written, and the function answers `false`; a Judge passes as before. A scene that is one of several of its place writes only its own flag, so it needs the scene alone. The Place row is drawn for a scene's owner and lists the places the seat owns and the one already linked; its new-place button stays the Judge's, and **Unlink map** is drawn only where the seat owns the scene the place names.

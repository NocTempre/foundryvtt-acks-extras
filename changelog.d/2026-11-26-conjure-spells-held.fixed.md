- **Every conjure spell is held by concentration and its creature turns on its caster when control is lost, not only Conjure Cacodemon Spawn.** Conjure Dybbuk, Fiend, Genie, Hellion, Imp, Incubus and the Petty, Major and Supreme Elemental imported with no summoning row, so a cast of one was not held by concentration, ended with the clock and left its creature standing as the caster's. They now import as conjurations: the cast is held, the caster can dismiss the creature at will, and a lapse in concentration, or a duration that runs out with the creature not dismissed, leaves it hostile and out of the caster's hands. Spells already in your world need a re-import to gain the row: Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, choosing the conjure spells. A Genie, which prints no Hit Dice, imports with that figure blank.

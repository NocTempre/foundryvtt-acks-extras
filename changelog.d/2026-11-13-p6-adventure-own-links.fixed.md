- **A book's Adventure links to what it carries.** Imported from the Compendium Packs tab, a book's Adventure rewrites the documents its roll-table results name, the links on its journal pages and the links in its items' descriptions to the world copies it brings, as it does for actors and maps. An Adventure imported a second time, so that its maps take other ids, has its map-to-map links joined to its own regions.

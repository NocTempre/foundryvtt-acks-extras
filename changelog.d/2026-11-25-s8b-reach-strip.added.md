- **A body in the reach of an item now sees it on its own sheet.** Actors sidebar → a character or monster sheet shows an "In reach of" strip above the tabs when something reaches its token: one row a reach with the item, its state (Save owed with a Save button, Always on, or Immune with the turns left), the source token's name, the distance between the two tokens' nearest edges and the reach with its page. A raised item's card (Chat tab) measures distance the same way, gives a counted bearer a Count out beside Count in, and a once-only item's card lists the bodies it could not settle for the item's owners to count in; a morale roll's working (Influence → the morale page) names the item whose reach changed it, with its page.

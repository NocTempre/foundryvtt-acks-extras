- **Character sheet → cast column → Cast, for a character marching in a party:** the creatures a summoning spell places join the summoner's party as members while the party token is on their scene — at the cast, when the summoner rejoins, and when a deployed summoner is recalled — march in its order, deploy and come back with it, and leave with the summoner. The cast menu's *Dismiss*, the Magic tab's standing count and *Dismiss*, and the card a cast's end posts reach them inside the party token as well as on the map. A summoned creature in a party is no person: it is never a casualty or carried, and at 0 hit points it is removed as a dismissal removes it. The world copy a summon is made from, and a summoned creature whose summoner is not in that party, are refused when dropped on the **Party sheet**.

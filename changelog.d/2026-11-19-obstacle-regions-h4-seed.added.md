- **Chat sidebar → the Judges' hazard card → Begin:** a squeeze obstacle whose place stores a shape opens its crossing in that shape, and a swim obstacle whose place stores a water penalty (none, one of the imported steps, or a larger figure) opens with it. No window sets these yet; a module or macro writes them through the place-things API. A shape or penalty the place does not store, or a step the world no longer imports, leaves the crossing's own default, and movement and the Judge's modifier are still set at the table.

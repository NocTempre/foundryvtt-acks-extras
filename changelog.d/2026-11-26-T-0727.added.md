- **A special maneuver that calls a saving throw now asks it.** When a Knock Down, Force Back, Wrestle, Disarm, Overrun or Sunder rolled from a weapon's sheet (Rolls tab → Special Manoeuvres, with the maneuver overlay on) hits a targeted token, the chat log posts a card titled "<maneuver>: <attacker> against <target>" with the target's Paralysis save and its modifier (Combat Trickery, a hooked weapon, a two-handed grip on a disarm), each named in the figure's tooltip with the page. The target's owners press Save on the card, and the verdict is recorded there; what a failed save does (prone, disarmed, forced back) is applied by hand, and the card changes neither actor. Where the save's modifier cannot be read, the card names no save and the Judge picks Passed or Failed: a size-adjusted maneuver between creatures not known to be one size, a sunder, a disarm whose target is not a character holding one weapon, or a figure the import did not read. A miss, and Incapacitate, post no card.

- **Imported rules tables → Magic items and arms:** the Judges Journal's magic-item generation tables (the random item type, the classic category lists, the heroic rarity lists, the scroll and treasure-map sub-tables) and its arms-and-armour tables are read from your own Journal and appear in the Ruledata Browser (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`) as RollTables you can roll and edit; the treasure tables now also carry the gem and jewelry dice legend, the coin columns' unit and each special list's lot unit.

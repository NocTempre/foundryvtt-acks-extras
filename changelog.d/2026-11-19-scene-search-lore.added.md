- **The scene screen's picker (party sheet → Dungeon tab → Search here, or the fight-over card's Search the scene, opens the screen; entry card → `+ Assign`):** a layer the Judge keyed to a proficiency (Theology, Loremastery, Naturalism, Magical Engineering or Sensing Power) is thrown by the assigned character's own imported proficiency, secretly, and a character without it gets only the hint that something more might be here. A proficiency that prints no throw (Sensing Power) is made without one. A holder whose imported proficiency has no figure cannot be assigned, and the row's tooltip names the page the proficiency is read from.

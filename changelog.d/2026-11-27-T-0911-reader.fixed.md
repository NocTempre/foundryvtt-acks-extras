- **The party's camp and its Supplies press count only what is food or water, by what an item IS and not by a fragment of its name.** A Ring of Regeneration or a Potion of Brilliant Restoration counted as a day of food each ("-ration"), a Ring of Water Walking, Eelskin armor or a flask of holy water as a day of water each, and Supplies could spend a Ring of Regeneration. Items the importer made from the rations and waterskin entries (and class-kit items cut from them) are read by that identity, a wineskin holds no water, a hand-made item is read only where its name has the word ration, waterskin or canteen, and a part-eaten week's ration now counts the days it still holds, so the Camp forecast and Supplies agree. Seen in Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Wilderness tab → Camp, and on the sheet's Supplies press.

- **Hidden Regions (Scene controls → Regions → the Region's hide toggle):** a Region you have hidden no longer springs its traps for a party standing in it, and no longer counts as an encounter zone or a district zone. Foundry turns every behavior of a hidden Region off, and the module's zone lookups used to ignore that, so a hidden trap zone still fired and a hidden encounter or district zone still applied. Unhide the Region to bring its zones back.

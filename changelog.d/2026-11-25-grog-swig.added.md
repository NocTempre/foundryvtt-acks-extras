- **A beastman's grog (Actors tab → the body's sheet → the item's use control, or the hotbar) can now be drunk, and the Chat tab shows what it did (MM p. 56).** A beastman heals by the imported dice. A human or demi-human gets a save card: the drinker's owner presses **Save**, a pass heals by the imported success dice, a failure gives the imported condition until the end of the drinker's next turn, and **Undo** takes either back. Where the module cannot tell the drinker's kind, the drinker's owner chooses **Heal** or **Ask the save**. The grog's swigs are rolled from the imported dice when it is handed to another character or creature, a counted grog with none left refuses, and a beastman made defeated or dead loses its grog with a line to the Judge. Figures that were not imported stay blank with the page reference.

- **Character sheet → Magic tab → a summoning spell's Cast → the cast dialog's Summon band:** a summoning spell now casts through the same dialog as any other spell, in place of the old creature prompt. The band offers the spell's creature (by its imported name) and a count, with the most the spell allows at the caster's level beside it and its source on hover, and says whether the creatures are placed within the spell's range of the caster's token or called from where they stand. A count over the most is a block for a Player and a warning for a Judge, and a Player is refused before anything is spent when no Judge is connected. You click where each creature stands before the slot is spent, so closing the dialog or cancelling a click costs the cast nothing; in a begun fight the summons is declared and its clicks wait for the caster's turn. A click beyond the range is refused to a Player and warned to a Judge.

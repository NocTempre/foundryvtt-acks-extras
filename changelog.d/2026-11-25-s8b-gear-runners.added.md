- **Beastman gear that acts at a distance runs.** A creature generated with an optional equipment piece that encourages subordinates, calls a save of whoever comes near, or gives its own unit a bonus now carries that item (Actors tab → Actor → the creature's sheet → Items). Using the item (its hotbar macro, or `item.use()`; a button on the sheets follows) runs it: the encouraging piece writes its damage part on each subordinate in reach, the save piece raises a reach until lowered and writes the condition on a failed save and an immunity window on a pass, and the bonus piece holds one part per body inside its reach and takes it off when the body leaves. Every figure is read live from the Judge's imported tables, so an override in the Ruledata Browser (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser) reaches the next use. Re-import the Monstrous Manual tables after updating: the equipment paragraphs gain a qualifier figure.
- **A morale roll reads a held bonus.** Pressing a creature's Morale button reads the standing part: the Influence morale page lists it as a ticked row, and a roll made with the system's skip-dialog key held adds it to the score and says so in a notice.
- **A Player's passed save is written correctly.** A save pressed from a Player's seat on a raised item now writes its immunity window from the item's imported figure on the GM's side, and a second press of the same row is refused.

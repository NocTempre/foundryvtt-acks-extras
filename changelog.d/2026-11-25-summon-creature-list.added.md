- **A summoning spell lists the creatures it may call.** Open the spell (Items sidebar tab, or Actors sidebar tab → a character's sheet → Magic tab → the spell), then its Mechanics tab, and open a summon row: the row window shows every creature of that summon, each with its own Number box. Drop a creature, a family or a token on the list from the Actors sidebar tab, a compendium or the map, or paste its uuid and press Add; the ✕ on a line removes it. A creature your seat cannot open shows as "A creature this seat cannot open".

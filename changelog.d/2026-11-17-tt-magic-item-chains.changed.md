- **Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports, the Rebuild tab, Shelf, book or tables → Rules tables** (or the **Reimport One Shelf (GM)** macro in `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`): with the Treasure Tome open, the import now reads the Tome's own magic-item generation chain for the random type, the potion, ring, scroll, implement and miscellaneous item lists, the common rarity list and their subtables, and the scroll sub-tables of the scroll entries (spell levels, languages, treasure maps). A classic magic-item roll on those categories follows the Tome's type, category and subtable. The sword, miscellaneous weapon and armour category lists and the other four rarity lists stay the Judges Journal's until the Tome's subtables for them are read.

- **Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports, the Rebuild tab, Shelf, book or tables → Rules tables** (or the **Reimport One Shelf (GM)** macro in `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`): with the Treasure Tome open, the import now reads the Tome's whole magic-item generation chain, classic and heroic: the random type, all eight category lists, all five rarity lists and every subtable (potions, rings, scrolls, implements, miscellaneous items, swords, miscellaneous weapons and armour), and the scroll sub-tables of the scroll entries (spell levels, languages, treasure maps). A classic or heroic magic-item roll follows the Tome's type, category or rarity, and subtable. Without the Tome the Judges Journal's lists stand.

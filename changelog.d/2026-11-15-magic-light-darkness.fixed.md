- **Party sheet (Actors sidebar tab → the party actor):** a member's magic light now counts as the party's light. A party in a dark scene lit only by a magic light no longer moves at the blinded pace, no longer shows the Darkness and "no light" warnings, and its wandering-monster card reads lit. Before this, only a burning torch, lantern or candle counted. A magic light with no reach typed in its effect still sheds nothing.

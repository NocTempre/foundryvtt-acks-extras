- **Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser → `formation`:** the Rules tables import reads the Spelunking grid's rates of progress (RR p. 268), a share of the member's combat or running speed for each obstacle and the one failed throw that still moves a climber, each editable in the Ruledata Browser and blank until imported. Nothing on the crossing uses them yet.

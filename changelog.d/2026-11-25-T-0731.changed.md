- **Influence reads a creature's kind from its typing, as every other kind-scoped rule does.** The Influence window's race relations, hatred notes and "versus" effects (Actors sidebar tab → a character → its sheet → the rail's Influence entry → Roll Influence) took a monster's kind from words in its name and counted any character whose class it could not place as human. A monster now counts as what its Full Monster sheet types and subtype say (Actors sidebar tab → a monster → its sheet → the type and subtype fields), and a character as the race its class is bound to in the library; a character whose class is bound to no race counts as no race, so a campaign relation row for humans does not reach it. Type a monster on its sheet to have its kind read.

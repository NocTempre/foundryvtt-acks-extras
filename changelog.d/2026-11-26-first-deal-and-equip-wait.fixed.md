- **The first Deal and the first Draw after loading no longer wait for the whole imported library.** A treasure pot's Deal button (a staged pot's window) and a weapon row's Draw control (character sheet → Equipment tab) held for as long as it took to load every imported shelf, many seconds in a large library, and a hoard with no magic in it waited just the same. The Draw now reads only the weapon and masterwork documents it needs, a Deal of a hoard with no magic reads no library at all, and a Deal that rolls an arm reads only the weapons, armour and masterwork documents. A weapon edited after the page had sat idle also keeps every weapon row: it used to republish its rows from the one document still held.

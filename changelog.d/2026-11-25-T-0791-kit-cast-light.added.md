- **Cast a light spell from the party sheet.** The Kit tab (Actors sidebar tab → the party's sheet → Kit tab → Light Sources) has a menu of the light spells your members carry, one for each list a caster holds it on, with its caster; **Cast** opens that caster's own cast window. The line under the menu reads the list's level and slots in the character sheet Magic tab's words and tooltips. A player sees only the casters they own; where the list refuses the cast, a player's Cast is off with the Magic tab's reason beneath it, and the Judge reads the reason as a warning.

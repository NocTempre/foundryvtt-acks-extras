- **Two macros and the checks that cleaned up after earlier versions are gone.** The ACKS Extras Macros compendium no longer holds Clean Up After the Merge (GM) or Merge Fog Copies on This Scene (GM), and the formation api no longer has `mergeFogCopies`. The repair tool (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Repair this world) no longer lists old rider and mount links, what the separate acks-* modules left behind, a market purchase that was never unpacked, a party member listed in two parties or a stocked hex point with no id. A world still holding any of these keeps it as it is.

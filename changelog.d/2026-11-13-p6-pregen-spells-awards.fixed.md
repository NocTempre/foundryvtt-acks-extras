- **A character imported from a book holds its spellbook's spells and its class's fixed awards.** A pregenerated character or a person the book prints in full, imported from the Manage Imports window, fills its repertoire from a spellbook its equipment lists with its contents, each spell at its level, and lists any spell the world does not hold as missing. It also holds its class's Adventuring and fixed awards through its level, without doubling a power its stat line names; choice awards stay open. A spell the conversion table renames to two alternatives takes the one the world holds.

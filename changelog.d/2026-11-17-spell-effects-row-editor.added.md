- **Spell item sheet → Overview and Mechanics tabs (open a spell from the Items sidebar):** the Overview now edits a spell's Hit Dice cap (Target) and the Hit Dice below which no save is made (Save), each a flat or per-level figure; hover either for the figure held and the page reference, and a blank one shows the page reference alone. The Mechanics tab marks each effect row as written by the book import or set by hand, shows the option a row has within its choice group, and lists a row's blank fields beside the page reference. Each row's own editor (the pen button on a row) gains the cast fields a spell needs: the choice group and the option within it, a ceiling on a row's count, how many rounds a row lasts, where a repeating row resolves (the caster's turn, or the start or end of its bearer's), the saving throw its bearer makes before a repeat and who a repeat meets, the size at which a target is exempt from that row, and what a modifier adds to. A spell row can now also last until its bearer's next turn ends, repeat each later round, and be an Ignite row. Importing spells reads the new figures from your own copy of the book for ten first-level spells: their dice, counts that grow by level (including a count the page writes as "a" or "an"), rounds, and the size exempt from a row; anything the import cannot read is left blank.

- **Scene controls → Walls → ACKS door actions (the door window):** each door has its own window, so two doors open two windows and pressing the tool on an open door brings its window forward. The basher is the token you have selected: the window names it and follows when you select another, and a token with no actor, or a creature with no Dungeonbashing throw, is refused on the window with Bash it disabled. The Partner list holds only the other members of the basher's party, when that party is on the door's scene. Batter and Bash it are pinned under the scrolling body, so they stay in view on a short display. Every figure and part has a hover tooltip naming where it came from: the character's own throw, each term's count times its imported figure with the page, the most a door holds, and the construction's row with RR p. 266; with nothing imported the figures stay blank, Bash it is disabled and each tooltip is the page reference alone. The window's width and type follow the type size setting, a hint that sat under two fields is said once, and the spike counter and the buttons carry the board's labels (Basher, Partner, Jammed, Spike it, Batter, Bash it). A frame beside the door's fields shows the door glyph from the game-icons-net module when it is active.

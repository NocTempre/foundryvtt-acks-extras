- **A weapon is silver when the system says so, and nowhere else.** A weapon
  marked silvered in the item sheet's Construction tab (the Silver control) is
  now silver to the damage-against-defenses stage, which reads the system's own
  silver field; picking Silvered sets both that field and the plating that
  raises the price, Not silvered clears the field, and Auto leaves it alone.
  A weapon is no longer called silver by its name alone ("Silvered Sword") or by
  a weapon-list row. A weapon plated before this keeps its price and is not
  silver until Silvered is picked again. Ammunition still answers from its
  Silver control, its list row and its name.

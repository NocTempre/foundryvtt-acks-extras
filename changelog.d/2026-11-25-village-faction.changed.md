- **A rolled village is a faction, with its warbands and its other parts as the top rows of its chart.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a monster template, press Generate, then Roll a village: the Roll makes the faction card, writes one top row for each warband (with its gangs inside) and one for each part the roll entry lists that is not a body, each row holding its own group and stack, and sets the village's chieftain as the faction's leader. The group Generate made is deleted by that Roll, and the faction opens on its Org chart tab. A part row has the controls a warband or gang row has except Roll and Make: it draws no Roll, and its officer line appears only where its group names an officer. A lone warband or gang Roll is unchanged. A faction whose leader was deleted or cleared shows **+ Make the chieftain** in the leader line on its Overview tab, and a warband on the faction reads its leader as a sub-chieftain.

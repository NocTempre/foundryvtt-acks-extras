- **Character sheet → casting a spell that has effect rows (the cast dialog and the cast card):** a damage or healing row that prints a number of instances now acts that many times, at your caster level and up to its cap. A row with no attack throw rolls once, with its dice scaled by the count. A row with an attack throw is thrown that many times, each throw aimed at its own target, and the cast dialog's "Throws" split lets you divide them among your targets (even by default; Players cannot cast a split over the count and a Judge is warned). The cast card shows how many throws hit, applies each hit's amount (rounded and checked against the target's defenses on its own) as one entry with one Undo, and the row's tooltip names the count, your caster level, the cap and the page. A count the import left blank makes no roll and no throw and shows the page reference alone. Your own targets are put back after each throw, and a target on a scene you are not viewing waits at Throw with a notice. The dialog now asks for a caster level when a row's count depends on it.

- **A cast is counted against the list it was pressed under, and the row says when it would be refused.** Pressing Cast on a spell's row (Actors sidebar tab → the character's sheet → Magic tab) spends a slot of the list in that row's heading, so a spell on two lists spends the one it was cast from. Where that list's slots at the level are all used, or the spell is on a list and none of them is the class's, a Player's Cast is off and the row says why in a line under it; a Judge reads the same line as a warning and can cast anyway. The cast window refuses and warns the same way for a cast started from anywhere else.

- **A special troop whose proficiency is printed in another word form now receives it.** A beastman generated from its family template (Compendium Packs tab → the library's actors → the family template → Generate) with a special troop or tribal trait whose page names its proficiency in another form of the word than the library proficiency's name, such as the same word with an `-ing` ending, was granted nothing and logged a miss. The word now matches the one library proficiency it differs from by a plain English ending; a word that fits two proficiencies still grants none.

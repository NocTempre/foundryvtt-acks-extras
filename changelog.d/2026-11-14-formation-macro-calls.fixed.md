- **The saved marching orders are callable from a macro as `acksExtras.formation.marchingOrders` (save, apply, get, list, delete, formUp, reconcile), and `acksExtras.formation.getFormation(id)` is published.** A macro reads a formation from `acksExtras.formation.getFormations()` or `acksExtras.formation.getFormation(id)`, then saves, applies or forms up an order on it. The formation guide's "Calling it from a macro" section, under Saved marching orders, lists each call and says which ones write.

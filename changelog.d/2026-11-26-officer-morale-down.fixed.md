- **A group's own officer stops lending morale once the officer falls.** The part each unit above a group brought already left with its officer; the group's own officer's part now does too. The group's morale, as the influence feature reads it as a morale rating, drops when its officer (named on a faction card's Org chart tab or a group's Organisation section) is at zero hit points, marked dead, no longer resolves or is not linked, and returns with the officer.

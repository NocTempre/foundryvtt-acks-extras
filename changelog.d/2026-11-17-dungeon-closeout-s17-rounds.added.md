- **Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing → Rounds spent:** a throw on a climb, a rappel, a crawl or a ledge now puts rounds on the party's clock. Each throw spends its stretch's feet over the rate the Rules tables import gives for the member's pace, a miss that covers no ground spends its imported duration, the party's clock is advanced by the slowest member's whole time rounded up once, and the header line shows the rounds paid with each member's working in its tooltip. Nothing is spent while the party is in a fight or its clock is paused, a jump, a swim, a squeeze and the climb up spend none, and the line is blank until the rates are imported.

- **An organisation Roll no longer makes a creature's dependants or casters as its warriors when its family template has no cell for them.** A recipe part naming a role (such as a whelp or a shaman) that the creature's imported Role cells do not hold was minted under the Role axis's fallback, so the stack held full warriors under the part's name. Such a part is now a labelled stack under its own name, as a captive is, in the organisation chart's Roll (tool row, beside Roll at random).

- **An attack on a monster now says which of its condition steps its abilities leave out of its Armor Class.** When an ability of a monster waives the Armor Class step of a condition it is under, the attack's throw left that step out and nothing said so. Now the attack's chat card shows the target's Armor Class line (the footer of the card) with the steps counted, then "not counted:" and each step left out, naming the ability that waives it and its page, and no figure for it; a viewer who cannot see the target's Armor Class reads the same names without the counted figures. A monster with nothing waived shows no line, as before.

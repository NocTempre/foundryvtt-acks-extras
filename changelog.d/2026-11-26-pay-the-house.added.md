- **A place's sheet can now be paid coin directly, with no sale.** Actors sidebar tab → a place → its sheet → Storage tab → **Pay the house** (beside **Leave something here**): pick one of your characters and the coins to pay, each with a count (blank pays the whole stack), and exactly those coins leave the character and become the place's own coin, shown as the **House** figure on the same tab. No price is charged and no change is made. A character out of reach of the place is refused with the reason; a Judge gets the reason as a warning and the payment goes through. One whispered chat line tells the Judges and the character's owners who paid what. The House figure's tooltip now names its coins and counts, as does each owner's figure on that tab.

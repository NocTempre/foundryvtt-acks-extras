- **Roll a Magic Item:** a GM-only macro that rolls a magic item down the imported Judges Journal and Treasure Tome tables and whispers the result to the GM (`Compendium Packs tab → ACKS Extras Macros → Roll a Magic Item`). The window asks for a Style (Classic, Heroic or Gritty), a Category (Roll the type, or one of the imported classic lists) and a Rarity (the imported heroic lists); the card shows a row per list rolled, with its roll and the row's text, then the item. Each roll's tooltip gives its die, the band it landed in and the page the list was read from, or "Set by hand" where you overrode the list. With the tables not imported the macro says so and rolls nothing. Scripts and sheets can roll the same item without the window through `acksExtras.magicItems.rollAndPost`.

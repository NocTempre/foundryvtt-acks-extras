- **A summoning spell names its creature to a player who can open it, even where the world holds a copy they cannot see.** On the Mechanics tab of the spell's sheet (Actors tab → the character → Magic tab → the spell) and in the effect row window opened from it, a player was shown "A creature this seat cannot open" whenever the world held a copy of the creature they could not observe, although the creature's compendium was open to them and the cast summons from it. The sheet now names the copy the cast would summon.

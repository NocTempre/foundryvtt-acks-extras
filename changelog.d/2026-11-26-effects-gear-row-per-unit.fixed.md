- **Gear that carries Active Effects of its own, bought or opened from a bundle in a count, now arrives as one row per unit and each can be equipped alone.** Three such swords bought at a market (a market's window → Buy) or dropped as a bundle onto a character's sheet used to land as one row of 3 that could not be divided or drawn one at a time; they now land as three rows of 1 on the character sheet's Equipment tab (Actors sidebar tab → a character's sheet → Equipment tab). A row of several already in a world is left as it is, and its Quantity field is still the way to change it.

- A creature a spell called to your side that turns hostile when its control ends (lost concentration, the duration running out, or the Judge's hand) now leaves the party instead of staying a member. A creature riding inside the party token is set down hostile on the party's scene beside the party token, and one already out as a token stays where it stands; the card in chat says who left the party. If the party has no token on a scene to set it down on, it stays on the roster, hostile, and the card says so (take it out with Party sheet → Order tab → Remove from the formation). A member of the party who is not a spell's creature and whose token is set to Hostile stays in the party: the warning box at the top of the Party sheet, on every tab and the same for the Judge and the players, names them. Nothing is removed or blocked by it. Drop a creature's token on the open Party sheet, or use Add to party on its token HUD, to put one back.

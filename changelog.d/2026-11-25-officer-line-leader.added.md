- **The officer line shows the leader's portrait, and Make on a faction's top row sets the faction's leader.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a faction, its Org chart tab, and press a card's pen: a live officer line draws the actor's own picture in a frame (an actor with no picture draws none), and the **+ Make** tooltip says it makes the leader only, with no gear or mount rolled. **+ Make** on the faction's top row also sets the faction's leader while that field is blank, and never replaces a leader already set.

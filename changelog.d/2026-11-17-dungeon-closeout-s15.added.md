- **Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing:** a new crossing kind, **Squeeze · chokepoint**. The Judge picks the Shape (a chokepoint, which one made throw clears, or a squeeze of a Length) and sets a Judge modifier; every member's row shows the squeezing throw against the stone they carry (a Contortionism holder's is lower by the imported bonus), how far they have come, the last throw, and, once stuck, Stuck with the Vulnerable it brings, how many escapes were tried and the penalty failed pushes add, and two presses, **Wiggle back** (core's Paralysis save, with the stone and the penalty on it) and **Push forward** (the Dungeonbashing throw). In a fight the primary GM's client posts a prompt card at each member's turn, with a Throw button for a squeezer and the two escapes for a stuck member; nothing rolls by itself. A player presses for members they own; a Judge is warned where a player is stopped. Hover any figure for its working and the page; a figure nobody imported is blank and its tooltip is the page alone. A throw leaves a member stuck only once the botch band has been imported (re-run the Rules tables import), and a made throw on a squeeze of a set length does not yet carry the member along it; a chokepoint is cleared by one made throw.

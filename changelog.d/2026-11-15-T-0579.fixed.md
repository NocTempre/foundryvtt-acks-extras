- **Scene controls → Battlemap → Map setup, on a square or scale-only map:** a box you drag over a strip of squares, such as three squares in a row, now reads one square's size correctly. Each box's row under the samples has two small boxes, "across" and "down", in place of the single "spans" count, so "3 across, 1 down" is a count you can state. Before, the one count divided both the box's width and its height, so a one-square-tall strip read a third as tall as it was.

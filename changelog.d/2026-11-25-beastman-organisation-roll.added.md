- **A beastman organisation can be rolled into a chart row.** `acksExtras.monsters.rollOrganisation` rolls a village, warband or gang for a faction or a group and makes its groups, its stacks and one actor for each leader (named as its unit's officer, and a village chieftain as the faction's leader). It shares one prototype between the stacks that name it, builds a stack from another creature's library entry where the word matches one and labels it where none does, adds the treasure rows to a pot, and whispers the Judges a chat card listing what was thrown, the units made and what it could not settle. `acksExtras.lib.org.deleteRow` deletes a row with its subtree and the prototypes nothing else names. There is no sheet button yet: the API reaches both.

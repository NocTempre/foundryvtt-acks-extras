- **Trapbreaking, the disarm-or-discharge question:** after a made throw, the question "Disarm it, or set it off?" now opens on the thrower's own screen, whether the attempt came from a player's request, the turn plan (Formation sheet, Turn plan) or the Judge's own Trapbreaking window, instead of on the Judge's. The trap is saved as disarmed before the question is put, so a question nobody answers leaves it disarmed. A character's name containing markup no longer renders in the question, and a natural automatic-failure face now fails a Trapbreaking throw even when the total reaches the target.

- **Trapbreaking, the disarm-or-discharge question:** after a made throw, the question "Disarm it, or set it off?" now opens on the thrower's own screen, whether the attempt came from a player's request, the turn plan (party sheet, Dungeon tab, Turn plan) or the Judge's own Trapbreaking window (party sheet, Dungeon tab head, Trapbreaking), instead of on the Judge's. The trap is saved as disarmed and the roll card posts at once; the question never holds up the clock or the turn plan, so a question nobody answers leaves the trap disarmed, and a discharge answered later is noted in one whispered line, provided the trap is still disarmed. A character's name containing markup no longer renders in the question, and a natural automatic-failure face now fails a Trapbreaking throw even when the total reaches the target.

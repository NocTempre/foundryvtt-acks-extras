- **A sheet attack keeps its damage buttons while the actor's token is on the
  scene.** A weapon attack, or a spell cast, made from an actor's sheet opened
  in the Actors sidebar while one of that actor's unlinked tokens is on the
  viewed scene posted its chat card with the system's raw apply button.
  The card now gets the damage-against-defenses apply buttons, as it does when
  the attack is made from the token.

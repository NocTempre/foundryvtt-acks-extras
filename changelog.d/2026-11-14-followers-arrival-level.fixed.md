The followers roll (`Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Generate Followers (Selected)`) no longer carries the level followers arrive at in its code: that level is read from the Rules tables you import from your own book (RR p. 334), and its warning for a character below it now names the level and the page. Without that figure the roll does nothing, and a grant whose table prints no level is listed without one instead of at a first or zeroth level. No world sees a change until the followers tables the roll also reads are imported, which no import does yet, so the roll stays off for now.

- **A spell or a held state that puts a condition on a creature now puts the condition's figure with it.** The effect a cast leaves for a condition, and the effect a mapper's role or a crossing holds, is the one Foundry's own status toggle would make: it carries the condition's changes from the system's list and the conditions it brings, and the figure counts once however many effects name the condition. A condition the system gives no figure is written as before. Spell casting card: Chat tab → a cast's card → Apply.

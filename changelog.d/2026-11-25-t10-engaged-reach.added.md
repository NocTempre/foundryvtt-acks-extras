- **Chat tab → an "engaged" card, and Actors tab → a character or monster → its sheet's "In reach of" strip: a beastman generated with an engaged trait now holds its reach for as long as it is in a started fight.** When a token is beside it at the start of that token's turn, or steps into the adjacent square on its own turn, the card shows one row for each kind of item with the bearers named under "Engaged by", the save is whispered to the token's owners, and a failure or a pass is kept on the token as a timed condition or immunity. A seat that owns every bearer on a row can press **Not engaged** on the card to close it with no save, and Undo reopens it. The strip shows the reach as Save owed, Failed or Immune with the turns left, and the Save can be pressed there.

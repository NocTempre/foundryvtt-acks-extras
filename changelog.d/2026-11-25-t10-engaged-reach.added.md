- A beastman generated with an engaged trait now holds its reach for as long as it is in a started fight: when a token is beside it at the start of that token's turn, or steps into the adjacent square on its own turn, the Chat tab shows an "engaged" card with one row for each kind of item and the bearers named under "Engaged by", the save is whispered to the token's owners, and a failure or a pass is kept as a timed condition or immunity on the token. A seat that owns every bearer on a row can press **Not engaged** on the card to close it with no save, and Undo reopens it. The token's own sheet (Actors tab → the character or monster) shows the reach in its "In reach of" strip as Save owed, Failed or Immune with the turns left, and the Save can be pressed there.

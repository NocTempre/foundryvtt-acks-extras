The shared trailing words now reach the first alternative; one spell held is granted, and with both held neither is granted and the biography's As printed block gains a "One of" line naming them, until you drag the right one onto the character, after which a rebuild keeps it and adds no line. The As printed block is on the character's Notes tab: Compendium Packs tab → the Judge's shelf of Actors → the book's Pregenerated folder → the character.

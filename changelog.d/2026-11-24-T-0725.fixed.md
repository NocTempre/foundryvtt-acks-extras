- **Delayed damage whose fight ends before it is due now lands, and no longer stays on its bearer for good.** When a combat is deleted, the Judge's seat that runs the automation moves each pending delayed-damage record of that fight onto the world clock, at the rounds it still had to run times the round length imported from the Judge's Revised Rulebook, and the damage lands, with its card, when world time reaches that point however the clock moves. A creature that is defeated or marked dead is owed nothing, and its record is removed. If the world has not imported the round length, the record is removed instead and the Judge gets a notification naming the creature, the amount owed, the source and the page, to apply by hand (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports adds the figure).

- **Delayed damage whose fight ends before it is due now lands, and no longer stays on its bearer for good.** When a combat is deleted, the Judge's seat that runs the automation moves each pending delayed-damage record of that fight onto the world clock, at the rounds it still had to run times a round's length, and the damage lands, with its card, when world time reaches that point however the clock moves. A creature that is defeated or marked dead is owed nothing, and its record is removed.

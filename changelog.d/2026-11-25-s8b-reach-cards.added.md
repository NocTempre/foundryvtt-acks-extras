- **An item raised with a reach now posts a card in chat that lists who is in reach, and asks each body for its save when its turn starts or it moves in.** The raised card (Chat tab) shows each body's distance, its save target and result, with a Save for the body's owners and Undo for whoever applied it or owns the body; the bearer's owners get Check range again and Lower, and an owner of the item can count the bearer in. The body's owners are whispered a save card of their own, a failed save names what it wrote and for how long, and a failure outside a fight lasts to the body's first initiative.

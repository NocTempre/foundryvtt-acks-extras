- **Pressing Unarmed Strike on a character sheet's Rolls tab, under Attack, no longer asks for a damage formula on every throw.** With no unarmed die imported, the strike opened a prompt each time, and an answer typed into it could not be kept. The strike now makes its attack throw with no damage roll, and so does a spell's attack whose row states no roll. A weapon you own with no damage formula still asks once and keeps what you type.

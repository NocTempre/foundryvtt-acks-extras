- **Making a place by hand no longer opens the actors made after it to every player.** After a place was created from the Actors sidebar tab, every actor the same browser created without naming its ownership (another place, a monster, an imported actor) was given Owner for all players, until that page was reloaded. Now only the hand-made place itself is opened to the table. An actor a world already holds at Owner this way is not changed: Actors sidebar tab → right-click the actor → Configure Ownership sets it back.

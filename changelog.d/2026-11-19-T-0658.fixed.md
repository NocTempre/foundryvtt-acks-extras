- **A readied torch rolls fire damage.** A torch readied from a character sheet (the sheet's vision strip, in the torch menu) or from the ready control on a torch stack's item sheet was given its damage type only in a note the attack roll never read, so its swing and its throw rolled untyped, and fire-immune and fire-vulnerable targets took it as plain damage. The type is now written to the weapon's own Damage Type (item sheet → Details), as for any weapon, and the attack roll carries it.

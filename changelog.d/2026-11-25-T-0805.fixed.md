- **A treasure type whose cell has one clause the book gives no count for now deals the rest of that cell.** In Scene controls → Token controls → New treasure pot, pressing Deal refused the whole cell as soon as one of its clauses printed a chance and a noun but no count, so none of its other clauses was thrown. That clause alone is now refused and named in the deal's notice, and the others are dealt. A Judge who wants it dealt supplies the count by overriding the table in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables.

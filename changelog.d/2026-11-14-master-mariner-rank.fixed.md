- **Vehicle sheet → Seamanship block → Hazard throw dialog, and the Aboard tab's Seafaring field:** the Seafaring rank that makes a master mariner is read from your own copy of the Revised Rulebook when you import the voyages tables. The dialog's "Master mariner at the helm" box is ticked from the best Seafaring aboard once the rank is imported, and its label names the pages; the Travel tab's master-mariner tack line and the Seafaring field's ceiling follow the same rank. With nothing imported the box is left unticked, the field has no ceiling, and no vessel counts as having a master mariner.

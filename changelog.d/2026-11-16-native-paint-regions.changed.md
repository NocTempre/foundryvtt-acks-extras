- **Scene controls → Battlemap → Country brush:** a hex you paint is now a cell of a core grid shape on the value's Region (one Region per terrain, territory or domain, drawn with no polygon per hex), so the cells can be added or removed in the Region's own shape editing (Scene controls → Regions) as well as with the brush, and the map, the Territory and Domains modes and a domain's territory rows follow such an edit at once. Brush Regions are created unlocked for that. A drag as fast as the mouse moves now keeps every hex it crosses, and **Apply** (Scene controls → Battlemap → Map setup) re-keys the grid-shape cells of imported zones as well as the painted hexes when the grid moves under them.

- **Items sidebar tab → a weapon's sheet → its alternate damage field, and the grip choice on its attack, no longer offer a second, two-handed grip on a weapon that was never given one.** The system fills a weapon's alternate damage with a one-handed die when none is stated, so a Medium melee weapon created or stored with a base die alone offered a grip nobody set. The last release already hid this by reading such a weapon's record as it loaded; an untagged change removed that, and without this fix this release would have shown a second grip on weapons that have none today. A weapon now starts with an empty alternate unless one is stated, and one already stored with a die keeps it, since the record states it.

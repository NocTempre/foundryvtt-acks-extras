- **Importing the official character sheet no longer unequips what it just placed, and no longer raises a "Not enough hands" toast for every item.** An import puts gear into hands one item at a time, and each placement alone could overflow the hands and be resolved before the next one arrived, so a re-import ended on the old loadout and a first import silently dropped a shield or a bow. The character's hands are now held while the sheet's gear is placed and settled once after the last: the sheet's own placements are kept in the sheet's order until the hands are full (a placement that does not fit is carried, and a later one that does fit is still held), then whatever was held before and the sheet did not place. Where anything was carried, the report shows a **Hands** section after its counts line (Actors sidebar tab → a character → its sheet's window menu (⋮) → Official sheet (PDF) → Import, or Actors sidebar tab → Import sheet for a new character), naming what was carried; swap it into a hand from the character sheet's Equipment tab. An ordinary equip outside an import still warns as before (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Equip-limit enforcement).

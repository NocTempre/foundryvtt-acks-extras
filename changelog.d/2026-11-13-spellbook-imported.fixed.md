A spell book's page capacity, a blank book's price and the value of a scribed spell level are read from your own book, and are editable under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables. A spell book's item sheet (Contents tab) shows its pages used against the imported capacity with each figure's source on hover; where the capacity is not imported it shows the pages used and "not imported", with no capacity bar and no over-capacity warning. A book's value, from the equipment API's `spellbookValue`, is null while a figure it needs is not imported, and a new book's record stores no page count. The library API version is 41.

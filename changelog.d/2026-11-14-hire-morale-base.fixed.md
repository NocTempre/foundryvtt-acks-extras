A mercenary hired from the market (location sheet → Mercenaries tab → **Attempt to recruit**) starts with the base morale your book prints for its troop type (RR p. 166), read from the imported mercenary table; any other market hire, and a troop whose figure is not imported, has no recorded base and shows the hireling sheet's own Morale field instead. The hireling's Service tab names which one a base is: hover the Morale figure.

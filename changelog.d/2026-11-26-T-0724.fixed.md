- **An imported monster's damage modifier is no longer counted twice on a die attack.** A stat line printing a die or "by weapon" with a modifier gave the creature a damage modifier that core adds to every weapon it rolls, so a die standing for the by-weapon damage rolled the modifier on top, and a natural attack beside a by-weapon one gained it. Each such weapon now takes the modifier back on its own Damage bonus (Actors sidebar tab → the monster → Items → the weapon), so it rolls its die alone, and the creature keeps its modifier where it was. A monster imported earlier takes the change with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, with its row ticked (Repair in place).

- **Item sheet → Construction tab, a container's Access row:** which carriers cost an action to retrieve from and which do not is read from your own copy of the Revised Rulebook when you import (RR p. 294), and the row's tooltip names the page. The **Annotate Equipment (RAW profiles)** macro (ACKS Extras Macros compendium) stamps the side the page gives a carrier; a carrier the page does not name stays unset until you choose, and with nothing imported no access is stamped.

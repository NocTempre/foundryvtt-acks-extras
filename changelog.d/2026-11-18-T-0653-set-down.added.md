- **Set a pack down on the ground.** Actors sidebar → a character → Equipment tab → a container's header → **Set down** puts the container you carry, and everything in it, on the ground where your token stands. The container leaves its worn place, your Load drops, and **Kept elsewhere** lists it as "On the ground · <name>" tagged **At risk** (anyone standing at the pile can take from it, and it stays where it was left), with **Pick up** (back to the slot it was worn in) and, for the Judge, **Open**. Only the character's owner sees the control, and only on a container carried at the top level.
- **Take from a pile.** Actors sidebar → a character → Equipment tab → **Kept elsewhere** → the pile's row → **Show** → **Take** moves one line of the pile onto your character, loose under **Carried**; a stack takes the number you type, or the whole stack when the box is blank. A character standing at someone else's pile sees its row too, "On the ground · <owner>'s <container>", with **Take** on every line and no **Pick up**. A pile is reached only by standing at it: dropping one on a character's sheet does not pin it. On the system sheet, Actors sidebar → a character → Storage tab lists your pile with **Take everything** and no **Leave something here**.

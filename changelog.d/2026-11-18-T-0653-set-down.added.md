- **Set a pack down on the ground.** Actors sidebar → a character → Equipment tab → a container's header → **Set down** puts the container you carry, and everything in it, on the ground where your token stands. The container leaves its worn place, your Load drops, and **Kept elsewhere** lists it as "On the ground · <name>" tagged **At risk**, with **Pick up** (back to the slot it was worn in) and **Open**. Only the character's owner sees the control, and only on a container carried at the top level.

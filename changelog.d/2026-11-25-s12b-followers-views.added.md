- **A character's Followers tab can show its followers as an org chart or by place.** Open a character sheet and choose the Followers tab: once the character has a henchman, a hired unit or a monster follower, a Cards / Org chart / Locations switch appears under the tab's heading. Org chart draws the henchmen and hired units as a tree by who serves whom, each card leading with its kind (henchman, mercenary, specialist) and its follower card, with Expand all and Collapse all; Locations lists them by the place each stands on, those on no place standing with their employer. On either view the owner can send a follower out to a place and recall it from there, and Open shows the follower's own sheet. Cards is the tab as it was.

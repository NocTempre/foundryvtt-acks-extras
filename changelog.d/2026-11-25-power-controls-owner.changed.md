- **The power controls belong to whoever owns the character.** In a power's panel (Actors sidebar tab → the character's sheet → Magic tab → the button beside the Divine power or Arcane power tag), Add, Remove, the Fades / Stays switch, the arcane lifetime's count and Undo are offered to every user who owns the character, where they were the Judge's alone; a user who can see the sheet without owning the character reads the tags and the entries. An entry made by hand is named for the user who pressed, and several presses by one user on one day show as one entry. Undo now also takes back a gain, as long as none of it has been spent; a gain partly spent says so on its row.

- **Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing:** a new crossing kind, **Swim · deep water**. The Judge sets the water's conditions (cold, rough, fast-moving), a penalty (none, one of the imported sizes, or a larger figure typed), whether the party moves at a Combat or a Running pace and a Judge modifier; a player reads "Penalty in force" instead. Each member's row shows the swimming throw or "No throw", Afloat or Drowning, the pace for the movement picked (a member a condition bars from running reads "Cannot run"), the last throw and, for a drowning member, the breath left, how far they sink each round and the Vulnerable that drowning brings. In a fight, the primary GM's client posts a prompt card at each member's turn whose Throw button makes the throw, and counts a drowning member's rounds once each; nothing rolls by itself, and the round the breath runs out is reported to the Judges without writing hit points. A failed throw makes the member drowning until they are hauled to the surface or the crossing ends. A drowning member's **Rescuer** pick names who carries them (the rescuer's own throw is priced with the body's stone, and a rescuer who fails lets go); the Judge may name any member who is not drowning, a player only one they own. **Haul to the surface**, the Judge's alone, brings a drowning member back afloat. Hover any figure for its working and the page; a figure nobody imported is blank and its tooltip is the page alone. A creature with a swim speed of its own takes no throw, and a monster with none throws against the load it carries (its own items and coin).

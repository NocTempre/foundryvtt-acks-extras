- **Henchmen, formation, markets, equipment, vehicles, influence and classes text:** hints, refusals and chat lines that restated a rule from the book now say what the field, result or record does, and keep their page reference. A result hint names the result and its page; a refusal names what was compared, such as an attempt already recorded at the character's current level. The influence roll's chat card no longer adds a note about intimidation beside its result, and examples that quoted a printed figure are gone. Language strings that nothing read any more are removed.

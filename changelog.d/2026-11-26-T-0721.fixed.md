- **An imported creature's encounter recipe now scopes a part to the group its own stat block names.** A creature whose encounter lines count its groups in a word other than gang, warband, lair or village had the parts its Ecology prose places at that group (captives, a shaman or witch doctor, a guard, its dependants, its chieftain) read with no unit, so a Roll of its organisation (the organisation chart's tool row, beside Roll at random) never made them. They now carry the group's word, and the Roll lists and makes them; the recipe editor shows it (Actors sidebar tab → the monster → Ecology tab → Encounter → Organisation…). A creature imported earlier takes the change with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries, with its row ticked (Repair in place).

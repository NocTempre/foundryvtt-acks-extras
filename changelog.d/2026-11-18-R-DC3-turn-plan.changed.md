- **For module authors, in the Turn plan on the party sheet's Dungeon tab (Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → Turn plan):** an activity registered with `registerTurnActivity` can now name its own choices and refusals there. A string `label` on an option shows after the activity's name in the select (`Activity (Label)`), as a lang key when one exists and as plain text otherwise. A `reasons: {prefix, root}` on the activity declaration words every refusal it answers as `<prefix><code>` from the lang key `<root>.<code>`, falls back to `<root>.other`, and shows nothing for `<prefix>notImported`; a prefix nothing declared still reads as the generic line.

- **A location actor's sheet → Things here → an obstacle's row → Crossing:** the Judge sets the figures a crossing begins with from that place. A climb obstacle offers its length and depth, a jump its gap and depth, a squeeze its shape and length, and a swim its water penalty (None, one of the imported steps, or Larger… with its own figure). A blank field sets nothing, so the crossing panel's own default stands. The scene screen's Keying aside (party sheet → Dungeon tab → Search here, then select the obstacle's card) carries the same fieldset.

- **＋ Officer, and an actor dropped on an officer line.** Actors sidebar tab → a faction → Org chart tab → a card's pen → its officer line. A row that cannot offer **+ Make the …** (a part of a village, for one) now offers **＋ Officer**: one actor, a copy of the creature the row's group holds or else a blank actor, named by the row and set as its officer. On any row, drag an actor from the Actors sidebar tab onto the officer line to make it the officer; the one it replaces is unlinked, not deleted, and a group, faction or place dropped there is ignored. A part row's officer line no longer says "champion": it names the actor alone.

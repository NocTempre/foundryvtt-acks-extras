- **A token drawn at a size other than its footprint now carries a frame:** a pink rim along the token's shape and a short handle from its lower right, like a magnifying glass, so a man drawn a hex wide on a six-mile map reads as not to scale at a glance. Hover the token to see which way it is off and the size it is drawn at in the scene's units; the Judge and the token's owner also see its footprint in feet and where that comes from. The party token and a marker's token never carry it, and it needs the monster book's square imported. Every player sees it; turn it off for yourself in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Not-to-scale mark on tokens.

- **A market assessment's chosen goods wait for the Judge, and a false assessment's figures are the Judge's.** A trader who chooses goods on a partial or false market report (character sheet → Trade tab → Research → the report's sheet) now sees each chosen good as "Awaiting the Judge" with no figure; the module no longer rolls a wrong figure for a false report. The Judge is whispered a card for each choice, and answers on the report's sheet: "Answer from the market" fills a partial report's goods from the market's demand, and on a false report the Judge types each good's modifier (clearing the field returns the good to waiting). Both outcomes read the same to the player at both stages. Reports chosen before this keep the figures they were given.

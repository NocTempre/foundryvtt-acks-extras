- **A monster's ability scores now count in the module's throws.** A score a Judge enters gives the acks system's own modifier for it on a drowning monster's breath, a squeezing monster's push, a door bash, a night's Endurance and an ability's score term; a blank score is the default specimen and adds nothing, as before. The scores are entered on the Monster sheet → Classification tab → Attributes, and an entered score shows as its own part in the figure's tooltip.

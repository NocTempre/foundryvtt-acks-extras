- **A monster's ability scores now count in the module's throws.** A score a Judge enters gives the acks system's own modifier for it on a drowning monster's breath, a squeezing monster's push, a door bash, a night's Endurance, an ability's score term and, for Wisdom, a saving throw against magic, and a jump reads the entered Dexterity; a blank score is the default specimen and adds nothing, as before. The scores are entered on the Monster sheet → Classification tab → Attributes, and an entered score shows as its own part in the figure's tooltip. The rules the Monstrous Manual gives a monster's scores apart from a character's (Strength on natural attacks and load, Dexterity on speed, Constitution on hit points, Charisma on morale) are not applied yet.

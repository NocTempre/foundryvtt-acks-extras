**Repair in place resets the library's copies to the book.** Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Repair in place: a library document's name, picture and the edits you made are listed read-only in *Your edits and the book* and reset when you press *Apply these choices*; what play has set (a trap's level, a stack's quantity, a spell's cast count) stays. A creature, NPC, template or family you changed is listed under *Changed in the library since its import*, with *Keep a copy, then reset it* (the default), *Leave it as it stands* and *Reset it*, and *For every actor with no record:* answers every row with no record of its import. A copy dragged out of the library into the sidebar is listed under *Copies outside the library*, each with an unticked *Bring up to the library* box that writes the fields it still holds as the library had them. *Take the book's for every edit* and *Keep every edit of mine* are gone; closing the window keeps everything.

- **A dealt hoard's magic finds are rolled on your item tables.** In a staged
  treasure pot's left rail (Scene controls → Token controls → New treasure pot),
  Deal now rolls each magic find on your imported magic item tables, in the
  table style chosen there: a find whose magic kind names one of the lists
  becomes the item rolled, stored undetected and unidentified, and one that
  names no list (or two, or none while the tables are not imported) stays
  unnamed, with a notice that some magic was not rolled.

- **A weapon or a suit of armour is a stack, and drawing one takes one from it.** Three swords bought, dropped from a bundle or read from a character PDF arrive as one row holding 3, as the system's own drop makes them, and identical resting rows fold into one. Equipping a stack (the equip control on the item sheet, or the character sheet's Equipment tab) leaves a resting row of 2 and a drawn row of 1, and putting the sword away or recovering a thrown one folds it back. A weapon or armour row shows no count control yet: no divide, take-how-many or sale count on the character sheet's Equipment tab, a place's Storage tab or Trade tab, or a vehicle's Hold tab.

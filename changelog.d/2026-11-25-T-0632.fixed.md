- **Ctrl+Z in the map-setup capture tools now removes the sample you made last, whatever its kind.** It used to take the scale bar first whenever one existed, then a corner or a box by count, so undoing after drawing a box, a bar and a corner removed them in the wrong order. With a tool armed (Scene controls → Battlemap → Draw box, Pick corners or Scale bar), each Ctrl+Z takes the newest box, corner or bar still on the map; a sample removed with the Eraser or its row's remove button is skipped, and a redrawn scale bar counts as the newest.

- **A beastman template offers special troops, tribal traits and optional equipment, and a second source of leaders.** Open the family template (Compendium Packs sidebar tab → ACKS II — Extras → From your books → ACKS Cookbook — Actor → the Beastman template) and its builder gains a Special troop select, a Tribal trait select and an Optional equipment list, plus a Leader table choice under Leader figures. A troop or trait a breed has no roll band for is listed disabled, and Roll on the table rolls one on the breed's bands. Generate writes the standing figures (attack, armour class, initiative, surprise, damage, morale, natural attacks, and the leader table's armour class, Hit Dice and hit points) with a tooltip naming each part and its page; the rest of each entry, and the equipment, is recorded on the creature and its notes name each with its page. The tables come from your own Monstrous Manual: import it with its tables (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports), then repair the Beastman family in place (Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries) so the template gains the axes.

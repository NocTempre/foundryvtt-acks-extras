- **An imported creature's encounter recipe now reads four more wordings its page may use for what a group is made of.** A dependant share worded as a percent of the males, dependants worded as eggs, females, children or noncombatants (a bracketed note after the word is fine), a lair a page says is ruled by a rank rather than led by one, and a chieftain's escort worded "accompanied by" or "escorted by" (a rank or a creature, a count or a die, "for every" gang or warband) were skipped, so a Roll of the creature's organisation made none of them. Each is now a part of the recipe, and the parts show in the organisation chart's Roll panel under "This roll will make" (Actors sidebar tab → a faction or a group → Org chart tab, tool row beside Roll at random) wherever the creature's own lines count a unit the part names, and in the recipe editor (Actors sidebar tab → the monster → Ecology tab → Encounter → Organisation…). A guard sentence that names one place (dungeon or wilderness) now keeps it as the part's Found in. A share of the males counts every body of the unit, as a share of the warriors does on a creature the page never splits. A creature imported earlier takes the change with Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → (Re)import individual entries.

- **Chat sidebar → "The fight is over" card (Judges only, after a combat ends):** when a combat is deleted, a card whispered to the Judges lists each defeated hostile combatant with what it carried, its treasure type and whether a treasure table is linked to it. **Search the scene** on the card opens the scene search for the fallen (see the scene-search entry); each linked table is drawn without posting to the table, and a carried item leaves its body when the scene's pot opens, only from bodies that are still there. Nothing reaches a player until the Judge shows the scene. A treasure type is listed on the card and deals nothing until the treasure tables are imported.

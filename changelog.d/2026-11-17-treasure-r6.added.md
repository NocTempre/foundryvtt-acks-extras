- **Chat sidebar → "Search the fallen" (Judges only, after a combat ends):** when a combat is deleted, a card whispered to the Judges lists each defeated hostile combatant with what it carried, its treasure type and whether a treasure table is linked to it. **Stage a pot** on the card makes a treasure pot of the carried goods, draws each linked table without posting to the table, and opens the pot's window; the goods leave the bodies when the pot is opened, and only from bodies that are still there. Nothing reaches a player until the Judge opens the pot. A treasure type is listed on the card and deals nothing until the treasure tables are imported.

- **A Fades / Stays switch on the power ledger (Actors tab → a character → its sheet → Magic tab → casting strip, and the same strip on the system's own sheet; Judge only):** beside each power's plus and minus, a pair of buttons sets what that power's plus adds: **Fades** (the default) adds a point that lapses after the lifetime you imported, **Stays** adds one that never expires and is never due to fade. A point that stays shows a pin and "stays until removed" on its row and in the tag's tooltip, and it counts in the balance whatever the lifetime. The minus spends points that fade first, oldest first, and only then points that stay. The switch is not saved: every render of the sheet starts it on Fades, and a plus re-renders the sheet, so press Stays before each plus that should stay. A Player's strip draws no switch, and a Player's own request for a point that stays is refused.

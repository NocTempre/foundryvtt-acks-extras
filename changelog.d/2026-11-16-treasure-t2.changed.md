- **Item sheet → Details tab, Effects tab and the item's art, on a player's sheet:** a magic item an earlier deal marked undetected no longer shows a player that it is magic: no identification panel, no note that effects are hidden, no aura. A Judge sees everything as before. **Character sheet → Equipment tab:** a place's badge counts only the magic a player knows of, while a place over its capacity is still marked full. A successful identification now marks the item detected; Loremastery and Magical Engineering also restore a disguised item's name, and a full identification removes the disguise. A Judge marks it detected, or not, with the **Detected** checkbox on the item sheet → Appearance tab → What The Players Know (a magical item only); a player's value badge on an undetected item no longer says "apparent worth". Items bought or made by hand are unchanged.

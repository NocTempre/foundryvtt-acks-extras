- **Judges: a player's summoning spell may need a grant.** A spell whose row names a creature from a Judge's book, or a world actor the player cannot open, no longer casts for that player. Give the player Observer on the creature (`Actors tab → right-click the creature → Configure Ownership`), or give the Player role Observer on its compendium (`Compendium Packs tab → right-click the pack → Configure Ownership`), which opens the whole compendium to every player. Spells that call a creature from the monster book's compendium are unaffected while that compendium keeps Foundry's default ownership.

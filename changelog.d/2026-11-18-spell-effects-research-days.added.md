- **Research days on a project (Actors tab → a location → its sheet → Research tab):** a project now shows a strip of its days, a chip with the day worked against the days due, and each participant's daily rate beside their name. Pick how today is worked (dedicated, ancillary or both) and press **Post day**: the day is posted, and credited to the project once the world clock has passed it, so research is paid out as game time moves, never ahead of it. Each counted participant is credited their rate for the pace, read from your Revised Rulebook's research rate by class level and its ancillary share (Import Everything, or the **Reimport One Shelf (GM)** macro in `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`). An assistant counts only at or above the level the rulebook sets for aiding and within the number the researcher manages; a Player who adds one below that is blocked and a Judge is warned. A Player posts today only, and a facility below its minimum blocks a Player's post and warns a Judge's. On the top rungs the rate follows the rulebook's footnote: which rungs it marks and which projects it names are read from your copy at import, and a rate that needs one that was not read stays blank. The due is computed from the rates while they are imported; with nothing imported the rate chips stay blank, carrying the page reference, and a passed day stays posted, uncredited and with no gold written, until its rates are read, when the next time step credits it. The connected Judge's window credits the passed days on each time step, once per day. Entering the due in days by hand where nothing is imported, and withdrawing a posted day, are not built yet.

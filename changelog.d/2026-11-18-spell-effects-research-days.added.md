- **Research days on a project (Actors tab → a location → its sheet → Research tab):** a project now shows a strip of its days, a chip with the day worked against the days due, and each participant's daily rate beside their name. Pick how today is worked (dedicated, ancillary or both) and press **Post day**: the day is posted, and credited to the project once the world clock has passed it, so research is paid out as game time moves, never ahead of it. Each counted participant is credited their rate for the pace, read from your Revised Rulebook's research rate by class level and its ancillary share (Import Everything, or the **Reimport One Shelf (GM)** macro in `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`). An assistant counts only at or above the level the rulebook sets for aiding and within the number the researcher manages; a Player who adds one below that is blocked and a Judge is warned. A Player posts today only, and a facility below its minimum blocks a Player's post and warns a Judge's. On the top rungs the rate follows the rulebook's footnote: which rungs it marks and which projects it names are read from your copy at import, and a rate that needs one that was not read stays blank. The due is computed from the rates while they are imported; with nothing imported the rate chips stay blank, carrying the page reference, and a passed day stays posted, uncredited and with no gold written, until its rates are read, when the next time step credits it. The connected Judge's window credits the passed days on each time step, once per day. A Judge's **Post day** button reads **Post days…** and opens a prompt for how many days, from which day (today by default) and at which pace: each day is posted once, a day already posted or credited is skipped and named in the notification, and a facility or assistant warning shows once. Where the due cannot be computed (no rate imported, or no research cost entered) a Judge types the days due in a box in the project's parts, under the credited row; once it is computed the box is gone and the computed due is text, which a Player always sees as text. Under the strip each posted day that is not yet credited is listed with its pace, a tag (posted, or waiting, with the rate's page reference, where the day is held for a rate not imported) and an X that withdraws it: a Judge may withdraw any, a Player the posted days of their own project, and a credited day has none. A waiting day's cell in the strip has a dashed warning border.

- **Actors tab → a monster's sheet → Inventory tab, or a character's sheet → Equipment tab: a beastman's grog shows a "Swigs left" box, and a fuse bomb reads Light and Lit.** The box shows the swigs the grog has left, blank where none were rolled yet. An owner of the item can type a count or clear it. Its tooltip says whether the count was rolled when the grog was handed on (the dice and the total, with the page) or typed. A fuse bomb's press on its row, on the group sheet's stack row and on the Token HUD says **Light**, and while it burns its row reads **Lit** with the rounds left. Light is offered again during a burn only where the stack holds a spare.
- **Chat tab → a swig, on-hit or fuse-burst card that names no save: the Judge can settle it.** Where the save was not imported, the Judge's seat shows **Passed** and **Failed** on each open row. The Judge's call stands for the throw, nothing is rolled, and the result reads "(the Judge's call)". **Undo** takes it back.

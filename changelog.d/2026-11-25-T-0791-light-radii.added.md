- **A light spell's reach is typed on its Light row, and its cast lights the caster.** A spell's Light effect row (Items sidebar tab → a spell → its sheet → Mechanics tab → the row) now has Daylight, Bright and Dim radius, in feet, blank until you type them from your book; hover a blank one for the page the figure is printed on. Casting the spell puts a light of that reach on the caster, or on the creature the row names, for the spell's duration: the token sheds it, and the party sheet's Kit tab (Actors sidebar tab → the party's sheet → Kit tab → Light Sources) lists it with its Shutter. A spell with no duration leaves its light until its owner removes it; a reversed cast and a cast held by concentration light nothing.

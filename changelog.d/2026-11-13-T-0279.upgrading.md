- **A Judge-made character may stop reaching a Judge-made place it reached before.** A character and an
  unlinked place that the same Judge created, and a Judge-only NPC and any unlinked place, no longer reach
  each other by that shared owner, so a deposit or a payment that passed can now be refused as out of reach.
  To restore it, pin the place to the character: drag the place from the Actors sidebar tab onto the
  character's sheet. Or give the player ownership of the place: Actors sidebar tab → right-click the place →
  Ownership. Standing at the place also reaches it.

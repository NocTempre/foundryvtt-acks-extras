- **On the token, and in the Combat Tracker (a spell's condition that lasts a turn or a few rounds):** a spell condition whose page gives it a next turn or a number of rounds now stops by itself. In a fight, with the target one of its combatants, the condition ends at the end of that creature's next turn, or at the start of its turn that makes the rolled number of rounds; outside a fight, or if the target holds no turn in it, the world clock ends it after the same rounds. Whichever comes first removes it, and nothing is pressed. The count of a rounds condition is rolled once for each target when the spell is cast and shown on the cast card, and the entry's tooltip names the roll, which end the condition was written with and the page. A count the import left blank leaves the condition untimed and shows the page reference alone.

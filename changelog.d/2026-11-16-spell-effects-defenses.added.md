- **Chat sidebar → a weapon attack card that hit a target (Judge only):** the card's Apply is now the module's own, shown under the card, and it takes the target's immunities, resistances and susceptibilities into account, with Undo after it. A hit the module does not work out (a resistance whose fraction has not been imported, a susceptibility with no figure, or a hit that two defenses meet differently) shows no Apply, names the defense and its page, and is applied by hand with the hit-point tool. The Judge and the target's owners read which defense applied; other players read "a defense". Resistance's fraction and save modifier, once imported, can be overridden in the Ruledata Browser (`Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`).

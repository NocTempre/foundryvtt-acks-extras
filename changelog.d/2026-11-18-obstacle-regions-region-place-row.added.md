- **Regions scene control → a Region → Region window, Appearance tab:** a **Place** row under Name picks the place that holds this region's traps, obstacles and conditions, with **+** to make one. It shows what the place holds (names on hover) and takes a drop: drag a trap, or a hazard from the **Hazards** compendium, onto it and the place gains it, made first where the region has none.

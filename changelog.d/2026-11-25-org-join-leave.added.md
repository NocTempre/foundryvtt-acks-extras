- **A group can be listed under a faction card, and never has to be.** Open a group's sheet (Actors sidebar tab → the group), and its first line, Faction, reads None with **Join a faction…** and **Make its own…**. Join lists the group under a faction card you pick, its chart rows moving under that card with the group and its groups the same actors; Make its own makes a faction card named from the group, led by the group's officer where one is set, and joins it. Once joined the line names the card with **Open** and **Leave**, and Leave takes the group out again. A group with no chart of its own joins as one row that holds itself. A seat that cannot edit the card gets a warning and nothing is written.

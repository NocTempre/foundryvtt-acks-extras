- **Remove from chart takes only the row you press.** Open the Actors sidebar tab, a faction, its Org chart tab, press a card's pen, and use **Remove from chart** in the form's foot: the rows that stood directly beneath it stay on the chart and move up to its parent row, or to the top, and nothing is deleted. Before, the rows beneath went with it onto the removed row's group. The confirm names the rows that move and where, and asks only where rows move; a line above the chart says where they went. Rows lifted from the row that held the chart's family template keep that template, so they can still be rolled.

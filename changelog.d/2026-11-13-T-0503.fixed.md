The trap rules read their distances and dice from the books you import. The automatic search's reach (bare and with a pole), how far a pole probes ahead of its bearer, the secret die and the band that springs a trap, how far a Trapbreaking attempt reaches, the botch band of each Trapbreaking mode, the methodical bonus and a spiked pit's dice come from the Revised Rulebook and Judges Journal when Rules tables are imported, and the pit's per-depth die is the fall table's. Edit any of them in `Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables → Open Ruledata Browser`. A rank's depth is the column's imported interval, else the scene's cell size (Scene config → Grid). A figure that is not imported is said on the card, the Trapbreaking dialog or the trap sheet: with no trigger die or band a crossing is rolled by hand, with no pole lead no pole probes, and with no fall table a pit deals no derived damage. A trap's "Springs on" field on the trap sheet is blank to read the imported band, and a number typed there wins.

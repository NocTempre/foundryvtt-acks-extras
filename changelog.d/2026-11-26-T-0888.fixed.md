- **A player whose character a Judge casts a spell for can now Throw, Apply to their own targets, Undo and Revert on the Judge's cast card.** On the card in the Chat sidebar tab, the owner of the caster sees Throw on an entry waiting for it (and on a row with no target), the Apply to my targets button on a card with no target, Undo on an entry within the reach of the spell's own figures, and Revert on a performed step. The throw dialog opens on the player's own seat and the roll posts as the player's, while a connected GM's client writes the result onto the card; with no GM connected the press asks first and spends nothing. A player who owns neither the caster nor the target still sees none of them.

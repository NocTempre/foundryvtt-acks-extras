- **Macros and companion modules → `acksExtras.formation.swimming`:** the water penalty on the swimming throw is one figure the Judge sets, not a price per water condition. `swimmingThrow` and `partySwim` take `penalty` (a size: an imported step from the new `waterPenaltySteps()`, or a larger figure typed by hand) and `conditions` (a list of `WATER` keys ticked as the reason), and `swimmingThrow` returns the ticked `conditions` beside its target. `swimmingThrow` no longer reads a `water` key, and `partySwim`'s second argument is now `{ penalty, conditions }`. `waterModifier` is removed. `WATER` is now `cold`, `rough` and `fast`; calm is no key, since nothing ticked is no penalty. The `swimWaterModifiers` table is replaced by `swimWaterPenaltySteps`, an ordered list of positive sizes. A caller that passed `water` passes the size and the conditions instead; the old names are no longer read. Formation `apiVersion` 30.

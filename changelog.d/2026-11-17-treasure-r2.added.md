- **Treasure pot → value bands, backs, faces and deal toggles (Scene controls → Token controls → New treasure pot):** a face-down card shows its pool's icon on a colour that ranks it within the pot by apparent worth (four bands, Low to Highest, also shown as one to four marks), fixed when the pot opens. A magic item nobody has detected is ranked and drawn as the item it passes for, so its back is a plain item's; detecting it changes only the icon on its back. A turned magic item's face turns the magic colour once it is identified, and a cursed one's turns the cursed colour once the curse is shown to the table; the Judge's tiles always show the truth. The staging window gains a **Deal toggles** panel beside the pot (below it in a narrow window): **No magic** takes every magic card out of the pot when it opens, and **Force cursed** marks every remaining magic card cursed, so an item taken from it is created cursed; on a card already identified in full the curse is shown as well. Whichever is on, a whisper to the Judges lists the cards it changed.

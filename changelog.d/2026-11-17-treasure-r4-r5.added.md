- **Cards sidebar tab → the pot → its window, once the pot is open:** a turned card can be dragged to a character's sheet, or to a member's zone in the new party panel beside the pot, to take it for that character. The panel lists the party's members with their speed and how loaded they are, and its **Quick grab** button, offered to the Judge and to a player who owns a member of the party, shares everything that is turned across the party so it keeps moving as fast as it can, splitting a pile of coin where it must, weighing a value card as the coin it becomes, and telling you what fits nobody. A player is told when a character is not theirs to give to.

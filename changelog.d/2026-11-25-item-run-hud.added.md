- **Right-click a token → its Token HUD: an item the token's actor carries that is raised and lowered or used up has its own button in the HUD's right column.** The button wears the item's picture, or Foundry's aura icon where it has none, reads "<item> · Raise", "· Lower" or "· Use" as its label, and lights while the item is raised. Pressing it does what the item's Raise, Lower or Use does on its sheet row or hotbar macro. It is drawn for whoever owns the item, and an item that is used up draws no button.

The posting form no longer holds a level to a ceiling of its own. On a location sheet, Recruitment tab → Recruitment postings → New Posting, the Presented level box and the stored posting levels accept any whole number from 0 up, and a requested candidate level is held only to the employer level cap read from your own book (where none is imported, nothing is refused). A candidate's generated age at a level past the last imported age row takes that row's age.

A rider's body weight is read from the Revised Rulebook (RR p. 161) by the importer instead of a figure held in the module: until it is imported, a ridden mount's load, the rider's line on the monster sheet's Inventory tab and the Mount line on the character sheet's Equipment tab show no figure and their tooltips name the page, a mount or wagon is no longer refused or priced on a weight the module does not have, and a vehicle's hold counts a passenger of unknown weight for nothing.

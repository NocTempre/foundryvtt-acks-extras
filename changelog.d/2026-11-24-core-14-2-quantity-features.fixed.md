- **Stacks count again on `acks` 14.2.0.** The system now keeps an item's quantity as one plain number, and the places that still asked for the old `{value}` shape read every stack as none and wrote nothing back. Lighting a torch from a stack takes one off it again. A stack two or more deep splits one off and restacks it: the item sheet's equip control on the stack, or the Equipment tab on the character sheet, with its Split one off the stack control. Selling part of a stack leaves the rest with you: a place's sheet → Trade tab. Rations and foraged food are counted and eaten in the marching order, ammunition is spent from its stack, cargo manifests and the Ventures tab on a place's sheet draw and add loads, class and template gear arrives with its count, and a vehicle sheet's Hold tab lists a stack and changes its count.

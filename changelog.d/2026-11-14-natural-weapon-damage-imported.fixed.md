A monster's natural attacks take their damage type and their extraordinary mark from your own Monstrous Manual's Damage entry (MM Overview, p. 12) when Apply Stats or the first-pass monster import fills an actor: the damage type each natural weapon is listed against, and the hit dice and size an animal and the other listed types must pass, are read at import and editable under Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Imported Rules Tables (the `monsters` document, tables `naturalWeaponDamage` and `extraordinaryRule`). A weapon the page does not list carries no damage type beyond the one its stat block's icon names, and with the rule's figures not imported an attack of a type that needs one carries no `extraordinary` flag. The library's `NATURAL_WEAPONS` carries keys and labels only, and the library API version is 42.

- **Item sheet → Effects tab → Add effect → Magic Light:** a magic light now has a third range, daylight, nearest the light and brighter than torchlight. It counts as bright light everywhere bright light counts (the party's light, the mapper, the token), and the party sheet's Kit tab shows it as "N ft daylight" ahead of the bright and dim ranges. To give an existing magic light a daylight range, open its effect, Changes tab, and add a change keyed `flags.acks-extras.magicLight.daylight` of type Upgrade with the feet as its value. Nothing yet applies daylight's own consequences, such as light sensitivity.

- **The organisation is a tree you can work on the faction card.** Open a faction card (Actors sidebar tab → the card) and its new **Org chart** tab draws every row as a card with a count, its leader and **Roll**, **+**, a pen and **Send out**; **Roll at random**, **+ Warband**, **Expand all** and **Collapse all** sit above the tree. A card with nothing in it can stay empty, a Roll fills a card and the cards beneath it, and the pen changes a card's label, note and a stack's count; its **Remove** (faction sheet → Org chart tab → a card's pen → Remove) takes the card and the cards beneath it off the chart and deletes nothing: every group, creature and leader stays in the Actors tab, and a line above the chart names the groups that now host what came off. The **Locations** tab beside it shows where the groups stand, with **Recall** on each. A group that has joined a faction draws the same chart above its stacks, with the groups inside it listed below (Actors sidebar tab → the group). Generate on a family template with an encounter line (Actors sidebar tab → the family template → Generate) makes a group and opens it on its Organisation section; a Roll that makes a village then makes the faction card for it. A leader's card is the Follower Card in a frame for its rank, with its troop, trait and kit as badges, and a card's pen turns it into a form with the troop, the bodies and Done; a count that differs from what its Roll threw is marked as set by hand. Hidden cards are a Judge's alone, and the Members tab lists everyone the organisation affiliates.
- Removing a chart row that has rows under it asks first, naming the row and the rows that go. Turn the ask off at Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Ask before removing several chart rows. A row on its own is removed at once.

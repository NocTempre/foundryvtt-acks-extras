- **The organisation is a tree you can work on the faction card.** Open a faction card (Actors sidebar tab → the card) and its new **Org chart** tab draws every row as a card with a count, its leader and **Roll**, **+**, a pen and **Send out**; **Roll at random**, **+ Warband**, **Expand all** and **Collapse all** sit above the tree. A card with nothing in it can stay empty, a Roll fills a card and the cards beneath it, and the pen changes a card's label, note and a stack's count; its **Remove** takes the card, the cards beneath it and their groups, and lists the leaders left in the world. The **Locations** tab beside it shows where the groups stand, with **Recall** on each. A group that has joined a faction draws the same chart above its stacks, with the groups inside it listed below (Actors sidebar tab → the group). Generate on a family template with an encounter line makes the faction card and opens it on its Org chart tab. Hidden cards are a Judge's alone, and the Members tab lists everyone the organisation affiliates.
- Removing a chart row that has rows under it asks first, naming the row and the rows that go. Turn the ask off at Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Ask before removing several chart rows. A row on its own is removed at once.

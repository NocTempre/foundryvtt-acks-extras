- **A hand-made spell now spends a slot of its list, and a Player is refused when none is left.** A spell the cast window does not open for (one with no effect rows), cast by a character whose class casts from a list, raises that list's used count at the spell's level when it is cast. A Player is refused, with a notification, when that level's slots on the list are all used or the spell is on none of the class's lists; a Judge sees the same notification as a warning and the spell is cast anyway. Where the world holds no slot figure for the list, the cast goes through as before. A slot is given back with the pips beside that list's Slots figure at the spell's level (Actors sidebar tab → the character's sheet → Magic tab).

- **A summoned creature riding in a party is stamped when its control ends, and a hostile one loses its owners and turns hostile (T-0842).** It was left as it was, so its summoner could still dismiss a creature that had turned on them and it still read friendly when the party deployed. It now carries the same mark a placed creature does, and stays a member of the party until a Judge dismisses it; the end card in the chat log names the outcome, and the summoner's Dismiss (Character sheet → Magic tab → the summoning spell's row) is refused for a hostile one.

- A player who owns a Region now sees the Place row in its window (Scene controls → Regions → double-click the Region → its window → Appearance tab). It shows the name of the Region's place, or "A place you cannot open" where the player cannot open it, and, where the player owns the place, what it holds that is not hidden and a drop zone for a trap, obstacle or condition. A player who may create Actors and whose Region has no place can drop one there to make the place. A drop made by a player runs on the Judge's client and is written only onto a place that player owns; a drop onto a place they do not own, or onto bare ground, is refused in the same words.
- A trap a player drops is hidden, as every trap is, so the player who dropped it cannot see or remove it afterward and the Judge can. A trap a player drops beside a wall they cannot edit now lands on the place under the drop, and the wall is unchanged.
- The place row on the ACKS District behavior's window (Scene controls → Regions → double-click the Region → its window → Behaviors tab → the ACKS District behavior) now follows whether that window is editable for the seat, not whether the seat is the Judge's.

- **A trap reads the party's ranks as the column stands on the map.** When the party token walks into a trap
  (a Trap Zone region or a trapped wall), the Judge-only card in the `Chat` sidebar tab takes each member's
  rank from the column drawn behind the party token: a blank square and every body of a stack take their ground,
  and a member a wall squeezes out of a rank stands a rank back, so they meet the trap later and sit farther from an
  area effect. Where the Revised Rulebook's frontage tables are imported and the map is a square grid, a rank is the
  imported rank interval deep, which sets how many ranks an area trap's radius reaches and how far a searcher's reach
  is cut by the rank they stand in; with nothing imported a rank keeps the module's standing rank depth. The frontage tables arrive
  with Rules tables (the Reimport One Shelf (GM) macro in
  `Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Import from your books`).

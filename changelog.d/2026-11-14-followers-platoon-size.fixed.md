The followers roll (`Compendium Packs → ACKS II — Extras → Macros → ACKS Extras Macros → Generate Followers (Selected)`) no longer carries the troops that make one platoon in its code: that figure is read from the Rules tables you import from your own book (RR p. 335), a troop grant of no troops makes no type roll, and a world without the figure lists the troops with no platoon types instead of counting platoons from a guess. No world sees a change until the followers tables the roll also reads are imported, which no import does yet, so the roll stays off for now.

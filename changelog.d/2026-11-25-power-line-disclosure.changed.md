- **Divine and arcane power fold away behind their tags.** On a caster's sheet (Actors sidebar tab → the character's sheet → Magic tab, and the casting strip on the system's own character sheet), each power shows its tag with the balance and a button beside it with the number of entries. Press the button to open that power's entries and controls; press it again to close them. They start closed each time the sheet opens. The panel has an amount box: type a number and press Add or Remove to move that much at once, where the plus and minus moved one point a press.

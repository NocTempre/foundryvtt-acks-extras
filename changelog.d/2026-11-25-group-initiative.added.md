- **A group's bodies in a combat roll initiative as groups.** When a group's token, a gang's or a warband's, is added to a combat, the bodies it puts on the map are grouped for initiative gang by gang, with the call the system's own group control makes (the Combat Tracker sidebar tab's group button): each gang rolls once, and a gang with a single body rolls alone. A gang that is already in the combat, whether its bodies are out or its token stands as one row, fights as one of the warband's gangs and has no entry of its own. Regroup with the same Combat Tracker control. The system's tracker has two known defects that show once a second gang is grouped: a later grouping can hand an earlier gang its initiative bonus, and the system does not wait for its own write.

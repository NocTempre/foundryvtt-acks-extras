The Judge's client writes each field, and a forced march into a party that has a member the survival rules bar is refused on the player's own seat with the barred names, where the Judge is asked whether to set it anyway. The Hunted here box and the hunter's name stay the Judge's, and a player's pick never clears the Judge's mark.
- A player who owns a member can now press the Wilderness tab's movement tiles, Back to automatic and Undo, set flight's fields, set the Sky and press Roll weather, plan the Day, and declare Camp's exposure and declarations (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Wilderness tab → Movement, flight, Sky, Day and Camp). The camp's throw lines stay the Judge's. Roll weather is announced to the table with the player's name.
- A player who owns a member can now use the Order tab's head (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Order tab): Fastest, Undo, Auto-arrange, Frontage, the token's shape, the column's colour, Add a blank, a blank's move arrows and Remove, and Load order. Save order, and Forget in the load dialog, are drawn to a user who may change world settings (Settings tab → Game Settings → Configure Permissions → Modify Configuration Settings), since the saved orders are one list for every party. Disband stays the Judge's.
- A Player seat's audit window has a live "Used for" select for each ability on a character it owns, and a disabled one for any other. Path: Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → Order tab → Skill audit.
- The Monster level row on a table is now drawn to a player who owns the table (RollTables sidebar tab → a table → its sheet → Summary tab), and what they type is saved with the sheet's own Save.
- The strip's city progress, the blocks, the turns and the turned-around warning, now shows on every seat (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → City tab → the strip at the top).

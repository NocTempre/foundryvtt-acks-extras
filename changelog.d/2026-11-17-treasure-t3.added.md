- **Treasure pot → replace seeds take the place of the lots they replace (Cards sidebar tab → the pot → its window):** in the staging window, an item dropped in the **Replace** bucket now strikes the fewest of the pot's lots that cover its apparent worth, the smaller overshoot first and pot order after; those lots show struck through with "Replaced by" its name, and the seed's row says what it takes and by how much it is over, with the figures in its tooltip. When the pot opens the seed stands where the first struck lot stood, so a player cannot tell it by its place, and the struck lots are never turned, claimed or counted in the haul. A seed the lots cannot cover takes none and says how far short it fell, and the Judge who opens the pot is warned that it holds more than the table rolled. The whisper to the Judges lists what each seed took. Both **Who rolls** modes take the same pick; a control to change the lots is not built yet.

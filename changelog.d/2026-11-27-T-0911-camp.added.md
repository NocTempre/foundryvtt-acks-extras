- The **Camp** section (Actors sidebar tab → a party actor → its sheet → the **Wilderness** tab → **Camp**) shows its parts and takes your hand. Hover the **Food** or **Water** figure for each item carried, its size and where the size was read, and for any item with no size the page that prints it. **Vessels** lists every water item a member carries with what is left of it, and **Refill** fills the sized vessels of every member you own (a Judge owns all). **Going without** replaces the list of only the suffering: every member is a row with a hunger rung and a thirst rung, a menu for each on a member you own, and a *set by hand* mark on a rung you set; the next **End day** that steps that ladder goes on from the rung and takes the mark off. Exposure and Constitution lost show at the end of the row.

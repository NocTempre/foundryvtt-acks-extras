- **Actors sidebar → party sheet → Dungeon tab → The crossing:** a jump now has an attempt. Each member's jump row keeps its verdict and gains a **Jump** button (the Judge on any member, a player on a member they own). The press rolls the imported die against the gap and the row reads "Rolled N ft, clears" or "short". Where the landing is precarious or the jump charging, the member's own saving throw follows through the system's save, and a failed one rolls the imported shortfall die for how far they slip back: past the edge is solid ground and prone, exactly on it is the edge and hanging, short of it is empty space and a fall of the depth you set, with the imported fall dice. A jump that does not reach the gap falls short over empty space. Hover any figure for its working and the page. A jumper left hanging at the edge gets a **Climb up** button, thrown as one stretch of a sheer climb on the member's own climbing figure (the setup's *Rope fixed* makes it the easy climb). The distance jumped is kept on the member's record for the round's movement, which the crossing does not yet count. A player's press is declared in chat, rolled on the Judge's client and carded to the Judges; a figure nobody imported (the die, or the shortfall die for a precarious landing) refuses every seat, a Judge included, and rolls nothing. The Judge's own landing save press and footing pick stay as the override.

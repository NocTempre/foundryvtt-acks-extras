- **A group's officer morale bonus can be negative.** A warband modifier that lowers morale can now be held on the group (`system.unit.officerMoraleBonus`, written by a macro or the console, by a beastman organisation roll or by Hire as Group), where the field used to refuse anything below zero. The field takes -4 to 4, and the morale the group is read at subtracts a negative bonus while its officer is up. A bonus already stored stays as it is.

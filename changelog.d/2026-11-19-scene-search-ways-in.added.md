- **Chat sidebar → "The fight is over" card → Search the scene (Judges only):** opens the scene search for the party on the fight's scene, at the place its token stands in, with the fallen as the scene's bodies. Their treasure is dealt when it opens, a body's linked table is drawn without posting to the table, and carried items leave the bodies when the scene's pot opens. The card no longer stages a pot by hand. **Party sheet → Dungeon tab → Turn plan → Search here:** opens the same scene search with no fight, or its screen where one is open. A player who owns a member of the party sees the button too; their press tells the table the party searches here and the Judge opens the scene.

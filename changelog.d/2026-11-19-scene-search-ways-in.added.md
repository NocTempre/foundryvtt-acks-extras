- **Chat sidebar → "The fight is over" card → Search the scene (Judges only):** opens the scene search for the party on the fight's scene, at the place its token stands in, with the fallen as the scene's bodies. Their treasure is dealt when it opens and a body's linked table is drawn without posting to the table. A carried item stays on its body until the lot it became is found, and leaves it then. Once the scene search has ended, a Judge pressing the card again (it then reads "Open the scene") reopens the scene at the same place with the same bodies and the same pot, found marks included, and deals nothing again; a player cannot. If the pot was closed meanwhile, the scene still reopens with its bodies and no lots. The card no longer stages a pot by hand. **Party sheet → Dungeon tab → Turn plan → Search here:** opens the same scene search with no fight, or its screen where one is open. A player who owns a member of the party sees the button too; their press tells the table the party searches here and the Judge opens the scene.

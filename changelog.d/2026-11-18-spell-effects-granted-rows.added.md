- **On a character sheet's Rolls tab, in Proficiencies & powers (a throw a spell grants):** a throw a spell record gives the character shows in gold and rolls, whoever cast the spell, and a throw the character could get by casting a spell on itself shows pale: its spell or spells named in the tooltip, no roll and no star. Rolling a granted throw, or an Adventuring throw a spell made automatic, posts one line on the party roll card's format. A pinned granted throw keeps its pin when its record is replaced by another cast of the same spell.

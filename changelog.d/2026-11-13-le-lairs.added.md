- **Lairs & Encounters is a book you can connect, and its lairs are places.** Connect your copy in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports and import it there: Chapter 3's how-to becomes a Lairs place and each registered lair listing a place inside it, on the Judge's shelf like the AX adventures. This first part registers the book, the Lairs place and one lair; the other listings, their monsters and their plate maps follow.

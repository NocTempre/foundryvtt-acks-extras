- **Lairs & Encounters is a book you can connect, and its lairs are places.** Connect your copy in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports and import it there: Chapter 3's how-to becomes a Lairs place and each of its 167 listings a place inside it, numbered in print order (Lair 1 to Lair 167), on the Judge's shelf like the AX adventures. The 37 lairs that print a full-page plate in Appendix Two (the book's lair maps) also get a Scene of it, on a 5 ft square grid fitted to the plate's drawn squares, linked to that lair's place; a plate two listings share is one Scene on the listing whose text points to it. A lair is linked to its ACKS II monster, never a copy of it, so importing again makes no second one, and it rolls that monster's in-lair encounter as an encounter group, once, when your party arrives on its Scene (a party token placed there, or teleported there) or when you press Roll in the Lair section of the place's sheet (Contents tab; open the lair from the Compendium Packs tab, in the book's Judge shelf of Actors, or from the Actors sidebar for a lair with a Scene), which is how a lair with no Scene is rolled; the section shows the monster (or "Not imported"), the group, and why there is none where the monster's in-lair line counts units or states no count. A lair whose printed stat line already binds to its monster rolls too, and a deleted group rolls again at the next entry; the listing's stat lines link to the ACKS II monster where the book's creature has one, under another name where ACKS II renamed it, and are otherwise made from the stat line as the AX books' are. Import the Monstrous Manual first, or run Repair on the book afterwards (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Repair everything, on the Import tab), which lists a roster that already holds stat-line creatures for you to take.

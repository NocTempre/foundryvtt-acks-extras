- **Lairs & Encounters is a book you can connect, and its lairs are places.** Connect your copy in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports and import it there: Chapter 3's how-to becomes a Lairs place and each of its 167 listings a place inside it, numbered in print order (Lair 1 to Lair 167), on the Judge's shelf like the AX adventures. The 37 lairs that print a full-page plate in Appendix Two (the book's lair maps) also get a Scene of it, on a 5 ft square grid fitted to the plate's drawn squares, linked to that lair's place; a plate two listings share is one Scene on the listing whose text points to it. The monsters' links and the stat-line creatures follow.

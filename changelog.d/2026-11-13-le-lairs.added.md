- **Lairs & Encounters is a book you can connect, and its lairs are places.** Connect your copy in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports and import it there: Chapter 3's how-to becomes a Lairs place and each of its 167 listings a place inside it, numbered in print order (Lair 1 to Lair 167), on the Judge's shelf like the AX adventures. The plate maps, the monsters' links and the stat-line creatures follow.

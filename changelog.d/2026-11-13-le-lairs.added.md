- **Lairs & Encounters is a book you can connect, and its lairs are places.** Connect your copy in Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports and import it there: Chapter 3's how-to becomes a Lairs place and each of its 167 listings a place inside it, numbered in print order (Lair 1 to Lair 167), on the Judge's shelf like the AX adventures. The 37 lairs that print a full-page plate in Appendix Two (the book's lair maps) also get a Scene of it, on a 5 ft square grid fitted to the plate's drawn squares, linked to that lair's place; a plate two listings share is one Scene on the listing whose text points to it. A lair is linked to its ACKS II monster, never a copy of it, so importing again makes no second one; the listing's stat lines link to the ACKS II monster where the book's creature has one, under another name where ACKS II renamed it, and are otherwise made from the stat line as the AX books' are. Import the Monstrous Manual first, or run Repair on the book afterwards (Settings tab → Game Settings → ACKS II — Extras → Manage imports → Repair everything, on the Import tab), which lists a roster that already holds stat-line creatures for you to take.

- **The rounds to a dungeon turn and the turns to an hour are read from your Revised Rulebook and edited in the Ruledata Browser.** Both were figures the module carried in its own code; they are now read at import from the Turns and Rounds passage (RR p. 263), and the turn's length in seconds follows from the turns to an hour. With nothing imported the delve clock counts whole turns, a hand round and a combat round advance nothing, the world clock is not moved by the delve, the Dungeon tab's strip shows the turns alone, and a spell duration counted in turns has no length to set. Ruledata Browser: Settings tab → Game Settings → Acks Extras → Ruledata Browser, document `formation`.

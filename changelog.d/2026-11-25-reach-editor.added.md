- **The effect-row editor, opened from an ability's Mechanics tab or from Edit on a carried item's effect row (Details tab), ends each row with a reach group.** **How it is used** picks Raised and lowered, Used up or Always on, and is held while the item is raised, with a tooltip saying to lower it first. **A distance or Engaged** picks how the reach reaches. **Who counts** picks the bearer's foes, its side or everyone, **Kinds counted** and **Kinds left out** are picks among the creature kinds the module knows, and **Players see the ring** shows the reach to the players. The distance and an imported unit read as text. The group is editable by whoever may edit the item or ability.

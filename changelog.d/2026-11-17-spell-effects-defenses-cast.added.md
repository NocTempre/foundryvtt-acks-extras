- **Chat sidebar → a spell's cast card:** the damage a cast applies now meets each target's immunities, resistances and susceptibilities, and the target's saving throw meets them too. A target immune to all the damage passes its save with no roll (the save column reads "Auto"); a resistant target's save gains resistance's modifier once it has been imported, and a defense the module cannot price is listed and left to the Judge. An applied figure's tooltip lists the roll, the save's fraction, each defense and the rounding, then the amount applied. A case the module does not work out (a resistance whose fraction has not been imported, a susceptibility with no figure, damage whose quality the import did not read against a defense that waits on it) applies nothing, says which defense and its page on the card's line, and is applied by hand with the hit-point tool. The Judge and the target's owners read which defense applied; other players read "a defense". Healing and conditions meet no defense.
